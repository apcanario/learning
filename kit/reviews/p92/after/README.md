# P92 · the home after the change

The Academy home as P92 built it, shot whole by `verify.py --shots` at 320, 360,
390 (`phone`), 430, 768 (`tablet`) and 1280 px (`desktop`), dark and light, with reduced motion emulated; `*-hero-motion-*.png` are the hero at five moments with motion allowed (390 px and 1280 px). `verify.py`
shoots phones and the tablet at device scale 3; these are scaled to device scale 2 (2 image pixels to a CSS pixel); the
desktop shots are at scale 1.
