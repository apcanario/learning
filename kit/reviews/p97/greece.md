One style note that does not affect any verdict: in several figures the value label starts inside the grey track and runs past its end, so the track edge cuts through a digit. Examples are "3|20 sites" (m13), "9|0 ships" (m102), "50,000 tal|ents" (m113) and "50 mar|ks" (m94). Every value stays fully legible and nothing is clipped or overlapping. If wanted, the renderer could put the label entirely outside the track, or end the track before the label starts.
