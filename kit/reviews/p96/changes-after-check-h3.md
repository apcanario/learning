# What changed after the third check of Greece and Rome (its report: check-3-report.md; earlier: check-1/2-report.md and changes-1/2.md)

1. Greece 5.3: now the coins, deciding sentence from the section "Coinage from Lydia": "The earliest coins are electrum, a natural gold-silver alloy, stamped with a lion's head or other badge…"; the section goes on to Croesus' gold and silver, Aegina's turtles, Corinth's Pegasi and Athens' owls.
2. The outlines' courses now include every course whose texts name them: the tablet and the vase serve Rome, the eagle Greece. Mapped from the sections the check named: Rome 7.1 the tablet ("The Lyon tablet": the bronze from Lyon preserves Claudius' speech); Rome 9.2 the tablet ("The soldier's life: tablets and tombstones": the military diploma on two bronze tablets, the Vindolanda tablets); Rome 1.6 the tablet ("The Pyrgi tablets", its own subsection); Rome 1.2 the vase ("Metals, princes and the vase trade": Attic vases from Etruscan graves, the Tyrrhenian amphorae); Greece 2.4 the eagle ("The Prometheus cycle: Mekone, fire, the eagle, Heracles": the eagle sent to eat his liver, which Heracles kills). No neighbour shares an outline with any of them.
3. Greece 7.1: the deciding sentence is the description of the Indo-Greek coin types ("Coins became bilingual, Greek and Kharosthi, with Buddhist wheels beside Zeus").
4. Greece 5.5: the deciding sentence is now "Ostracism was first used in 487…" from the "Where sources disagree" paragraph.
5. library.md's courses column: it records the courses whose texts name the outline (it is matched against them); an outline in no page's use is marked spare. The libation serves Greece by its texts and is in use in Rome only; settled.
