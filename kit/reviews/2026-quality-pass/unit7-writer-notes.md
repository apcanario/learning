# Unit 7: the writers' unverified items, and what the maintainer checked

Written 25 October 2026. The place to start the unit 7 review. "Checked" items were verified by the maintainer when each report landed and need no recheck.

## 7.1 The Julio-Claudians
- Writer unsure: how much of Dio book 58 (the fall of Sejanus, 58.9–11) is Dio's own text rather than epitome; Pliny, Natural History 22.92 (Agrippina's mushroom) section number; Josephus, Jewish Antiquities 20.148; Dio 58.28 (Gaius and Macro smother Tiberius); Dio 61.3.1 (Seneca wrote Nero's first speech); the Senate's decree on Piso (summarised from a BMCR review, 1997.07.22); CIL VI 920 "eleven kings" (restored); Suetonius, Galba 11 for the Senate recognising Galba; Champlin, Nero (2003) on the fire and the Golden House; Barrett, Rome Is Burning (2020); Barrett, Agrippina (1996); Ferrill (1991) as the one modern historian who thinks Caligula was mad; Philo ranges (Against Flaccus 25–72; Embassy 8–21, 188–348); P.Lond. 1912 barring Jews from the gymnasium's games.
- Checked: Champlin, Tiberius and His Age (Princeton, November 2024): the Capri stories as "incoherent fictions" of "a brilliant fantasist" (publisher); Sejanus modelled on Agrippa and perhaps a candidate for the succession (Classics for All review); the pointers to 0.1, 1.4, 1.5, 2.4, 2.5, 3.2, 6.4 and Greek 7.4.
