## Findings
1. [error] [accuracy] Section "Hannibal after Zama" (dispute box).
   Current: "The snake jars rest on Nepos and Justin (32.4) alone."
   Problem: Frontinus has the trick too: Hannibal suggests jars of vipers to Antiochus, and Prusias does the same when his fleet is giving way.
   Fix: "The snake jars rest on Nepos, Justin (32.4) and Frontinus, who has Hannibal suggest them to Antiochus as well as Prusias (<em>Stratagems</em> 4.7.10–11)."
   Basis: Frontinus, Stratagems 4.7.10–11, Loeb text on LacusCurtius (https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Frontinus/Strategemata/4*.html).

2. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Self-test" (answer 2).
   Current: "Rome's case rested on its later Saguntine friendship"
   Problem: Polybius dates Saguntum's appeal to Roman good faith only to some years before Hannibal's time (3.30.1), and whether it came before or after the Ebro treaty is disputed; "later" takes a side the body does not ("some years earlier").
   Fix: "Rome's case rested on its separate Saguntine friendship"
   Basis: Polybius 3.30.1; the report's section "Spain, the oath and the question of war guilt".
