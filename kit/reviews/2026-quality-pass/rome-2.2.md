2. [minor] [attribution] Section "Venus, Apollo, Diana and Mercury".
   Current: "Rome's first temple, to Venus Obsequens, was paid for in 295 by fines on adulterous matrons (Livy 10.31.9)."
   Problem: Livy 10.31.9 names only "the temple of Venus near the Circus". The epithet Obsequens comes from Servius.
   Fix: "Rome's first temple, to Venus Obsequens (the epithet is Servius', on <em>Aeneid</em> 1.720), was paid for in 295 by fines on adulterous matrons (Livy 10.31.9)."
   Basis: Livy 10.31.9, "Veneris aedem quae prope Circum est"; Servius on Aeneid 1.720 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_Obsequens, citing Buszard 2023).
