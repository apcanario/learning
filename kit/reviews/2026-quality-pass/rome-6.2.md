## Findings
1. [error] [accuracy] Section "The house on the Palatine and the temple of Apollo".
   Current: "gave the adjoining house of the rex sacrorum to the Vestals (Dio 54.27.3)"
   Problem: Dio says the rex sacrificulus' house adjoined the Vestals' quarters, in the Forum; "adjoining" here reads as next to Augustus' Palatine house. The same wording is in self-test answer 2.
   Fix: "gave the house of the rex sacrorum, which adjoined their quarters, to the Vestals (Dio 54.27.3)"; in self-test answer 2: "gave the house of the rex sacrorum, next to their own, to the Vestals (Dio 54.27.3)"
   Basis: Dio 54.27.3, Cary's translation (https://lexundria.com/dio/54.27/cy): the house of the rex sacrificulus went to the Vestals "since it adjoined their quarters".

2. [error] [attribution] Section "Genius, Lares and divus: the cult of the princeps" (last dispute box).
   Current: "Suetonius (<em>Augustus</em> 52) and Dio (51.20.8) say no one in Rome or Italy dared such honours."
   Problem: Only Dio says that no one in Rome or Italy dared. Suetonius says Augustus accepted temples in the provinces only jointly with Roma and firmly refused the honour in the city; he says nothing of Italy, which is the point the Pola temple tests.
   Fix: "Suetonius says he refused such honours in the city (<em>Augustus</em> 52), and Dio that no one in Rome or Italy dared them (51.20.8)."
   Basis: Suetonius, Augustus 52 ("in nulla tamen provincia nisi communi suo Romaeque nomine recepit. nam in urbe quidem pertinacissime abstinuit hoc honore"); Dio 51.20.8. Checked from the texts as known.

3. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Genius, Lares and divus: the cult of the princeps".
   Current: "melted his silver statues into tripods for Apollo"
   Problem: The tripods were gold, bought with the silver: Suetonius says he melted the statues and from them dedicated golden tripods to Palatine Apollo.
   Fix: "melted his silver statues to pay for golden tripods for Apollo"
   Basis: Suetonius, Augustus 52 ("exque iis aureas cortinas Apollini Palatino dedicavit"); Res Gestae 24.2.

4. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Genius, Lares and divus: the cult of the princeps".
   Current: "it was dedicated on 1 August in 12 BC according to the epitome and Dio (54.32.1), in 10 BC according to Suetonius (<em>Claudius</em> 2.1)."
   Problem: The day comes only from Suetonius, who dates Claudius' birth on 1 August 10 BC to the altar's dedication; the epitome and Dio give the year 12 and no day.
   Fix: "it was dedicated in 12 BC according to the epitome and Dio (54.32.1), on 1 August 10 BC according to Suetonius (<em>Claudius</em> 2.1)."
   Basis: Livy, Periochae 139 (the altar of the god Caesar dedicated, Vercondaridubnus priest); Dio 54.32.1 (the festival at the altar in 12 BC); Suetonius, Claudius 2.1. Checked from the texts as known.
