## Findings
1. [error] [attribution] Section "Virgil before the Aeneid: the Eclogues and Georgics" (dispute box).
   Current: "Friedrich Skutsch defended the story; most modern scholars doubt it, since Donatus is silent and Orpheus is integral to the book's design."
   Problem: The defender was Franz Skutsch (1865–1912), in Aus Vergils Frühzeit (1901) and Gallus und Vergil (1906); there is no Friedrich. The doubting side has no name, and the classic reply is W. B. Anderson's.
   Fix: "Franz Skutsch (<em>Aus Vergils Frühzeit</em>, 1901) defended the story; W. B. Anderson ("Gallus and the Fourth <em>Georgic</em>", 1933) and most since doubt it, since Donatus is silent and Orpheus is integral to the book's design."
   Basis: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Skutsch (both books his); W. B. Anderson, "Gallus and the Fourth Georgic", Classical Quarterly 27 (1933) 36–45, which examines Skutsch's arguments in Aus Vergils Frühzeit 140–147 and rejects Servius' story (https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/classical-quarterly/article/abs/gallus-and-the-fourth-georgic/93D3C5BEBC051EFAF010D9AA69641CC8).

2. [minor] [attribution] Section "Livy: a history for these times".
   Current: "Augustus considered the name Romulus before choosing his own (Suetonius, <em>Augustus</em> 7.2)"
   Problem: Suetonius says only that some proposed the name; that Augustus himself wanted it is Dio's statement. Module 1.4 cites Dio for it.
   Fix: "Augustus considered the name Romulus before choosing his own (Suetonius, <em>Augustus</em> 7.2; Dio 53.16.7)"
   Basis: Suetonius, Augustus 7.2 ("quibusdam censentibus Romulum appellari oportere"); Dio 53.16.7; rome/r-1.4.html. Checked from the texts as known.

3. [minor] [style] Section "Maecenas and the friendship of poets" (dispute box).
   Current: "Ronald Syme's chapter "The Organisation of Opinion""
   Problem: A quoted title keeps its published spelling, and Oxford printed the chapter as "The Organization of Opinion".
   Fix: "Ronald Syme's chapter "The Organization of Opinion""
   Basis: not confirmed online (the OUP page did not show the contents); from the 1939 edition as known.
