## Figure 3 (timeline): FIX

Spans and points against the text (Flower: which republic fell?), with positions checked against the axis (about 2.35 px a year; every bar end and point matches its date):

- 300–180, 180–139, 139–88: "The third, fourth and fifth are the republics of the nobles: 300 to 180, 180 to 139 and 139 to 88". The dates are correct. The labels are not (see below).
- "REPUBLICAN GOVERNMENT CEASED, 88–81": "From 88 to 81 republican government ceased." Correct, in the text's words.
- "SULLA'S REPUBLIC, 81–60": "The sixth republic was Sulla's, from 81 to 60." Correct.
- "ALLIANCE OF THREE, 59–53": "the alliance of Pompey, Crassus and Caesar from 59 to 53." Acceptable.
- "TRANSITION, 53–49", "CAESAR'S DICTATORSHIP, 49–44", "TRIUMVIRATE, 43–33": all three as in the text.
- Point "180 BC, Law fixing the ladder of office": "the law of 180 that fixed the ladder of office".
- Point "139 BC, Ballot law": "the ballot law of 139".
- Point "88 BC, Sulla marches on Rome": "ends the republic of the nobles with Sulla's march in 88" (module 0.3); "when a consul first led a Roman army against Rome".

Every span label carries its years, so each reads as a period.

**Fault 1, the labels.** "REPUBLIC OF THE NOBLES I", "NOBLES II" and "NOBLES III" give the republics of the nobles a numbering, I to III, that the text does not use. The text numbers them third, fourth and fifth in Flower's own sequence, and calls Sulla's republic the sixth. A reader comparing the figure with the paragraph above it meets two numberings for the same periods, and "Nobles I" can be taken for Flower's first republic (450–367/6). The caption has to explain the figure's own invention. Corrected labels, in the text's words:

- `THIRD REPUBLIC (NOBLES), 300–180`
- `FOURTH REPUBLIC (NOBLES), 180–139`
- `FIFTH REPUBLIC (NOBLES), 139–88`
- Optional, for consistency: `SIXTH REPUBLIC: SULLA'S, 81–60`

**Fault 2, the caption.** The caption does not name the source of the 88 point, Sulla's march, which the text attributes to Flower through module 0.3. It does not say that the text gives every date from Flower without a passage or page. And it rests on the I to III relabelling. Full replacement caption:

> The later part of Harriet Flower's sequence of republics, 300 to 33 BC, from the section Flower: which republic fell?, after Flower, Roman Republics (2010): her third, fourth and fifth republics, the republics of the nobles, divided at the law of 180 that fixed the ladder of office and the ballot law of 139 (deep dive module 11.1); the end of the republic of the nobles with Sulla's march in 88, which module 0.3 records; the years 88 to 81, when republican government ceased; Sulla's republic, the sixth, from 81 to 60; and the alliance of Pompey, Crassus and Caesar (59–53), the transition (53–49), Caesar's dictatorship (49–44) and the triumvirate (43–33), which she counts as no republic. The text gives all these dates from Flower without a passage or page.

Form and render: periods as spans plus three dated points fit a timeline. The bars are short bars above the axis, which is a house rule. The labels are readable and unclipped in both themes, and the white label inside the red first bar holds its contrast in both. On the phone the figure scrolls sideways across two frames, which is a house rule. One minor point, not a fault: the categorical colours repeat (red for the first bar and for Caesar's dictatorship, orange for the second bar and for the triumvirate). The colours carry no meaning, and any change would be a renderer matter.
