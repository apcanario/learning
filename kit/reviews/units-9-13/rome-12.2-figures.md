Checked from `text.txt`, `captions.md` and every desktop and phone frame in dark and light. Phone frames show only part of figures 1 to 3, and that is the house sideways scroll, not a fault. In all three figures, nothing is clipped, overlapping or unreadable in either theme.

## Figure 1 (matrix): FIX

**Trace.**
- Book 1: "sea; Carthage". The storm, the Libyan coast at 1.157–169 and Carthage are all in the text. "Odyssey 5–8" summarises the section's Odyssey 5.291–332, 6.102–109, 7.14–17, 7.19–36 and 8.499–531. "12.6 (Juno)" matches the text: "Why Juno hates … is deep dive module 12.6's".
- Book 2: "Troy, told" matches the text (Aeneas narrates, 2.3). "Odyssey 8; the Cycle" matches the text: Demodocus 8.499–520, and "for the rest" the Little Iliad and the Sack of Ilion. "Greek 4.3" is in the text.
- Book 3: "sea, told" matches "Aeneas' Apologoi, the voyage told at the banquet". "Odyssey 9–12" matches the text ("books 2–3, as Odyssey 9–12").
- Book 4: "Carthage" matches. "Odyssey 5 (Calypso)" matches "The Homeric model is Calypso. In Odyssey 5.28–147". "12.4" matches "The book scene by scene … deep dive module 12.4's".
- Book 5: "Sicily" and "Iliad 23" match ("The model is the funeral games for Patroclus in Iliad 23").

- Kind and reading: a matrix fits, and the headers and cells read correctly in both themes.

**Faults.**
1. **The "Greek 4.3" cell for book 2 breaks the caption's own rule.** The caption says a blank cell is a book the report does not hand on as a whole, so a filled cell means a book handed on whole. Only books 4 and 6 are handed on whole, to 12.4 and 12.5. Book 1 is handed on only for Juno's anger, and the cell's "(Juno)" says so. Book 2 is not handed to Greek 4.3 at all. The text sends the reader back to Greek 4.3 for the Cyclic sources, Arctinus' Laocoön and the two Cyclic deaths of Priam. That is the same kind of reference book 5 makes to Greek 4.2 (Iliad 23), Greek 10.12 (the Troy game) and 12.3 (Nisus), and book 5's cell is blank. Greek 4.3 is also an earlier module, so "taken further in" reads it the wrong way round. **Fix:** leave the book 2 cell blank, and reword the caption's rule as in the replacement below.
2. **The header "Main Homeric model" covers a non-Homeric source.** The book 2 cell includes "the Cycle", and the text sets the Little Iliad and the Sack of Ilion beside Homer, not as Homer's. The caption also calls "Proclus' summaries" a Homeric model. In the text, Proclus' summaries are only how the Cyclic poems survive; the poems themselves are the model. **Fix:** change the header to "MAIN MODEL". In the caption, name the Cyclic poems as the model and Proclus as the witness.
3. **The caption claims more than it lists.** It says "The Homeric models are those named in each section", but for book 1 it gives four passages and leaves out others the section names. Most notably it leaves out Odyssey 7.19–36 (Athena disguised as a girl), the direct model for Venus the huntress, while it includes 7.14–17 from the same scene. The section also names Odyssey 13.96–112, 10.156–184 and 12.208–212. **Fix:** say "the chief models each section names" and add 7.19–36.
4. **The shading is never explained.** Rows alternate green (books 1, 3, 5) and red (books 2, 4, 6) across all three columns. This matches the text's sea–land alternation ("The odd books (1, 3, 5) move by sea …, the even books (2, 4, 6) stay on land"), but the caption does not say so. **Fix:** name the encoding in the caption.

**Replacement caption.**

> Books 1–6 by setting, main model and the module that takes each further, as this report's book sections give them; "told" marks the books Aeneas narrates, and the shading follows the alternation the text notes between the sea books (1, 3, 5) and the land books (2, 4, 6). The models are the chief ones each section names: book 1, *Odyssey* 5.291–332 (the storm), 6.102–109 (Nausicaa), 7.14–17 and 7.19–36 (Athena's mist and disguise) and 8.499–531 (Demodocus); book 2, *Odyssey* 8.499–520 and, for the rest, the Little Iliad and the Sack of Ilion of the Epic Cycle, known from Proclus' summaries; book 3, *Odyssey* 9–12; book 4, *Odyssey* 5.28–147 (Calypso); book 5, *Iliad* 23; book 6, *Odyssey* 11. The last column names the deep dive the report hands the whole book to (books 4 and 6) or, for book 1, Juno's anger (12.6); a blank cell is a book the report hands on in neither way.

**Data changes:** header "MAIN HOMERIC MODEL" becomes "MAIN MODEL"; the book 2 "taken further in" cell "Greek 4.3" becomes blank.
