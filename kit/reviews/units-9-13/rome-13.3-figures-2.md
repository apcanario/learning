**Caption trace.** "Marcus Aurelius' salaried chairs at Athens in 176": text, "the endowment of 176 ... an annual salary (Dio 72(71).31.3)". Section names match the text's headings (two given in short form, which is fine). Dio 72(71).31.3, Philostratus, Lives of the Sophists 566 and Lucian, Eunuch 3 are the passages the text cites for the chairs; "recorded in Greek module 7.4" is the text's. "Brutus and Sextius are disputed, as the text's dispute boxes show": correct.

**Faults and fixes.**

1. *Old Academy chair cell asserts continuity the text does not state.* Setting "Platonist chair" against "Old Academy (Antiochus)" tells the reader that Antiochus' school held the chair of 176. The text says only that "the Academy now meant Plato taught as doctrine, a Platonist chair", and separately that after Antiochus "its ethics passed into Middle Platonism". Fix the cell to **"Platonist chair (Plato as doctrine)"** and change the caption's clause to: **"the chair of the Academy in 176 was Platonist, Plato taught as doctrine, not Cicero's scepticism; the text says Antiochus' ethics passed into Middle Platonism but does not make the chair his school's."**
2. *"none (sceptical)" reads as if the chair were sceptical.* Change the New Academy chair cell to **"none (the chair was Platonist)"**.
3. *Cicero's son is not named as an adherent.* The text makes him Cratippus' pupil only. Either change the cell to **"Cratippus, Cicero's son (pupil)"**, or replace him with a man the text places in the school: **"Cratippus, Staseas"** (Staseas is one of the school's "house philosophers" in The Peripatetics and Cratippus).

**Caption trace.** "88 BC to AD 176" matches the first and last points. Every point is given its passage item by item, and the one date the text gives without a passage (Against Piso, 55) is named as such. Correct apart from the Cassius entry.

**Faults and fixes.**

1. *86 BC label.* Change to **"Sulla takes Athens, seizes Apellicon's library"**. The caption entry ("Apellicon's library: Plutarch, Sulla 26, and Strabo 13.1.54") stands.
2. *Cassius' passage in the caption.* Change "Cassius: Letters to Friends 15.19" to **"Cassius: Letters to Friends 15.19.2–3, his reply to Cicero's letter of January 45 (15.16.1)"**.
3. *AD 121 label.* Change to **"Plotina asks Hadrian's favour for the Garden"**, matching the act the text dates to 121.

**RENDERER notes (not fixable in the figure's data or caption).**

- *Missing AD 150 tick.* The axis is ticked every 25 years (75, 50, 25 BC; AD 25, 50, 75, 100, 125, 175), but there is no tick or label at AD 150, in either theme. The reader sees an unexplained 50-year gap between AD 125 and AD 175. The gap at the BC/AD boundary is expected, since there is no year 0. This is a fault in the tick generator.
- *88 BC and 86 BC markers overlap.* Two years is about 4.7 px at this scale, and the markers are about 6 px wide, so the 86 BC dot partly covers the 88 BC dot, which shows as a crescent. The separate leader lines and labels keep both readable, so this does not mislead. It could only be met by a smaller marker or an offset in the renderer.
