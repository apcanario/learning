Faults:

1. **Mercury's passages are incomplete.** The caption promises "the passages of the *Aeneid* the text cites for each". The text's Mercury row (L52, acts for Jupiter) cites three scenes: 1.297–304, 4.219–278 and "returns in a dream (4.554–570)". The figure and the caption give only the first two. 4.259–267 lies inside 4.219–278, and 4.206–218 is Iarbas' prayer, not a Mercury scene, so neither is needed.
   **Fix:** change the node to "Mercury (1.297–304; 4.219–278; 4.554–570)". In the caption, change "Jupiter sends Mercury (1.297–304, 4.219–278)" to "Jupiter sends Mercury (1.297–304, 4.219–278, 4.554–570)".

2. **Aeolus and Juturna lose the text's qualifiers.** The figure marks Venus' Neptune and Vulcan "asked", so a bare node reads as "sent". The text does not say Juno sends either of these two. She "bribes Aeolus with a nymph" and "urges Juturna … to snatch her brother from death if she can" (L54). The caption carries both verbs; the drawing drops them.
   **Fix:** change the nodes to "Aeolus, bribed (1.65–75)" and "Juturna, urged (12.134–160)". The caption needs no change: it already says "Juno bribes Aeolus (1.65–75) … and urges Juturna (12.134–160)".

No fault here needs a change to the renderer or the theme.
