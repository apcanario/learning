- Each span label carries its years. Nothing is clipped or overlapping in either theme; the death label stays inside the 760 px column. Phone frames split the axis at 40 BC, as the house rule allows.

Caption faults:
1. "dated by Pollio's consulship (4.11–12)": the text cites 4.11 only. Change it to "(4.11)".
2. "the Eclogues about 42–38 (module 6.3)": the text does not credit 42–38 to module 6.3. It gives "usually dated about 42–38" with no passage, and Life 25 only for the "about three years". The caption also says these dates "rest on no single passage", which contradicts the module reference.
3. "the Aeneid from 29 to the poet's death": the text gives the start in 29 without a passage, and the caption does not say so. It names only the Eclogues and the start of the Georgics as unsourced.
4. "death from Life 35": the sentence that gives the date cites Life 35–36. Prefer "Life 35–36".

Corrected caption:
"Virgil's life and the composition of the three works. Birth from Donatus, Life 2; toga from Life 6; Atella from Life 27; the reading of books 2, 4 and 6 from Life 32, dated after Marcellus' death in 23 by its content; death from Life 35–36. The fourth Eclogue is dated by Pollio's consulship (4.11). The spans are those the text gives: the Eclogues usually dated about 42–38, written over about three years (Life 25); the Georgics seven years in the writing (Life 25) and finished in 29, so begun about 36; the Aeneid from 29 to the poet's death in 19. The text gives 42–38 for the Eclogues, about 36 for the start of the Georgics and 29 for the start of the Aeneid without a passage."

No change to the drawing.

Corrected caption:

The text gives the pairs without a passage; the scheme is a modern reading of the poems, not an ancient one."

## Figure 3 (flow): FIX

Trace of the drawing:
- "Prose draft in twelve books, Life 23": "first wrote the whole story in prose, divided into twelve books ... (Life 23)". OK.
- "Verse composed out of order, struts left, Life 23–24": Life 23 (no order) and Life 24 (tibicines). OK.
- "Books 2, 4 and 6 read to Augustus, Life 32". OK.
- "Journey to Greece, 19 BC, Life 35": "In 19 ... set out for Greece and Asia ... (Life 35)". OK.
- "Fever at Megara, death at Brundisium, Life 35": the Megara paragraph has no citation of its own and follows the Life 35 sentence. Death at Brundisium is cited at Life 35–36 in the first section. Acceptable, but the caption should say so.
- "Asks for his book-boxes to burn it, Life 39": "In his last illness Virgil kept asking for his book-boxes ... (Life 39)". The citation is right, but see fault 1.
- "Varius and Tucca edit at Augustus' order, Life 40–41": the text says "Varius published it at Augustus' wish" (Life 41) and "left his writings to Varius and Tucca" (Life 40). The text says "wish", not "order".
- "Published with the half-lines left, Life 41": "leaving even the incomplete lines as they were (Life 41)". OK.
- The flow kind fits. The wrap connector reads clearly. Nothing is clipped or overlapping in either theme. Phone frames cut the rows, as the house rule allows.

Faults:
1. The sequence is misleading. The arrows run from "death at Brundisium" to "Asks for his book-boxes to burn it", but the text puts the request "in his last illness", before the death. This follows Donatus' section order (35 before 39), not the order of events, so the flow shows the dead poet asking for his manuscript. Reorder the steps (still eight):
   1. Prose draft in twelve books, Life 23
   2. Verse composed out of order, struts left, Life 23–24
   3. Books 2, 4 and 6 read to Augustus, Life 32
   4. Journey to Greece, fever at Megara, 19 BC, Life 35
   5. In his last illness, asks for his book-boxes to burn it, Life 39
   6. Dies at Brundisium, 21 September 19 BC, Life 35–36
   7. Varius and Tucca edit at Augustus' wish, Life 40–41
   8. Published with the half-lines left, Life 41
2. Label: "edit at Augustus' order" becomes "edit at Augustus' wish", the text's word (Life 41).
3. The caption must match the new order and say which step has no citation of its own.

Corrected caption:
"The making and publication of the Aeneid in the order of events as Donatus tells them, from the sections Making the Aeneid and The order to burn it; each step carries the section of the Suetonius–Donatus Life the text cites for it. The fever at Megara has no citation of its own and is told after Life 35; the date of death is cited at Life 35–36 in the section The ancient lives."
