# Rome 12.6 writer: unsure list (Opus 5.5, 1 November 2026)

1. Servius on *Aeneid* 1.47 as reading Juno as air: from memory, not checked; Cicero, *On the Nature of the Gods* 2.66 is secure.
2. Servius on 12.841 (Juno won over in the second Punic war, brought to Rome by Scipio in the third): wording from a secondary article found by search, not from an edition of Servius.
3. Feeney 1984's thesis (book 12 not a definitive transformation; Juno's hostility resumes in history): inferred from the abstract and notes on Cambridge Core; the full text could not be read (fetch refused). Not checked beyond that.
4. Heinze (1903) and Bailey (1935) identifying fate with Jupiter's will: taken from Casali's BMCR review of Zanker, not from the books.
5. Feeney 1991 (Juno as Tanit, Hera, air and marriage goddess; Jupiter a character with no neutral vantage): from the BMCR 1992.02.08 review.
6. Jeffrey Fish in *Vergil, Philodemus, and the Augustans* (anger allowed without pleasure in punishment; the Helen episode as correction): from BMCR 2005.04.64. The volume's four editors are from memory.
7. Augustine, *City of God* 9.4 quoting *Aeneid* 4.449 for the Stoic sage: from memory.
8. Lucretius 2.651 for the line on divine freedom from anger: line number from memory (editions vary by a line).
9. Euripides, *Heracles*: Hera sends Iris with Lyssa; cited without line numbers.
10. *Aeneid* line ranges given for scene extents (e.g. 7.323–571, 5.604–663, 12.843–886) are from memory of the OCT; the short quoted lines were checked against my recall only.
