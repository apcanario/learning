**Fixes**

2. Caption, first two sentences: "Seneca's life in dated events and periods, from this report's first six sections and its account of the death. Birth as modern biographers estimate it; no ancient source gives the year; ..." (rest unchanged).
3. Name whose dates these are, as the text does and as the Gaius event already does with "(Dio)": `about 31 · Return from Egypt (modern dating)`. The birth label may likewise read `c. 4–1 BC · Born at Corduba (modern estimate)`.
4. Draw the birth so it does not read as 4 BC: place the dot at the middle of the range (c. 2 BC), or draw 4–1 BC as a short range mark.
5. Tick overlap: remove the `AD 65` axis tick, since the "Ordered to die" event label already prints AD 65. Or remove `AD 62`, since the Withdrawal span label prints 62–65. ## Figure 2 (flow): PASS

**Trace (box → text, in sequence)**

1. The tribune arrives · villa four miles from Rome → "The tribune Gavius Silvanus ... found him at a villa four miles from Rome" (Annals 15.60–61).
2. The image of his life · left to his friends → "he left them ... the image of his life (imago vitae suae, 15.62)".
3. Arms cut · with Paulina → "Paulina chose to die with him, and both cut their arms" (15.63).
4. Legs and knees cut · Paulina taken away → "he cut the veins of his legs and knees; ... he had her taken to another room" (15.63).
5. Dictation · words published → "called secretaries and dictated at length; Tacitus declines to paraphrase the words, since they had been published (15.63)".
6. The Athenian poison · fails → "the one used to kill those condemned by the public court of the Athenians; it failed" (15.64).
7. Warm pool · libation to Jupiter the Liberator → "He entered a pool of warm water ... offered the liquid to Jupiter the Liberator" (15.64).
8. Steam bath · suffocates → "Carried into a steam bath, he suffocated" (15.64).
9. Cremation · without ceremony, by codicil → "cremated without ceremony, as he had directed in a codicil" (15.64).

The order matches the text's narration. Nero's order to save Paulina (15.64) is left out, which is right for a sequence of Seneca's stages. The only number, four miles, is in the text.

**Caption:** it claims only the sequence shown. It names the section correctly ("The death in 65: a Socratic performance"), and its passages match item by item: arrival 15.60–61; image 15.62; veins (arms, legs) and dictation 15.63; poison, pool, steam bath, cremation 15.64. No dates are drawn, so no undated claim is needed.

**Legibility:** a snake layout of 4 + 4 + 1 boxes with return connectors. It reads clearly in both themes on desktop and across the two phone frames, with no clipping or overlap. Kind: a flow fits a strict sequence.
