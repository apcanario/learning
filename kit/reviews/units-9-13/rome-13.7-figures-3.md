- "Apollonius (Nicomedia or Chalcedon)": Dio's "Apollonius of Nicomedia (the Historia Augusta's Apollonius of Chalcedon, 2.7)". The "or" carries the disagreement.

The text never uses the word "teacher" for Sextus. But it says Marcus went to hear him "even as emperor", which makes Sextus a teacher he heard, so the root label holds. The box adds nothing beyond name, town and subject.
