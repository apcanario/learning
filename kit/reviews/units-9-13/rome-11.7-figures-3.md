### Trace of the drawing

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calpurnia's dream / the night before | Omens: "The day before, a small bird..." then "That night ... Calpurnia dreamed that the gable of the house fell and that her husband was stabbed in her arms" (81.3); Plutarch's two forms (Caesar 63). | Supported. |

| 3 | Artemidorus' note / carried unread | Omens: "On the way ... Artemidorus of Cnidus, pressed on him a note revealing the plot, which Caesar carried unread into the meeting (Plutarch, Caesar 65; Suetonius, Julius 81.4, without the name)". | Supported. |

| 5 | Trebonius holds Antony / outside | Murder: "Trebonius kept Antony talking outside" (Cicero, Philippics 2.34; Plutarch, Brutus 17; Appian 2.117; Dio 44.19); self-test: "Who kept Antony outside". | Supported. |

| 7 | Casca strikes first | Murder: "One of the Cascas struck from behind" (82.2), Plutarch's Casca exchange (Caesar 66); conspirators: "Publius Servilius Casca ... who struck first". | Supported. |
| 8 | Caesar falls / at the base of Pompey's statue | Murder: toga over the head (Caesar 66), Brutus' thigh wound (Appian 2.117), folds drawn down "so as to fall decently" (Julius 82.2); "He fell at the base of Pompey's statue" (no passage). | Supported. |

Order: night (1) → morning, led out (2) → "on the way" (3) → as he left his litter (4) → Antony kept outside while the attack begins inside (5) → Cimber (6) → Casca (7) → the fall (8). Matches the text's sequence. Omitted steps (the sacrifice, Appian 2.116; the closing in, Nicolaus 24) are omissions, not misorderings.
