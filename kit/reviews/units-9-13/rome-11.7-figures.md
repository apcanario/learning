# Rome 11.7 "The Ides of March": figure check

Checked against `text.txt` and `captions.md` only; every desktop and phone image looked at, dark and light.

## Figure 1 (flow): FIX

Trace, box by box:

- **Calpurnia's dream / the night before.** "That night ... Calpurnia dreamed that the gable of the house fell and that her husband was stabbed in her arms" (Suetonius 81.3); Plutarch's two forms (Caesar 63). Caption passages match.
- **Decimus fetches Caesar / takes him by the hand.** "he took Caesar by the hand and led him out (Caesar 64; Suetonius, Julius 81.4)". Matches.
- **Artemidorus' note / carried unread.** "pressed on him a note revealing the plot, which Caesar carried unread into the meeting (Plutarch, Caesar 65; Suetonius, Julius 81.4, without the name)". Label matches. The caption gives only Caesar 65 and leaves out Suetonius 81.4.
- **Spurinna / come, but not gone.** "On the morning, Caesar mocked him that the Ides had come; Spurinna answered that they had come but not gone (81.4; Plutarch, Caesar 63; Valerius Maximus 8.11.2)". The label matches. The caption gives only Suetonius 81.4 and leaves out Plutarch, Caesar 63 and Valerius Maximus 8.11.2.
- **Popilius Laenas / a private word at the litter.** "spoke privately to Caesar at length as he left his litter ... (Appian 2.116; Plutarch, Brutus 16)". The label matches. The caption leaves out Plutarch, Brutus 16.
- **Trebonius holds Antony / outside.** "Trebonius kept Antony talking outside (Cicero, Philippics 2.34; Plutarch, Brutus 17; Appian 2.117; Dio 44.19)". The label matches. The caption leaves out Plutarch, Brutus 17 and Dio 44.19. Its last sentence on Caesar 66 and Decimus matches the text.
- **Cimber's petition / seizes the toga.** "seized his toga at both shoulders ... (Julius 82.1)". Matches.
- **Casca strikes first.** "One of the Cascas struck from behind ... (82.2)". Casca "struck first" also appears in the conspirators list. The caption's "Suetonius, Julius 82.1–2" holds. The text also cites Plutarch, Caesar 66 for the Casca exchange.
- **Caesar falls / at the base of Pompey's statue.** "He fell at the base of Pompey's statue." That sentence has no passage. The caption's "(Plutarch, Caesar 66; Appian 2.117)" belongs to the sentences before it (the covered head and the thigh wound). Suetonius, Julius 82.2 (drawing the folds down to fall decently) is cited there too and is missing from the caption.

Order. Calpurnia (that night), then Decimus leading him out, then Artemidorus ("on the way"), then Popilius (as he left his litter), then Trebonius, Cimber, Casca and the fall: all of these follow the text. **Spurinna does not.** The text places the exchange only "on the morning". Nothing in the text puts it after Artemidorus or before Popilius Laenas. The flow therefore fixes a position the text does not give, and the caption's "in the order of events the sources cited give" claims a source order that the text does not state for this item.

Rendering: readable in both themes, with no clipping or overlap. The wrap connectors read correctly. The phone frames show the house-rule sideways scroll.

Fixes:

1. Spurinna's position. Either (a) add to the caption: "The text dates Spurinna's answer only to the morning; its place in the sequence is not given." Or (b) add a sentence to the text, with its passage, that says where in the morning the exchange took place (only if the writer can source it). Without one of the two, take Spurinna out of the chain.
2. Replace the caption's item list with the text's full citations:
   "Calpurnia's dream (Suetonius, *Julius* 81.3; Plutarch, *Caesar* 63); Decimus (Plutarch, *Caesar* 64; Suetonius, *Julius* 81.4); Artemidorus (Plutarch, *Caesar* 65; Suetonius, *Julius* 81.4, without the name); Spurinna (Suetonius, *Julius* 81.4; Plutarch, *Caesar* 63; Valerius Maximus 8.11.2); Popilius Laenas (Appian 2.116; Plutarch, *Brutus* 16); Trebonius (Cicero, *Philippics* 2.34; Plutarch, *Brutus* 17; Appian 2.117; Dio 44.19); Cimber and Casca (Suetonius, *Julius* 82.1–2; Plutarch, *Caesar* 66); the fall (Plutarch, *Caesar* 66; Appian 2.117; Suetonius, *Julius* 82.2; the text gives the place, the base of Pompey's statue, without a passage). Plutarch's *Caesar* 66 gives Decimus, not Trebonius, the task of holding Antony."
