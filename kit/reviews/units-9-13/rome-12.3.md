## Findings
1. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Book 12: the treaty, the duel and the death of Turnus".
   Current: "and if he wins the Latins will keep their laws and Lavinia will found the city (12.161–215)"
   Problem: Aeneas swears that the Trojans will build the walls and Lavinia will give the city its name (*urbique dabit Lavinia nomen*, 12.194). She does not found it.
   Fix: "and if he wins the Latins will keep their laws, the Trojans will build the walls and Lavinia will give the city her name (12.161–215)"
   Basis: *Aeneid* 12.192–194, own reading. Not checked online: the search for the line returned no text page.

2. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Book 11: funerals, council and Camilla".
   Current: "he and Hector were equals, <em>ambo animis, ambo insignes praestantibus armis, / hic pietate prior</em>"
   Problem: the sentence's subject is Diomedes ("Diomedes says that he faced Aeneas"), so "he" reads as Diomedes. *Ambo* at 11.291 means Aeneas and Hector.
   Fix: "Aeneas and Hector were equals, <em>ambo animis, ambo insignes praestantibus armis, / hic pietate prior</em>"
   Basis: *Aeneid* 11.289–292.

3. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Book 8: Evander's Palatine and the shield".
   Current: "and the Cyclopes forge them under Etna (8.416–453)"
   Problem: Virgil puts the forge in caves under Volcania, an island between Sicily and Aeolian Lipari (8.416–422). He calls the caves *Aetnaea* (Etna-like) but does not place them under Etna.
   Fix: "and the Cyclopes forge them in the caves under Vulcan's island, off Sicily by Lipari (8.416–453)"
   Basis: Latin and translation of 8.416–419 at https://www.pantheonpoets.com/poems/vulcans-forge/ ("insula Sicanium iuxta latus Aeoliamque / erigitur Liparen ... antra Aetnaea tonant").

4. [minor] [figure] Section "The Iliadic half and the second proem" (first figure).
   Current: "\"Iliad 10 (the night raid); 12 and 16\", null]"
   Problem: the caption says a blank cell is "a book this unit has no further module for". But the text of book 9 sends Ascanius and Apollo to module 12.6 ("a scene module 12.6 reads"), so the blank cell contradicts the text.
   Fix: "\"Iliad 10 (the night raid); 12 and 16\", \"12.6 (Apollo and Ascanius)\"]". Then delete " A blank cell is a book this unit has no further module for." from the caption.
   Basis: the report's own section "Book 9: the camp and the night raid"; `rome/r-12.6.html`, section "The agents" (Apollo stops Ascanius, 9.638–658).

5. [minor] [attribution] Section "Book 8: Evander's Palatine and the shield" (dispute box).
   Current: "and replaced the cosmos with Rome's history, so that empire becomes the order of the world"
   Problem: Hardie's thesis is that Aeneas' shield is itself a cosmic icon, with city and empire identified with the cosmos. Virgil does not swap the cosmos out for history; "replaced" understates the cosmic reading the box goes on to describe.
   Fix: "and gave Aeneas' shield, with Rome's history on it, the same cosmic meaning, so that empire becomes the order of the world"
   Basis: review of Hardie (1986) by J. Gómez Pallares, *Faventia* 10 (https://ddd.uab.cat/pub/faventia/02107570v10n1-2/02107570v10n1-2p199.pdf): the shield is "the last and most important iconographic symbol between cosmos and imperium", with Rome's development equated with the universe's.

6. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Book 11: funerals, council and Camilla".
   Current: "Aeneas grants the Latins a twelve-day truce (11.133–138)"
   Problem: Aeneas grants the envoys leave to bury their dead (11.106–119). The twelve days are agreed by both sides after Drances' reply (*bis senos pepigere dies*, about 11.133).
   Fix: "Aeneas grants the Latins leave to bury their dead, and both sides agree a twelve-day truce (11.106–138)"
   Basis: Latin text of 11.100–138 at https://www.studentville.it/appunti/libro-11-vv-100-138/.

7. [minor] [pointer] Section "Book 12: the treaty, the duel and the death of Turnus" (death of Turnus).
   Current: "and Achilles refuses in the cruellest speech of the poem (Greek module 10.6)"
   Problem: Greek module 10.6 gives "the poem's most savage speech" to Achilles' refusal of the pact before the duel (22.262–267). For his answer to the dying Hector it says only that he "wishes he could eat him raw" (22.346–348). The pointer gives the superlative to the wrong speech.
   Fix: "and Achilles refuses, wishing he could eat him raw (22.346–348; Greek module 10.6)"
   Basis: `greece/r-10.6.html`, section "Book 22: the death of Hector".

8. [minor] [pointer] Section "Book 10: Pallas and Mezentius".
   Current: "Virgil keeps the tyrant of Cato's version of the legend, which module 1.3 gives, and turns his end into a lament."
   Problem: module 1.3 gives Cato's Mezentius as the king who demands the first fruits owed to the gods (Macrobius, *Saturnalia* 3.5.10). That makes him impious. 1.3 never calls him a tyrant; the tyrant driven out of Caere is Virgil's own.
   Fix: "Virgil keeps the impious king of Cato's version, who claimed the first fruits owed to the gods (module 1.3), and turns his end into a lament."
   Basis: `rome/r-1.3.html`, section "The Roman version: Fabius Pictor to Varro".
