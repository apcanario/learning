   Basis: rome/r-4.4.html, first section and self-test question 1; Livy 31.7.1 (the contio called before the second vote), from memory. −7 words. Self-test question 9 still agrees with the body.

2. [minor] [attribution] Section "The ladder of office after 180".
   Current: "the same chapter records a Baebian law ordering four and six praetors in alternate years, soon abandoned (40.44.2)."
   Problem: Livy 40.44.2 says that four praetors were elected that year, for the first time in many years, under the Baebian law, which ordered four to be elected in alternate years. It does not say the law was abandoned. Because the citation comes after "soon abandoned", Livy seems to say so. The fact itself is right: the praetor lists give four for 179 and six for every year from 178. The writer's source (Wikipedia, "Lex Baebia") calls the law "never observed", but Livy contradicts that, and the report rightly did not follow it.
   Fix: "the same chapter records that four praetors were elected that year under a Baebian law ordering four and six in alternate years (40.44.2), but six were elected every year after."
   Basis: Livy 40.44.2, "praetores quattuor post multos annos lege Baebia creati, quae alternis quaterni ut crearentur iubebat" (from memory); Wikipedia, "List of Roman praetors" (after Broughton): 4 for 179, 6 each for 178 to 174. +11 words.
