### Caption traced

- "Cato's death, and the walk-out, deaths and exiles of the Stoic senators and their families, from Utica to Domitian": matches what is drawn (Cato's death, the 59 walk-out, the deaths of Arria the Elder, Seneca, Thrasea, Rusticus and Senecio, Helvidius' banishment, the expulsion, the two reigns).
- Plutarch, Cato Minor 66–70 → Cato at Utica, April 46 BC: the text attaches it so.
- Pliny, Letters 3.16.6 with Dio 60.16 → the elder Arria's dagger, 42: the text attaches both to the stabbing, and the self-test ties 42 to the dagger with 3.16.6.
- Annals 14.12.1 → walk-out, 59; 15.60–64 → Seneca, 65; 16.33–35 → Thrasea's death and Helvidius' banishment, 66: as in the text.
