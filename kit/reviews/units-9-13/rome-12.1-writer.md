# Rome 12.1 writer notes (Opus 5.5, 1 November 2026)

- Donatus, *Life* 25: the Durham Latin text reads XI years for the *Aeneid*, Rolfe's translation (LacusCurtius) twelve; the report gives eleven and notes the variant. Section numbers 1–46 follow Rolfe/Durham; other editions number differently.
- Donatus, *Life* 31: "from his Spanish campaign of 26–25" is from memory of the Latin (*ex Cantabria*); not checked.
- Horsfall's sceptical view of the life is attributed to his chapter in *A Companion to the Study of Virgil* (1995) from memory; not checked.
- Norden (1924), Wilkinson (1969), Thomas (1988), Knauer (1964), Jackson Knight (1939): views and titles from memory; not checked.
- Goold 1970 against and Austin 1961 for the Helen episode: checked (CAMWS 2016 abstract; CQ abstract). Horsfall 2008 on the clash with Deiphobus: from that abstract; the line range 6.494–547 for the Deiphobus scene is from memory.
- Macrobius, *Saturnalia* 6.2 (Naevius' storm and Venus' complaint) and 1.24.11 (Virgil's letter): the letter's wording checked, its section number not.
- Pliny, *Natural History* 7.114 (Augustus forbade the burning); Quintilian 10.1.56 (Nicander); Columella 10 preface; Seneca, *Letters* 86.15: passage numbers from memory.
- Ennius' *unus homo nobis cunctando restituit rem* cited as quoted in Cicero, *On Old Age* 10; Sophocles, *Ajax* 550–551; Catullus 66.39 for *Aeneid* 6.460: from memory.
- The pairing scheme of the *Eclogues* (1–9, 2–8, 3–7, 4–6, 5 centre, 10 close) is given as a modern reading, unattributed; Clausen's view of the book as planned is checked (BMCR 1995.11.06).
- Translations: Ruden (line for line, iambic pentameter), Bartsch (no added lines, up to six beats, UK 2020, Random House 2021), Ahl (hexameter, wordplay, OUP 2007, Fantham introduction) checked; Bartsch's UK publisher (Profile), Fitzgerald's line count and the quoted opening lines of Dryden, Fitzgerald and Fagles are from memory.
