# Rome 13.6 writer notes (Opus 5.5, 1 November 2026)

- Dio 65(66).13.2 (Musonius excepted from Vespasian's expulsion): Loeb numbering checked on LacusCurtius; module 7.2 cites the same passage as Dio 66.13 (Boissevain), so the forms differ.
- Juvenal 3.116–117 (Celer as Barea's "friend and pupil") and Annals 16.32 (Celer as Barea's client): line and chapter numbers from memory.
- "Some modern accounts add a second exile late in Vespasian's reign and a recall by Titus": no ancient passage found (IEP says Vespasian exiled him again; Jerome's Chronicle not checked).
- Epictetus, Discourses 3.22 section numbers (10, 23–25, 54, 67–72, 76, 81–82, 93–96), 1.19.19–22 (Felicio) and 1.1.26–27 (Thrasea): from memory.
- Photius, Library codex 58 (eight books of Diatribai, twelve of Homiliai): codex number from memory.
- Simplicius: lame from youth, the adopted child, and the preface's "most essential and most moving" wording of Arrian's selection: cited without passage, from memory.
- Ellis's appeal to Handbook 5: commonly reported, not checked against an Ellis text; Beck et al. 1979 p. 8 quotation checked via Robertson's site.
- Poliziano's Latin Handbook (fifteenth century), Carter's English of 1758 (SEP lists the 1759 Dublin printing), Stockdale's captivity 1965–1973: from memory.
- Dobbin 1998 follows Stellwag 1933 on Epictetus' own authorship: from BMCR 1999.11.21. Wirth 1967 (Arrian's own composition) deliberately left out: view known only from a Wikipedia footnote.
- Hays and Reydams-Schils: positions taken from the NYRB exchange page of 29 April 2021, not from Hays' original review; the last dispute box sets Beck and Robertson against Epictetus' own text, with no modern scholar named on that side.
