**Fault: the span.** The bar is labelled "FOUNDING PESSIMIST PAPERS, 1953–1965", and the caption calls it "the founding pessimist papers marked as a span". The span ends at 1965 only because of Putnam's book, which the text never calls a paper. The span's label and the caption therefore say more than the text does.

**Fix.**
- Relabel the span in the text's own words: `Pessimistic reading assembled, 1953–1965`.
- Replace the caption:
