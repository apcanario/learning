## Findings
1. [error] [accuracy] Section "Italian voters after 89".
   Current: "counted 910,000 citizens (<em>Periochae</em> 98)"
   Problem: The Periochae give 900,000 ("censa sunt ciuium capita DCCCC milia", Per. 98). The figure 910,000 is Phlegon of Tralles', not the epitome's; the writer flagged this as unsure.
   Fix: "counted 900,000 citizens (<em>Periochae</em> 98)"
   Basis: Latin text of Periochae 98, https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/livy/liv.per98.shtml; Livius.org translation of Per. 98.3 ("registered 900,000 citizens"), https://www.livius.org/sources/content/livy/livy-periochae-96-100/.

2. [error] [attribution] Section "Actors or audience?".
   Current: "followed politics from 70 to 55 through the crowds in the Forum"
   Problem: Millar's book runs from Sulla's dictatorship to Caesar's crossing of the Rubicon (about 80 to 50), not 70 to 55.
   Fix: "followed politics from Sulla's dictatorship to Caesar's crossing of the Rubicon through the crowds in the Forum"
   Basis: University of Michigan Press description of the book ("the period from the dictatorship of Sulla to Caesar's crossing of the Rubicon"), https://mitpressbookstore.mit.edu/book/9780472088782.

3. [should-fix] [figure] Section "The tribunate as an instrument".
   Current: "(Plutarch, <em>Sulla</em> 8; module 5.2 has the outcome)"
   Problem: Sulla 8 gives the three thousand swordsmen and "a body of young men of the equestrian order" he called his anti-senate, with no number; the six hundred comes from Marius 35. The figure is cited to a passage that does not contain it.
   Fix: "(Plutarch, <em>Sulla</em> 8, <em>Marius</em> 35; module 5.2 has the outcome)". In the bibliography, change the Plutarch line to "Plutarch, *Caesar* 8, 55; *Cato Minor* 26; *Cicero* 35; *Marius* 35; *Pompey* 49; *Sulla* 8. Loeb (Perrin)."
   Basis: Plutarch, Sulla 8 (Perrin), https://cts.perseids.org/read/greekLit/tlg0007/tlg033/perseus-eng2/8.1-9.2; Marius 35 (Perrin: "kept six hundred of the Knights about him as a body-guard, which he called his anti-senate"), https://cts.perseids.org/read/greekLit/tlg0007/tlg031/perseus-eng2/35.1-35.5.

4. [should-fix] [register] Section "Self-test", question 9.
   Current: "The citizen body grew to 910,000 by the census of 70"
   Problem: Must follow finding 1, or the answer will contradict the corrected body.
   Fix: "The citizen body grew to 900,000 by the census of 70"
   Basis: as finding 1.

5. [should-fix] [register] Section "Self-test", question 1.
   Current: "Plutarch (<em>Caesar</em> 55) and the <em>Periochae</em> (115) treat the drop as a census showing war losses;"
   Problem: The body (rightly) says only that the epitomator "also calls it a census of 150,000 citizens"; Periochae 115 gives no earlier figure and says nothing of war losses. The answer claims more than the body.
   Fix: "Plutarch (<em>Caesar</em> 55) treats the drop as a census showing war losses, and the <em>Periochae</em> (115) also call it a census;"
   Basis: Periochae 115 (a census of 150,000 citizens, with no comparison and no cause given), from knowledge, not fetched; the report's own dispute box in "The urban plebs: how many, and who".

6. [minor] [register] Section "Self-test", question 10.
   Current: "which Vanderbroeck and Mouritsen stress against Brunt's picture of spontaneous protest."
   Problem: The body describes Brunt's view as protest by "a populace with real grievances over grain, debt and housing"; "spontaneous" is not in the body and is not a fair one-word summary of Brunt's article.
   Fix: "which Vanderbroeck and Mouritsen stress against Brunt's picture of protest driven by real grievances."
   Basis: the report's own dispute box in "Actors or audience?"; Brunt, "The Roman Mob", Past and Present 35 (1966), from knowledge.

7. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Clodius and Milo, 58–53".
   Current: "after a slave of Clodius' was caught with a dagger Pompey kept to his house"
   Problem: Plutarch, the source cited, has the slave with a sword (the dagger is Cicero's and Asconius' version of the incident); the sentence also needs a comma after the clause.
   Fix: "after a slave of Clodius' was caught with a sword near him, Pompey kept to his house"
   Basis: Plutarch, Pompey 49 (Perrin), https://cts.perseids.org/read/greekLit/tlg0007/tlg045/perseus-eng2/49.1-49.7. The writer's paraphrase "while Clodius remained in office" is right (Perrin: "came no more into the forum as long as Clodius was tribune").
