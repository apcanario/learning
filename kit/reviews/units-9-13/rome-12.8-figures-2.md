Caption faults:
1. "What each later work in this module takes" overclaims. The matrix has nine rows. The module's other later readers, among them Servius, Fulgentius, the magician legends, Douglas, Surrey, Dryden, Eliot, Haecker, Kermode, the September 11 Museum and Heaney, are not in it. The sortes and the monument are also not "works".
2. "(the examples come from the Historia Augusta and Aubrey)" claims something the figure does not show, and it is incomplete: l. 31 also gives Rabelais' Tiers Livre (1546) as an example.
3. "Dante's choice of Virgil as guide (Inferno 1.85–87)": the text attaches 1.85–87 to Dante's answer, "you are my master and my author", from whom he took the fine style. The choice scene it cites runs from the self-naming at 1.70–72 to that answer (l. 45).
4. "The fourth Eclogue through Statius (Purgatorio 22.67–73)": the text gives the fourth Eclogue as a reason for the choice without a passage (l. 47, "through the fourth Eclogue, a prophet"). 22.67–73 is Statius' speech, with the Eclogue rendered into Italian in it. The caption should say both.
5. Proba: the caption does not say that the text gives no passage for the lines the Cento takes. The proem line 23 and Etymologies 1.39.26, the only passages cited, are for her declaration and her authorship.
6. Le Guin: "without a passage" is true of l. 77. But the figure's "Lavinia's silence" has a passage in the text, Aeneid 12.64–69 ("blushes once ... and never speaks"), which the text attaches to its account of the Roman d'Eneas (l. 41). The caption should say where that passage sits.
