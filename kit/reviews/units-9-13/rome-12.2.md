## Findings
1. [error] [accuracy] Section "Book 3: the wanderings".
   Current: "the town is a little Troy with a dry stream called Simois and a Scaean gate (3.300–351)"
   Problem: Virgil's dry stream is named Xanthus (3.350, *arentem Xanthi cognomine rivum*); the Simois is the "false Simois" by whose water Andromache pours her offerings (3.302, *falsi Simoentis ad undam*), not a dry stream. The two rivers are conflated.
   Fix: "the town is a little Troy, with a false Simois, a dry brook named Xanthus and a Scaean gate (3.302, 3.349–351)"
   Basis: Aeneid 3.302 and 3.349–351 (OCT, from knowledge); confirmed by the translation at theoi.com, https://theoi.com/Text/VirgilAeneid3.html ("by the waters of a mimic Simois"; "a dry brook that takes its name from Xanthus").

2. [error] [accuracy] Section "Self-test", question 8.
   Current: "Andromache and Helenus live in a little Troy with a dry Simois and a Scaean gate, honouring an empty tomb (3.300–351)"
   Problem: Same conflation as finding 1, repeated in the answer.
   Fix: "Andromache and Helenus live in a little Troy with a false Simois, a dry Xanthus and a Scaean gate, honouring an empty tomb (3.300–351)"
   Basis: as finding 1.

3. [should-fix] [accuracy] Section "Book 3: the wanderings", dispute box.
   Current: "hold games and a race for Aphrodite at Zacynthus (1.50.3)"
   Problem: Dionysius has the companions institute games for young men, including a foot-race to the temple called "the course of Aeneas and Aphrodite"; the sacrifice is to Aphrodite, the race is not "for" her.
   Fix: "hold games at Zacynthus with a foot-race called the course of Aeneas and Aphrodite (1.50.3)"
   Basis: Dionysius 1.50.3, Cary's Loeb on LacusCurtius, https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Dionysius_of_Halicarnassus/1C*.html

4. [should-fix] [attribution] Section "Book 3: the wanderings", dispute box.
   Current: "in his account Anchises reaches Italy alive (1.64)"
   Problem: Dionysius never narrates Anchises in Italy; the inference rests on 1.64.5, where "some say" the shrine on the Numicius was built by Aeneas for Anchises, "who died in the year before this war". The text drops the qualifier and the section number.
   Fix: "and some, he reports, held that Anchises lived to reach Latium and died the year before the war there (1.64.5)"
   Basis: Dionysius 1.64.5, Cary's Loeb on LacusCurtius (URL as finding 3).

5. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Book 2: the fall of Troy".
   Current: "Demodocus sings the sack in a dozen lines (8.499–520)"
   Problem: The cited span is twenty-two lines.
   Fix: "Demodocus sings the sack in some twenty lines (8.499–520)"
   Basis: Odyssey 8.499–520 (line count of the span cited).

6. [minor] [figure] Section "Book 3: the wanderings".
   Current: "Virgil gives the games a Trojan founder a thousand years earlier."
   Problem: A loose figure with no owner; on Eratosthenes' date for Troy (1184/3, which module 4.1 gives) the gap to Actium is over eleven centuries.
   Fix: "Virgil gives the games a Trojan founder more than a thousand years earlier."
   Basis: Eratosthenes' 1184/3, as rome/r-4.1.html gives it; Actium 31 BC.

7. [minor] [figure] Section "The Odyssean half", figure caption.
   Current: "A blank cell is a book this unit has no further module for."
   Problem: The column is not confined to this unit (book 2's cell is Greek 4.3), and the text does send book 5 matter onward (Nisus and Euryalus to 12.3, the Troy game to Greek 10.12) and book 3's Achaemenides to module 10.1. The caption should claim only what the figure shows.
   Fix: "A blank cell is a book the report does not hand as a whole to another module."
   Basis: the report's own sections; rome/r-12.6.html, greece/r-4.3.html.

8. [minor] [register] Section "Book 1: storm and Carthage", dispute box.
   Current: "whom Plutarch shows quietening a hostile crowd; others have seen Augustus."
   Problem: "Others have seen Augustus" is unnamed (the writer flags it); the box already has named parties (Austin, Harrison, Pöschl), so the unnamed clause can go. Austin's note itself was not reached (see Confirmed).
   Fix: "whom Plutarch shows quietening a hostile crowd."
   Basis: writer's unsure list item 1; Harrison's article confirmed (below) names no single statesman.
