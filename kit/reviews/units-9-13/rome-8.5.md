## Findings
1. [error] [accuracy] Section "Ricimer and the last emperors, 455–476".
   Current: "deposed Romulus, sparing him because he was handsome and sending him with an income of 6,000 solidi to live in Campania (<em>Anonymus Valesianus</em> 8.38)"
   Problem: The source gives two reasons: Odoacer spared Romulus' life out of pity for his youth, and gave him the income because he was handsome. The report makes his looks the reason he was spared.
   Fix: "deposed Romulus, sparing him out of pity for his youth and, because he was handsome, sending him with an income of 6,000 solidi to live in Campania (<em>Anonymus Valesianus</em> 8.38)"
   Basis: Anonymus Valesianus 8.38, "cuius infantiam misertus concessit ei sanguinem, et quia pulcher erat, tamen donavit ei reditum sex milia solidos"; LacusCurtius translation ("in pity for his tender years, granted him his life; and because of his beauty he also gave him an income of six thousand gold-pieces"), https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Excerpta_Valesiana/2*.html

2. [minor] [attribution] Section "Two courts and a general, 395–408".
   Current: "son of a Vandal officer (Orosius 7.38.1)"
   Problem: Orosius says only that Stilicho was of Vandal stock ("Vandalorum ... gentis genere editus"). His father's service as an officer comes from Claudian, not Orosius.
   Fix: "of Vandal descent (Orosius 7.38.1)"
   Basis: Orosius 7.38.1, Latin text and Fear's translation as quoted at https://mmp.acdh-dev.oeaw.ac.at/archiv/stelle/detail/2225

3. [minor] [accuracy] Section "Ricimer and the last emperors, 455–476" (table, Libius Severus row).
   Current: "<td>died; no emperor for two years</td>"
   Problem: Severus died on 14 November 465 and Anthemius was proclaimed on 12 April 467, a gap of about seventeen months, not two years.
   Fix: "<td>died; no emperor until 467</td>"
   Basis: PLRE II (Severus 18, Anthemius 3); standard dates. Not rechecked online: well established.
