## Figure 1 (timeline): PASS

Points traced to the text (I measured each position against the axis ticks; every one sits at its year):

- "55 BC / Toga of manhood at Cremona": "spent his boyhood at Cremona, took the toga of manhood on his fifteenth birthday, in 55 ... (Life 6 ...), then moved to Milan and Rome (Life 7)". The year matches. "At Cremona" is implied by the order of that sentence (boyhood at Cremona, toga, then the move), not stated outright. Acceptable. If a stricter label is wanted: "Toga of manhood".
- "40 BC / Fourth Eclogue, Pollio consul": "It begins in Pollio's consulship: te consule (4.11), so in 40." Matches.
- "29 BC / Georgics read at Atella": "the reading to Octavian at Atella in 29 (Life 27)". Matches.
- "after 23 BC / Books 2, 4 and 6 read to Augustus": "the second, fourth and sixth (Life 32) ... since Marcellus died in 23, the reading came after that." Keeps the text's qualifier ("after"). The dot sits at 23, as the earliest possible date, and the label keeps that from misleading.
- "21 September 19 BC / Dies at Brundisium": "died at Brundisium on 21 September 19 BC (Life 35–36)". Matches.

Spans (each label carries its years and reads as a period):
- "ECLOGUES, C. 42–38 BC": "usually dated about 42–38". Carries "about" as "c.". Bar runs about 42 to 38.
- "GEORGICS, C. 36–29 BC": "seven years in the writing (Life 25) and finished in 29, so begun about 36". Only the start is approximate in the text. The caption says so, so the single "c." on the span is acceptable.
- "AENEID, 29–19 BC": "begun in 29 after the Georgics and unfinished at his death in 19". Matches.

Caption traced: Life 2 and 6 for birth and toga; Life 27 for Atella; Life 32 and 6.883 for the reading, with Marcellus' death in 23; Life 35–36 for the death; 4.11 for the fourth Eclogue; Life 25 for the Georgics' seven years. All are correct. The caption's list of dates the text gives without a passage is also correct: the Eclogues 42–38 (Life 25 is cited only for "about three years"), the Georgics finished in 29 and begun about 36, the Aeneid begun in 29, and Marcellus' death in 23. It flags the Eclogues and the Georgics' start as approximate, as the text does.

Rendering: there is no overlap or clipping in either theme. The stepped point labels (55, 40, after 23, 19) stay clear of one another. Phone frames cut the Eclogues label and the 40 BC label at the frame edge, which the scroll rule covers.

## Figure 2 (matrix): FIX

Rows traced to "The Eclogues as a book": "1 and 9 on the confiscations, 2 and 8 on love's laments, 3 and 7 on singing contests, 4 and 6 on songs that rise above pastoral to prophecy and the making of the world. Poem 5, Daphnis' death and deification, stands at the middle; poem 10 gives the elegist Gallus, lovesick in Arcadia, a farewell that closes the book."
- 1 / 9 The confiscations; 2 / 8 Love's laments; 3 / 7 Singing contests; 4 / 6 Prophecy and the making of the world; 5 / – Daphnis' death and deification, the centre; 10 / – Gallus in Arcadia, the close. All ten poems appear, every pairing and theme matches, and the rows narrow in on the centre in the text's order.
- Headers "POEM / PAIRED WITH" read correctly, and the "–" cells for 5 and 10 read as "unpaired". The theme column has no header. That is optional, but "THEME" would complete the header row.
- Kind: it is a two-column pairing table, which fits what is drawn. It is legible in both themes. The phone frame splits the "PAIRED WITH" column, which the scroll rule covers.

Fault (caption): "a modern reading of the poems, not an ancient one". The text says only "Modern readers map the ten as pairs around a centre". It does not say that no ancient reader did so, and the figure does not show it. "Not an ancient one" is a claim found in neither the text nor the figure.

Fix: replace the caption with:
"The pairing of the ten Eclogues around a centre, as modern readers map it in the section The Eclogues as a book; the text gives no passage for the pairing."

(If the claim is wanted, the text would need a sourced sentence, for example one saying the pairing is a modern reconstruction not found in the ancient commentators, with its source. Without a source it stays out.)

## Figure 3 (flow): PASS

Steps traced in order:
1. "Prose draft in twelve books / Life 23": "first wrote the whole story in prose, divided into twelve books ... (Life 23)". Matches.
2. "Verse composed out of order, struts left / Life 23–24": "keeping to no order (Life 23) ... makeshift lines ... tibicines (Life 24)". Matches.
3. "Books 2, 4 and 6 read to Augustus / Life 32": Matches.
4. "Journey to Greece, fever at Megara, 19 BC / Life 35": "In 19 ... set out for Greece and Asia ... (Life 35). ... Visiting Megara in the heat, he caught a fever". The fever is told after Life 35 with no citation of its own, and the caption says so.
5. "In his last illness, asks for his book-boxes to burn it / Life 39": Matches.
6. "Dies at Brundisium, 21 September 19 BC / Life 35–36": the date and citation come from "The ancient lives", and the caption says so. "The order to burn it" has the death uncited ("died at Brundisium a few days after landing").
7. "Varius and Tucca edit at Augustus' wish / Life 40–41": Life 40 leaves the writings to Varius and Tucca, and Life 41 has the publication at Augustus' wish. The main text names only Varius at Life 41, but the self-test answer gives "Varius and Tucca published it at Augustus' wish with light correction ... (Life 41)", and Servius' "rule Augustus set the editors" is plural. Supported.
8. "Published with the half-lines left / Life 41": "leaving even the incomplete lines as they were (Life 41)". Matches.

Order: the steps follow the order of events in the text. The prose draft and versifying come first, then the reading (after 23), then the journey and fever (19), then the last illness, then the death, then the editing and publication. Placing the book-boxes (Life 39) before the death (Life 35–36) is correct as a sequence of events, though Donatus' section numbers do not rise monotonically. The caption's "in the order of events as Donatus tells them" is accurate as written.

Caption traced: "from the sections Making the Aeneid and The order to burn it" is right (steps 1–3 come from the first section, 4–8 from the second). The notes on the fever and on the death date are correct.

Rendering: there is no clipping or overlap in either theme. Box 4 wraps "19" and "BC" onto separate lines. That is readable and not a fault, though a non-breaking space in the data ("19&nbsp;BC") would keep them together. The return arrow from box 4 to box 5 reads clearly. On a phone the two rows split across frames (1–2 and 5–6 in frame 1, 3–4 and 7–8 in frame 2), which the scroll rule covers.
