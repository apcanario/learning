# Rome 12.7 writer notes: claims I was unsure of or could not verify

- Servius on 6.893 ("wants all that has been said understood as false"): from memory, not checked against Thilo–Hagen.
- Servius on 12.940 (pius in thinking of sparing, pietas in killing for Evander): checked only as paraphrased by Gaskin (HSCP 2020 draft), not against the Latin.
- Parry first hearing the "private voice" in the Umbro lament (7.759–760): the lament is attributed to Parry on Wikipedia; that it opens his argument is from memory.
- Galinsky 1988: citation checked (AJP 109, 321–348); the argument (Aristotelian just anger against a Stoic condemnation of all anger) is from memory of the article, not checked against its text.
- Otis 1963: "subjective style" and the moral-education reading (book 6 as turning point) from memory; date (Clarendon 1963) checked.
- Pöschl calling Turnus a "dark demon of passion": quoted from a 2006 MA thesis citing p. 15 of the English translation; not seen directly.
- Putnam, The Poetry of the Aeneid (1965): the four books (2, 5, 8, 12) from memory; The Humanness of Heroes (2011) summary from West's review only.
- Brooks, "Discolor aura" (1953): journal (AJP 74) and pages from memory; "splendid despair" from Wikipedia's summary.
- Thomas 2001 and Kallendorf 2007: content from BMCR reviews (McGill; Moul), not the books.
- Stahl 2016 and Tarrant 2012 (anger partly at himself): from the publisher's blurb and West's review respectively.
