## The sample and the split

The 23 Greek Opus reports were one stratum in the plan. The six-report sample gave a stratum mean of 4.44 with at most one factual error per report, which clears the rule (mean ≥ 4.4, no report with more than one error). But the sample divides by provenance: unit 12 (written with a fresh checker each) scored 4.8 and 4.8; 10.4, 10.9, 11.3 and 11.7 (written without a checker) scored 4.0, 4.5, 4.5 and 4.1, mean 4.28, below the bar, and their weakness is one dimension, disputes with unnamed sides, which a mechanical scan found across the unsampled units-10-to-11 reports and almost nowhere in unit 12. Decision (Fable 5.1, with the reasons given to Pedro at the time): unit 12's six unsampled reports clear on the sample as "Opus 5.5, reviewed with Fable 5.1 (sampled)"; the eleven unsampled reports of units 10 and 11 are audited in full.

## What is real rather than noise

1. **Dispute boxes with one or both sides unnamed** (dimension 5): units 10–11 mean 3.0 against 5.0 in the baseline and 4.5 in unit 12. Five of the nine units-10-to-11 reports scored 2 or 3. Every case was fixed with names and works under 13.5; two boxes were recast around the ancient evidence where no modern name could be given with certainty.
2. **Modern works by surname and year only** (dimension 2, every report at 4): the same habit the Rome review found; fixed in each audited report.
3. **Factual slips are small and few**: 6 in 27,830 words of the units-10-to-11 reports (0.22 per 1,000; Rome 0.11, Greek Fable 0). None load-bearing: a tale called Cretan that is not (10.4), Lloyd-Jones given the view he argued against (10.7), Lorimer's first name (10.11), Herodotus's 800 years read the wrong way (10.10), Gladstone's office and Blegen's date (10.12).
4. **Depth, carry-forward and style hold** (4.1, 4.8, 4.8): the cohort argues and ends sections on reasons as the baseline does; no padding found, and length is no longer scored.
5. **Not found**: hedging, retelling of survey modules (the 13.3 pairs put repetition at 5–30 percent, all passing), weak self-tests.

**Does the Opus cohort read as if the same author wrote it?** Unit 12 yes, without qualification. Units 10–11 yes in argument and structure, no in the discipline of naming both sides of a dispute: that is a pipeline effect (no checker) rather than a model effect, since unit 12, same model with a checker, does not show it.
