# Greece 2.1: Cosmogony: Chaos to Zeus: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Library* 1.1.2–1.2.1 (Ouranos' prisoners; Metis' drug); 1.1.6 (Dicte, Amaltheia, Curetes); 1.2.1; 1.6.1–2 (Gigantomachy); 1.6.3 (Typhon).
- Aristophanes, *Birds* 693–702 (the Orphic egg).
- Aristotle, *Metaphysics* 983b; 984b.
- Callimachus, *Hymn to Zeus* 4–9.
- Damascius, *De principiis* 123 (the Orphic *Rhapsodies*).
- Derveni papyrus (commentary on an Orphic theogony, c. 400 BC); cited without column numbers.
- Enuma Elish (Babylonian epic), Tablets VI–VII; cited as a whole, no edition named.
- Hebrew Bible: Genesis 1; Genesis 6:1–4; Psalm 74:13–14; Job 26:12.
- Hesiod, *Theogony* 53–62; 116–122 (116, 117, 119, 120–122); 118–119 (bracketed by some editors); 123; 124–125; 126–127; 129–132; 132–138; 135–136; 139–146; 147–153; 154–159; 160–172; 176–182; 183–187; 185–186; 188–200; 207–210; 211–232; 217; 337–370; 371–374; 375–382; 383–403; 390–396; 397–398; 404–411; 421–428; 453–458; 459–465; 463–465; 477–484; 485–491; 493–497; 498–500; 501–506; 507–511; 517–520; 617–620; 624–663; 629–638; 639–663; 687–712; 717–725; 732–735; 820–835; 836–838; 839–880; 881–885; 886–900; 891–893; 901–906; 901–929; 904; 915–917. *Works and Days* 635–640.
- Homer, *Iliad* 1.396–406; 2.783; 8.478–481; 14.201; 14.203–204; 14.246; 14.259–261; 14.279; 15.187–193. *Odyssey* 7.59.
- *Homeric Hymn to Aphrodite* 22–23.
- *Homeric Hymn to Apollo* 305–355.
- Horace, *Odes* 3.4.
- Hittite and Hurrian texts: the *Song of Kumarbi* (*Kingship in Heaven*) and the *Song of Ullikummi*; see Corpora below.
- Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 1.151–162.
- Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 10.24.6.
- Philo of Byblos (Sanchuniathon), *Phoenician History*; cited without fragment numbers (standard edition: FGrH 790).
- Pindar, *Nemean* 1.67–69; *Pythian* 1.15–28; 4.291.
- Plato, *Cratylus* 402b; *Symposium* 178b.
- Aeschylus, *Prometheus Unbound* (lost); cited only for its chorus of released Titans.
- Ugaritic Baal cycle (Baal and Yam); cited as a parallel, no text or edition named.
