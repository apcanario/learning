# Greece 4.4: The Returns and the Odyssey: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Epitome* 6 (generally); 6.1 (Nestor); 6.7–11 (Ajax and Nauplius); 6.9 (Diomedes' wife); 6.9–10 (Idomeneus and Leucus); 6.12–14 (Neoptolemus, Molossus).
- Aristotle, *Poetics* 1451a (one action); 1459b (the *Odyssey* complex and ethical; the *Iliad* simple and pathetic).
- [Aristotle] (Pseudo-), *Mirabilia* (*De mirabilibus auscultationibus*) 107 (Philoctetes' bow at Macalla).
- Diodorus Siculus 5.79.4 (Idomeneus and Meriones at Knossos).
- Epic Cycle, *Nostoi* (*Returns*, attributed to Agias of Troezen; Eumelus of Corinth for some fragments): known through Proclus' *Chrestomathy*. Eustathius and the Suda are cited for the poet's identity (no passages given).
- Euripides, *Andromache* 1085–1165 (also cited in 4.5); *Helen* 68–163 (Teucer in Egypt); *Trojan Women* 77–94.
- Herodotus, *Histories* 2.113–120.
- Homer, *Odyssey* (by book and line): 1.32–43; 1.160; 3.130–312 (with 3.130–183; 3.180–181; 3.182–183; 3.190; 3.191–192; 3.276–312; 3.311); 4.82–89; 4.227–230; 4.351–570; 4.499–511; 4.499–569; 4.561–569; 5.203–224; 6.181–185; 8.219–220; 9.19–20; 9.39–61; 9.80–81; 9.82–104; 9.105–566; 9.405–414; 9.502–505; 10.1–79; 10.80–132; 10.133–574; 11.100–137; 11.488–491; 11.505–537; 12.165–200; 12.201–259; 12.260–402; 12.403–453; 13.291–299; 16.172–219; 16.245–253; 17.290–327; 17.485–487; 18.125–157; 19.386–507; 21.11–41; 21.128–129; 21.188–225; 21.404–423; 22.35; 23.85–230; 23.177–230; 23.296 (the *telos* of Aristophanes of Byzantium and Aristarchus, reported in the scholia); 24.315–348. The report also gives a book-by-book summary of the poem without locators. *Iliad* 5 (Diomedes wounds Aphrodite; cited without lines).
- Horace, *Ars Poetica* 148–149 (*in medias res*); *Odes* 1.7.21–32 (Teucer).
- Isocrates, *Evagoras* 18.
- Justin, *Epitome of Pompeius Trogus* 44.3.2–3.
- Lycophron, *Alexandra* 592–632 (Diomedes' companions as birds); 911–929 (Philoctetes in Italy); 1141–1173 (Locrian maidens).
- Mimnermus fr. 22 West (Diomedes and Aphrodite; no edition named).
- Pacuvius and Sophocles, *Teucer* (lost; no fragments given).
- Pausanias 10.24.6 (tomb of Neoptolemus).
- Pindar, *Nemean* 4.46; 7.34–47; 10.7; *Paean* 6.98–120.
- Plato, *Phaedrus* 243a, with Stesichorus fr. 192 PMG (Palinode).
- [Longinus] (Pseudo-), *On the Sublime* 9.11–15.
- Servius, commentary on the *Aeneid*, at 3.121 (Idomeneus' vow).
- Strabo, *Geography* 1.2.9–19; 6.1.3; 6.3.9; 7.7.8; 14.1.27.
- Thucydides, *History* 6.2.1.
- Virgil, *Aeneid* 1.619–626; 3.121–122; 3.294–336; 3.400–402; 11.243–295.

## Modern works
- Erich Auerbach, "Odysseus' Scar", ch. 1 of *Mimesis: The Representation of Reality in Western Literature*, trans. Willard R. Trask (Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1953) (German original Bern: Francke, 1946).
- Philip Whaley Harsh, "Penelope and Odysseus in *Odyssey* XIX", *American Journal of Philology* 71 (1950) 1–21.
- Alfred Heubeck et al., *A Commentary on Homer's Odyssey*, 3 vols (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1988–92). The report cites "Heubeck in the Oxford commentary", "Stephanie West" and "Heubeck, Russo" without volume numbers; the volume authorship and dates are from memory (not verified).
- Richard Janko, *Homer, Hesiod and the Hymns: Diachronic Development in Epic Diction* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1982).
- Alan B. Lloyd, *Herodotus Book II: Commentary 99–182*, Études préliminaires aux religions orientales dans l'Empire romain 43 (Leiden: Brill, 1988) (identified from context: the report cites "Alan Lloyd's commentary" for Herodotus 2.113–120, which falls in this volume).
- Denys Page, *The Homeric Odyssey* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1955).
- Friedrich August Wolf, *Prolegomena ad Homerum* (Halle: Libraria Orphanotrophei, 1795) (imprint from memory; not verified).
- M. L. West (ed. and trans.), *Greek Epic Fragments from the Seventh to the Fifth Centuries BC*, Loeb Classical Library (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 2003).
- M. L. West, *The Making of the Odyssey* (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2014).
