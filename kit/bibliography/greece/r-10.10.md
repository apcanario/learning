# Greece 10.10: Troy the place: Hisarlik and the historicity question: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Demetrius of Scepsis: reached only through Strabo (below); no fragment edition cited.
- Duris of Samos (date of the war, 1334/3 BC), Eratosthenes (1184/3 BC), and the Parian Marble (1209/8 BC): cited for dates only, without fragment numbers.
- Herodotus, *Histories* 1.1–5; 2.145; 7.43.
- Homer, *Iliad* 2.494–759 (Catalogue of Ships); 5.640–42; 6.433–39; 10.261–65; 22.147–56.

## Corpora and inscriptions
- Hittite texts cited by Laroche's *Catalogue des textes hittites* (CTH) numbers: CTH 76 (treaty of Muwatalli II with Alaksandu of Wilusa); CTH 181 (Tawagalawa letter); CTH 182 (Milawata letter); CTH 191 (Manapa-Tarhunta letter). The texts are collected in Beckman, Bryce and Cline (above).
