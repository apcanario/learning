## Not resolved
- "Edmonds (1999, 2013)": 2013 is *Redefining Ancient Orphism* (above). The 1999 item is probably "Tearing Apart the Zagreus Myth: A Few Disparaging Remarks on Orphism and Original Sin", *Classical Antiquity* 18 (1999) 35–73, but this was not confirmed, so it is not listed as a full reference.
- "Bernabé" (Orphic anthropogony, with Graf and Johnston): named without work or year.
- "Cumont" (Iranian derivation of Mithraism): named without work or year.
- "Gordon and Clauss", "Merkelbach" (on Mithraism): named without works or years.
- "the 2013 corpus of Dakaris, Vokotopoulou and Christidis" (Dodona lead tablets): given without title; the corpus is probably *Ta chresteria elasmata tes Dodones ton anaskaphon D. Evangelidi* (Athens: Archaeological Society, 2013) but this was not confirmed.
- "the excavations of Evangelidis and Dakaris" (Dodona): no publication named.
