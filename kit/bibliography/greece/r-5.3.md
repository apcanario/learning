# Greece 5.3: Hoplites, lawgivers and tyrants: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Alcaeus, fr. 348 (Voigt) ("base-born Pittacus" set up as tyrant); edition: E.-M. Voigt, *Sappho et Alcaeus*.
- Archilochus, fr. 3 West ("not with bows or slings"); fr. 19 West (Gyges, "nor desire great tyranny"); edition: M. L. West, *Iambi et Elegi Graeci*.
- Aristotle (attributed), *Athenaion Politeia* (*Athenian Constitution*) 4; 4.1; 14; 14–15; 14–19; 15.4; 16.
- Aristotle, *Politics* 1.1257a31–41; 2.1271b25; 2.1274a22–31; 2.1274b15–18; 2.1274b18–23; 3.1279b; 3.1285a30–b1; 4.1295a; 4.1297b16–28; 5.1310b; 5.1310b26; 5.1310b29; 5.1313a34–1314a29; 5.1315b12–21; 5.1315b22–29; 5.1315b27–28.
- Cicero, *De Legibus* 2.15 (Timaeus denying Zaleucus existed).
- Demetrius of Phalerum, on the Seven Sages and Damasias (via Diogenes Laertius 1.40–42).
- Dicaearchus, on the Sages as "shrewd men with a turn for legislation" (via Diogenes Laertius 1.40).
- Diogenes Laertius, *Lives of the Philosophers* 1.40–42; 1.74–81; 1.75.
- Ephorus, on Zaleucus (in Strabo 6.1.8) and Pheidon (in Strabo 8.3.33).
- Herodotus, *Histories* 1.29–33; 1.59; 1.59.6; 1.59–64; 1.65–66; 1.94; 3.39; 3.39–60; 3.40–43; 3.48–53; 3.54–56; 3.60; 3.120–125; 5.55; 5.62–65; 5.67; 5.67–68; 5.68; 5.92; 5.92ζ; 5.92η; 5.99; 6.126–130; 6.127.
- Hesiod, *Works and Days* 654–659.
- Homer, *Iliad* 13.130–133.
- Nicolaus of Damascus, on Cypselus as polemarch before his coup (no fragment number given).
- Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 6.22.2.
- Plato, *Protagoras* 343a; *Republic* 8–9.
- Plutarch, *Life of Lycurgus* 6 (the Great Rhetra).
- Plutarch, *Life of Solon* 4; 17; 27.
- Plutarch, *On the Malice of Herodotus* 21.
- Pollux, *Onomasticon* 9.83 (rival claimants to the invention of coinage, including Pheidon).
- Solon, frr. 32–33 West.
- Strabo, *Geography* 6.1.8; 8.3.33; 10.1.12; 13.1.38.
- Thucydides, *History* 1.13.1; 1.13.6; 1.15.3; 1.17; 1.18.1; 6.54; 6.54–59; 6.56–58.
- Tyrtaeus, fr. 4 West (*Eunomia*); fr. 11 West (including 11.35–38).
- Xenophanes, fr. 4 (quoted in Pollux, *Onomasticon* 9.83) on Lydian coinage; edition: West, *Iambi et Elegi Graeci*, or Diels–Kranz (the report says only "fr. 4").
