# Greece 4.3: The fall of Troy: the lost Epic Cycle: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aeschylus, *Agamemnon* 1202–1212 (Cassandra's curse); *Psychostasia* (lost; weighing of Achilles and Memnon; cited via Plutarch and Pollux below).
- Alcaeus fr. 298 (Ajax's crime and Athena's wrath).
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Library* 3.12.6 (Paris and Oenone); 3.13.6 (Thetis and the fire). *Epitome* 5 (generally); 5.1 (Penthesilea); 5.3 (Achilles' ankle); 5.22 (Cassandra); 5.23 (Hecuba).
- Aristophanes, *Knights* 1056 (scholia; source of *Little Iliad* fr. 2 West).
- Aristotle, *Poetics* 1459b (ten plays from the *Little Iliad*; the *Odyssey* as complex).
- Bacchylides and Euphorion (cited via Servius on *Aeneid* 2.201 for Laocoön's offence; no fragments given).
- Conon, *Narrations* 34 (the Diomedean compulsion).
- Dares Phrygius 34; Dictys of Crete 4.11 (Achilles ambushed with the promise of Polyxena).
- Dio Chrysostom, *Oration* 52 (compares the three *Philoctetes* plays).
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 1.45–48 (Hellanicus and Menecrates on Aeneas); 1.48 (Sophocles' *Laocoön*); 1.68–69 (the Palladium at Rome); 1.72 (Hellanicus on Aeneas).
- Epic Cycle: *Aethiopis* (Arctinus), *Little Iliad* (Lesches; frr. 2, 3, 25, 28, 29 West cited), *Iliou Persis* / *Sack of Ilion* (Arctinus), *Cypria* fr. 34 West; Proclus' summaries as preserved by Photius, *Bibliotheca* cod. 239 and in the Venetus A. Fragments quoted in scholia, Pausanias and Tzetzes.
- Euripides, *Hecuba* 518–582 (Polyxena); 1259–1273 (Hecuba's transformation); *Trojan Women* 16–17; 709–739; 860–1059; 1123–1250; *Philoctetes* (lost, 431 BC; no fragments given).
- Hellanicus of Lesbos (via Dionysius 1.45–48, 1.72; no FGrH number given).
- Hesiod, *Theogony* 984–985 (Memnon).
- Homer, *Iliad* 2 (Thersites, no lines given); 7.303–305; 13.365–366; 19.416–417; 20.302–308; 21.166–167; 22.60–71; 22.209–213; 22.359–360; 24.734–738. *Odyssey* 4.187–188; 4.242–264; 8.517–520; 11.421–423; 11.467–540; 11.519–521; 11.543–567; 24.36–94.
- Hyginus, *Fabulae* 107 (death of Achilles); 110 (Polyxena); 135 (Laocoön).
- Ibycus fr. 307 (Polyxena).
- Livy 1.1 (Aeneas and Antenor).
- Lycophron, *Alexandra* (generally, as Cassandra's prophecy); 348–364; 1141–1173.
- Menecrates of Xanthus (via Dionysius 1.48; no work given).
- Naevius and Ennius (Aeneas in Latin epic; no passages given).
- Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 13.394–398 (the hyacinth); *Fasti* 6.421–436 (the Palladium); *Heroides* 5 (Oenone).
- Pausanias 10.27.2 (Priam's death in the *Little Iliad* and Polygnotus).
- Pindar, *Nemean* 7.20–27; 8.23–34; *Olympian* 2.79–80; *Paean* 6 (cited generally and at 6.112–117); *Pythian* 6.28–42.
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 36.37 (the Vatican Laocoön).
- Plutarch, *Moralia* 17a; Pollux, *Onomasticon* 4.130 (Aeschylus' *Psychostasia*).
- Propertius 3.11.13–16.
- Quintus of Smyrna, *Posthomerica* 1.18–32; 1.657–674; 10.259–489; 12.525–585; 13.420–429.
- Servius, commentary on the *Aeneid*, at 2.201 and 6.57.
- Sophocles, *Ajax* 646–692; 815–865; *Laocoön* and *Sinon* (lost; no fragments given).
- Statius, *Achilleid* 1.133–134; 1.269–270.
- Stesichorus (*Iliou Persis*, cited via the Tabula Iliaca; no fragment given).
- Tryphiodorus, *Sack of Troy* (cited generally).
- Tzetzes (source for *Little Iliad* frr. 25 and 29 and the capture of Aeneas; no work or passage given).
- Virgil, *Aeneid* 2.13–249 (generally); 2.50–53; 2.57–198; 2.162–170; 2.199–233; 2.246–247; 2.506–558.

## Modern works
- Jonathan S. Burgess, *The Tradition of the Trojan War in Homer and the Epic Cycle* (Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press, 2001).
- M. L. West (ed. and trans.), *Greek Epic Fragments from the Seventh to the Fifth Centuries BC*, Loeb Classical Library (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 2003). The report calls it "Loeb Greek Epic Fragments, 2003".
- M. L. West, *The Epic Cycle: A Commentary on the Lost Troy Epics* (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2013).

## Corpora and inscriptions

## Not resolved
- "Severyns, Davies, West" (editors preferring the grammarian Proclus), and "collected by Bernabé, Davies and West": no titles or years given.
- "Monro observed that the Odyssey never narrates an event told in the Cycle": no work named.
- Fragment citations to the *Little Iliad* (frr. 2, 3, 25, 28, 29) and *Cypria* fr. 34 are "West" numbers; the report does not say whether these follow the 2003 Loeb or his 2013 commentary.
- Vase evidence (Mykonos pithos, Exekias' amphorae, Penthesilea Painter's cup, François Vase, Chalcidian amphora, Kleophrades Painter's hydria, Tyrrhenian amphora): cited without catalogue numbers.
