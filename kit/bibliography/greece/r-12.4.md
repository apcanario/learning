# Greece 12.4: Aristotle: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aelian, *Varia Historia* 14.1 (Aristotle's letter on the loss of Delphic honours).
- Alexander of Aphrodisias, *On Fate* (addressed to Septimius Severus and Caracalla; cited without locator), and his identification of the productive intellect with god.
- Aquinas, Thomas, *Summa Theologiae* I q.2 a.3; I q.46 a.2. *On the Unity of the Intellect against the Averroists* (1270).
- Aristotle, *Categories* 1b25–27; 2a11–19; 2b5–6.
- Aristotle, *Prior Analytics* 24b18–20; 25b37–39. *Posterior Analytics* 71b19–22; 2.19. *Topics* 100b21–23. *Sophistical Refutations* 34, 184b1–3.
- Aristotle, *Physics* 1.7; 2.8, 198b16–199b33; 4.8; 8.10; 192b21–23; 200a10–13; 201a10–11; 212a20–21; 219b1–2. *On the Heavens* 1.2–3.
- Aristotle, *Metaphysics* 1003a21; 1003a33–b10; 1005b19–20; 1006a11–24; 1028a10; 1028b2–4; 1028b33–36; 1029a10–28; 1038b8–12; 1041b27–28; 1049b4–5 (Θ8); 1071b3–22; 1072b3; 1073b1–1074a14; 1074b34–35; 1075a11–15; books K, Δ, B, Γ, E named for doublets and lexicon. Standard text: W. Jaeger, Oxford Classical Texts (1957).
- Aristotle, *History of Animals* 491a7–14; 6.3. *Parts of Animals* 639b11–21. *Generation of Animals* 729a9–11; 736b27–28; 737a27–28.
- Aristotle, *On the Soul* 412a27–b6; 412b6–9; 412b18–22; 2.3; 424a17–24; 429a24; 430a1; 430a10–25.
- Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* 1097a25–b21; 1098a18–20; 1100a5–9; 1101a6–8; 1102a26; 1145b2–7; 1155a22–26; 1155b17–1156a10; 1156b7–12; 1160a31–1161a9; 1166a31–32; 1169b33–1170a4; 1174b31–33; 1177b33; 1178a9–10. *Eudemian Ethics* (books 4–6 shared with *NE* 5–7). Standard text: I. Bywater, Oxford Classical Texts (1894).
- Aristotle, *Politics* 1252b5–9; 1253b4–11; 1253b33–1254a1; 1254b20–23; 1254b27–1255a1; 1255a3–b4; 1256b15–22; 1258b2–8; 1259a37–b17; 1260a12–14; 1278b31; 1279b34–1280a6; 1281a40–b10; 1293b33–34; 1328b33–1329a2.
- Aristotle, *Rhetoric* 1354a1; 1355b25–26; 1356a1–4; 1356b4–5; 1358b6–8; 1378a30–32. *Poetics* 1448b4–19; 1450b26–27; 1451b5–11. *Constitution of the Athenians* (cited as the work returned on a papyrus published in 1891). Lost dialogues named: *Eudemus*, *Protrepticus*, *On Philosophy*.
- Athenaeus 1.3a–b.
- Averroes (Ibn Rushd), short, middle and long commentaries; Avicenna (Ibn Sina), *The Healing*; Al-Farabi: all cited generally, without locators or editions.
- Boethius, Latin translations of *Categories* and *On Interpretation* (cited generally).
- Cicero, *Academica* 2.119.
- Dante, *Inferno* 4.131; 4.144.
- Diogenes Laertius 5.1–35 (incl. 5.2, 5.3, 5.7–8, 5.11–16, 5.22–27).
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *First Letter to Ammaeus* 5.
- Iamblichus, *Protrepticus* (as the source from which Bywater recovered Aristotle's *Protrepticus*).
- Philoponus (objection to Aristotle's account of projectile motion; cited generally).
- Plato, *Gorgias* 462b–465a. Plato, *Sixth Letter* (addressed to Hermias, Erastus and Coriscus; cited without locator).
- Plutarch, *Sulla* 26.
- Porphyry, *Life of Plotinus* 24. *Isagoge* (cited generally).
- Simplicius, *Commentary on Aristotle's Physics* (Eudemus asking Theophrastus for a sound text; cited without page).
- Strabo 13.1.54; 13.1.57.

## Corpora and inscriptions
- *FD* III 1.400 (Delphic decree crowning Aristotle and Callisthenes for the list of Pythian victors, before 329 BC): *Fouilles de Delphes* III, *Épigraphie*, fasc. 1 (Paris: de Boccard) (edition and editor not verified; the writer confirmed the decree's content online).
