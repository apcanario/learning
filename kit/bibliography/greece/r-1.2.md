# Greece 1.2: Mycenaean Greece: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Homer, *Iliad* 2.494–759 (Catalogue of Ships); 6.117 (body-covering shield); 10.261–265 (boar's-tusk helmet); 11.632–637 (Nestor's cup).
- Homer, *Odyssey* 19.188 (cave of Eileithyia at Amnisos).
- Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 2.16.6 (the graves of Agamemnon and his companions).

## Modern works
- Gary Beckman, Trevor Bryce and Eric Cline, *The Ahhiyawa Texts*, Writings from the Ancient World 28 (Atlanta: Society of Biblical Literature, 2011). The report cites it for the Hittite texts naming Ahhiyawa (about 28 texts).
- M. I. Finley, *The World of Odysseus* (New York: Viking Press, 1954).
- Susan Sherratt, "'Reading the texts': Archaeology and the Homeric question", *Antiquity* 64 (1990) 807–824 (identified from context: the report gives "Susan Sherratt (1990)" for the layered-tradition view of Homer; the article's title and journal are confirmed, the pages are from memory).
- Michael Ventris and John Chadwick, "Evidence for Greek Dialect in the Mycenaean Archives", *Journal of Hellenic Studies* 73 (1953) 84–103. The machine list's "Journal of Hellenic Studies 73" is this article.
- Michael Ventris and John Chadwick, *Documents in Mycenaean Greek* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1956); named by the report as appearing weeks after Ventris's death in 1956.
- Anthony M. Snodgrass, "An Historical Homeric Society?", *Journal of Hellenic Studies* 94 (1974) 114–125 (identified from context: this report says only "Anthony Snodgrass called it an amalgam"; report 1.3 gives "Snodgrass (1974)" for the same point; pages from memory, not verified).

## Corpora and inscriptions
- Linear B tablets, cited by series and number: Pylos Er 312; Ep 704; Aa/Ab/Ad series (the ~750 women); Tn 316; An 657 (and related o-ka tablets); Jn 829; Sa (wheels); Ta 641 (tripod tablet read by Blegen). Knossos Sc, Sd, Se, Sf series (chariots and corslets). The report does not name the edition it used. Standard editions: Ventris and Chadwick, *Documents in Mycenaean Greek*, 2nd edn (Cambridge, 1973), for selected texts and interpretation; Bennett and Olivier, *The Pylos Tablets Transcribed* (Rome, 1973–76), for Pylos.
- Hittite texts from Hattusa naming Ahhiyawa and Wilusa, as the report lists them: Indictment of Madduwatta; Annals of Tudhaliya I/II; Alaksandu Treaty (Muwatalli II, c. 1280); Manapa-Tarhunta Letter; Tawagalawa Letter (Hattusili III, c. 1250); Shaushgamuwa Treaty (Tudhaliya IV, c. 1220). No edition is given other than the collection above.
- Statue base of Amenhotep III from Kom el-Hetan (the "Aegean list"): no edition cited.
- Uluburun shipwreck finds: no publication cited.
