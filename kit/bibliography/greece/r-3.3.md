# Greece 3.3: Theseus and Athens: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aeschylus, *Eumenides* 685–690.
- Alcman and Stesichorus, cited for the early abduction of Helen by Theseus; no fragment numbers given.
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Library* 2.5.12; 3.15.6–16.2 (with 3.15.6–7 and 3.15.7 cited separately). Apollodorus, *Epitome* 1.1–24, cited as 1.1–4 (road labours); 1.5; 1.5–6 (Medea and Aegeus); 1.7–9 (Ariadne); 1.16–17 (Amazons); 1.18–19 (Phaedra); 1.23–24 (Hades); 1.24 (Scyros). Loeb (Frazer) is the standard edition of both.
- Aristotle, *Poetics* 1451a. Aristotle (attributed), *Constitution of the Athenians* 41.2.
- Bacchylides 17 (17.33–36 cited); 18 (18.16–30 cited). Standard edition: Snell–Maehler (Teubner).
- Callimachus, *Hymn to Delos* 307–315. Callimachus, *Hecale*, cited without lines.
- Catullus 64.
- Cleidemus, Hellanicus, Philochorus, Paeon of Amathus: Atthidographers and other writers read only in quotation, chiefly through Plutarch, *Theseus* (17.3 Hellanicus; 20 Paeon; 19 Philochorus and Cleidemus; 26.1–2). Fragment numbers (FGrH) are not given in the report.
- Diodorus Siculus 4.59–63.
- Euripides, *Aegeus* (lost). Euripides, *Heracles* 619. Euripides, *Hippolytus* (428 BC; Phaedra, Hippolytus). Euripides, *Suppliant Women* 403–408.
- Herodotus, *Histories* 1.67–68 (Orestes' bones); 6.108; 9.27; 9.73.
- Hesiod, *Theogony* 947–949 (Ariadne and Dionysus). Hesiod, fr. 298 (Theseus and Aigle, via Plutarch, *Theseus* 20).
- Homer, *Iliad* 1.265; 2.552; 3.144. Homer, *Odyssey* 11.321–325 (Ariadne on Dia); 11.631; 21.295–304 (Eurytion).
- Isocrates, *Helen* 18–20; 35–37.
- Ovid, *Heroides* 10 (Ariadne to Theseus). Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 12 (Centauromachy).
- Pausanias 1.3.3 (Theseus with Demokratia); 1.15.3 (Stoa Poikile); 1.17.2–3 (the Theseion); 1.17.6; 1.22.5; 1.27.10; 2.32.1–4; 2.33.1.
- Plutarch, *Theseus* 1.3; 3; 6–7; 8–11; 10.2–3; 12; 13; 14; 15; 17.3; 17.5; 19; 20; 21; 22–23; 24–25; 26–28; 26.1–2; 29.3; 30; 31; 35; 35.5; 36.1; 36.2. Plutarch, *Cimon* 8.5–7.
- Sophocles, *Oedipus at Colonus*, cited without lines (Theseus received Oedipus).
- Thucydides 1.98.2; 2.15; 2.15.1–2.
- Lost epic *Theseis*, known only from the allusion in Aristotle, *Poetics* 1451a.

See also 3.2.
