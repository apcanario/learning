# Greece 5.6: Lyric poets and the first philosophers: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Alcaeus, frr. 6; 129; 332; 346 (Voigt numbering as the report's table gives them). Edition: E.-M. Voigt, *Sappho et Alcaeus* (Amsterdam, 1971).
- Alcman, fr. 1 PMGF (Louvre Partheneion). Edition: M. Davies, *Poetarum Melicorum Graecorum Fragmenta*.
- Anacreon, frr. 358; 417 PMG. Edition: D. L. Page, *Poetae Melici Graeci*.
- Anaxagoras, DK 59 B11; B12; B17.
- Anaximander, DK 12 B1 (via Simplicius).
- Archilochus, frr. 5; 114; 122 (the eclipse); 196a West (Cologne epode). Edition: M. L. West, *Iambi et Elegi Graeci*.
- Aristotle, *Constitution of the Athenians* 5–12 (quoting Solon).
- Aristotle, *Metaphysics* A 3, 983b–984a; 985b; 985b–986a; 986b.
- Aristotle, *On the Heavens* 295b.
- Aristotle, *On the Soul* 405a; 411a.
- Aristotle, *Physics* 239b (Zeno's four motion paradoxes).
- Bacchylides, *Odes* 3; 5; 17.
- Democritus, DK 68 B9; B118; B125.
- Diogenes Laertius, *Lives of the Philosophers* 8.69 (Empedocles and Etna); 10.13 (Epicurus on Leucippus).
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus (preserver of Sappho fr. 1; no passage given in the report).
- Empedocles, DK 31 B6; B17; B57; B89; B115; B117; B137.
- Heraclitus, DK 22 B1; B2; B12; B30; B40; B45; B53; B60; B61; B80; B90; B91 (via Plutarch).
- Herodotus, *Histories* 1.74 (Thales and the eclipse); 2.109; 7.228.
- Hippolytus, *Refutation of All Heresies* 1.14 (Xenophanes and sea-shells).
- Mimnermus, frr. 1; 2 West.
- Parmenides, DK 28 B2; B3; B8 (including 8.38–41); B14–15 (preserved by Simplicius).
- Pindar, *Olympian* 1; *Pythian* 1.
- Plato, *Cratylus* 402a.
- Plato, *Phaedo* 97b–98c.
- Plutarch, *Life of Pericles* 32 (Anaxagoras).
- Plutarch, the source of the "cannot step twice" version of Heraclitus (B91); work not named in the report.
- Pseudo-Longinus, *On the Sublime* 10 (quoting Sappho fr. 31).
- Sappho, frr. 1; 16; 31 Voigt. Edition: E.-M. Voigt, *Sappho et Alcaeus*.
- Simonides, fr. 543 PMG (Danae); fr. 11 West (Plataea elegy). Editions: Page, *PMG*; West, *Iambi et Elegi Graeci* (2nd edn, vol. 2).
- Simplicius (named as the source preserving Anaximander B1, Parmenides B2 and B8, and Zeno's plurality arguments); work not named in the report.
- Solon, frr. 4; 13; 20; 36 West.
- Theognis, *Theognidea* 19–38.
- Theophrastus, *Physical Opinions* (lost; named as the source of later compilers; no passage given).
- Tyrtaeus, frr. 10; 12 West.
- Xenophanes, frr. 1 and 2 West (the symposium; the wise man against the Olympic victor); DK 21 B7; B11; B15; B16; B23–26; B34.
- Zeno of Elea, DK 29 B1–2.

## Modern works
- Walter Burkert, *Lore and Science in Ancient Pythagoreanism*, trans. E. L. Minar, Jr. (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1972).
- Patricia Curd, *The Legacy of Parmenides: Eleatic Monism and Later Presocratic Thought* (Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1998). Identified from context: the report names "Patricia Curd's 'predicational monism'" without a title or year.
- André Lardinois, "Who Sang Sappho's Songs?", in Ellen Greene (ed.), *Reading Sappho: Contemporary Approaches* (Berkeley: University of California Press, 1996) (pages not verified). Identified from context: the report says Lardinois argued for a larger choral share than the "monodic Sappho" allows, with no title or year given.
- G. E. R. Lloyd, *Magic, Reason and Experience: Studies in the Origin and Development of Greek Science* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1979).
- Alain Martin and Oliver Primavesi, *L'Empédocle de Strasbourg (P. Strasb. gr. Inv. 1665–1666): introduction, édition et commentaire* (Berlin and New York: Walter de Gruyter; Strasbourg: Bibliothèque nationale et universitaire, 1999). Identified from context: the report gives "The Strasbourg papyrus of Empedocles (published 1999)".
- Oswyn Murray (ed.), *Sympotica: A Symposium on the Symposion* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1990).
- Bruno Snell, *Die Entdeckung des Geistes* (Hamburg: Claassen, 1946); English translation by T. G. Rosenmeyer, *The Discovery of the Mind: The Greek Origins of European Thought* (Oxford: Blackwell, 1953) (translation details not verified). The report gives the English title with the German date, 1946.
- Martin L. West, *Early Greek Philosophy and the Orient* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1971).
- Martin L. West, *Studies in Greek Elegy and Iambus* (Berlin and New York: Walter de Gruyter, 1974).

- Voigt, *Sappho et Alcaeus* (Amsterdam: Polak & Van Gennep, 1971), for the fragments numbered "Voigt" or cited without an editor's name (Sappho, Alcaeus).
