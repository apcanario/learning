# Greece 12.1: The Presocratics: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources

## Corpora and inscriptions
- Diels, H., and W. Kranz, *Die Fragmente der Vorsokratiker*, 6th ed. (Berlin: Weidmann, 1951–52) (the edition the report says it cites). Numbers cited: DK 12 B 1 (Anaximander); 13 B 1 (Anaximenes); 21 B 11, 14–16, 18, 23–26, 34, 38 (Xenophanes); 22 B 3, 9, 23, 31, 36, 51, 55, 59, 60, 61, 88, 94, 107, 111, 129 (Heraclitus); 28 B 6, 7, 8 (Parmenides); 29 B 1–2 (Zeno); 30 B 1–3, 6–8 (Melissus); 31 B 8, 13, 17, 61, 96, 109, 139 (Empedocles); 44 B 1–7, 6a, 13, 16, 17 (Philolaus; B 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 are argued from, the rest named as the genuine core); 59 B 3, 10, 11, 12, 17 (Anaxagoras); 64 B 2 (Diogenes of Apollonia); 67 B 2 (Leucippus); 68 B 9, 11, 117, 125, 156, 169–297 (esp. 191) (Democritus); 70 B 1 (Metrodorus of Chios). The report also refers to the A (testimonia) and C sections generally.
- Laks–Most numbering (P, D, R) mentioned as an alternative to DK; the report cites no numbers in it.
- Strasbourg Empedocles papyrus (P.Strasb. gr. inv. 1665–1666), ensemble d and the group overlapping B 17; ed. Martin and Primavesi (1999), above.
- Derveni papyrus, column IV (overlapping DK 22 B 3 and B 94). The report cites no edition; the standard text is in T. Kouremenos, G. Parássoglou and K. Tsantsanoglou (eds.), *The Derveni Papyrus* (Florence: Olschki, 2006) (not verified).

## Not resolved
- "Cornford" (named with Raven as building on Tannery's reading of Zeno): no work or year given by the report. *Plato and Parmenides* (1939) discusses Zeno, but I cannot confirm that it is the one meant.
- "Charles Kahn's reading" of Parmenidean "is" as veridical: no work or year given. Left unidentified to avoid guessing between his several studies.
- "Eduard Zeller" (held, with Mansfeld, that neither Heraclitus nor Parmenides knew the other): no work or year. Probably *Die Philosophie der Griechen*, but the edition and volume are not identifiable from the report.
- "Walter Burkert proposed Stesimbrotus of Thasos" and "Charles Kahn [proposed] Euthyphro" as authors of the Derveni papyrus: no works or years given. Candidates from memory: Burkert, *Zeitschrift für Papyrologie und Epigraphik* 62 (1986) 1–5; Kahn in Laks and Glenn Most (eds.), *Studies on the Derveni Papyrus* (Oxford, 1997) (neither verified).
- "Burkert and Zhmud", "Kirk and Popper" and "Owen and Curd" are named only as module 5.6 disputes; the report cites no work here.
- Aristotle, *Metaphysics* 1090a30–35 (objection that weightless numbers cannot build heavy bodies): the writer flagged this locator as the least sure.
- Locators the writer reported as from memory and unchecked: the Aristotle line ranges above (except as noted in the writer's list), Simplicius 144.25–28 and 24.26–25.1, Sextus 7.49 and 7.135–139, Pliny *NH* 2.149, and the *palintropos* / *palintonos* readings of B 51 in Hippolytus and Plutarch.
- The late first-century AD date of the Strasbourg roll rests on one source (Universalis) per the writer; not independently confirmed.
