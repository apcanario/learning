## Corpora and inscriptions
- *Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum* (CIL) VI 920 (arch of Claudius for the British conquest, with the restoration the report hedges); XIII 1668 (the Lyon tablet, Claudius' speech on the Gallic nobles).
- *Inscriptiones Graecae* (IG) VII 2713 (Nero's proclamation of freedom for Greece at the Isthmus).
- P.Lond. 1912 (Claudius' letter to the Alexandrians, AD 41); published as P.Lond. VI 1912 in H. I. Bell, *Jews and Christians in Egypt* (London, 1924) (not verified).
- The Senate's decree on Gnaeus Piso the father (10 December AD 20), described but not named by the report; standard edition W. Eck, A. Caballos and F. Fernández, *Das senatus consultum de Cn. Pisone patre* (Munich: C. H. Beck, 1996) (not verified; the report does not name an edition).
