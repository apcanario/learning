# Rome 5.1: The Gracchi: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Corpora and inscriptions
- Tabula Bembina, the extortion law on bronze (usually identified with the Acilian law of 123 or 122): *Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum* I² 583; M. H. Crawford (ed.), *Roman Statutes* (London: Institute of Classical Studies, 1996), no. 1 (numbers from memory; the report cites the inscription without a number).
- The Gracchan land commission's boundary stones from Campania, Lucania and Apulia, naming the IIIviri agris iudicandis adsignandis: the report gives no numbers. They are collected in *CIL* I² (nos. 639–642, from memory) and in *Inscriptiones Latinae liberae rei publicae* (ILLRP), ed. A. Degrassi (not verified).
- The road inscription from Polla in Lucania ("primus fecei ut de agro poplico aratoribus cederent paastores"): the report gives no number. Usually *CIL* I² 638 = *Inscriptiones Latinae Selectae* (ILS), ed. H. Dessau, 23 (from memory, not verified).
- The base inscribed *Cornelia Africani f. Gracchorum* (the report gives no number). Usually *CIL* VI 31610 = ILS 68 (from memory, not verified).

## Not resolved
- "Donald Earl read the veiling as the dress of sacrifice": no work or year in the report. The quality-pass review (finding 6) could not confirm the attribution and suggests Earl, *Tiberius Gracchus: A Study in Politics* (Brussels: Latomus, 1963), unchecked. Not listed above because the attribution is unverified.
- Livy's dating of a cap on holdings to 367 ("Livy dates such a cap to 367 (module 3.1)"): no passage given in this report (presumably *Ab urbe condita* 6.35; not verified).
- "Varro said he made the state two-headed, a source of civil discord (in Nonius)": no work, passage or Nonius reference. The quality-pass review gives the Latin as *bicipitem civitatem fecit, discordiarum civilium fontem*, probably from Varro's *On the Life of the Roman People*, from memory and unchecked.
- "Hans Ulrich Instinsky (Chiron, 1971)" and "Garnsey and Rathbone (Journal of Roman Studies, 1985)": the report gives no titles; completed above as noted.
- The inscription numbers under Corpora were not given by the report and rest on memory.
