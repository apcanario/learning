## Corpora and inscriptions
- None cited by number. The report mentions, without references, the bronze tablet of Veleia (the alimentary scheme), the tombstone of Tiberius Claudius Maximus found near Philippi, the inscriptions of Trajan's Column and of the column of Marcus (naming Adrastus), and Hadrian's speeches at Lambaesis (see Not resolved).

## Not resolved
- "Jérôme Carcopino cut to a tenth": a scholar is named for the reduction of Crito's figures, with no work or year.
- "Galen describes fever, diarrhoea and a dark pustular rash": no work or passage given.
- "Aelius Aristides" (the empire as one well-ordered city): no work or passage given.
- The bronze tablet of Veleia, the tombstone of Tiberius Claudius Maximus, the Column inscriptions and the Lambaesis speeches: described without corpus numbers.
- John Lydus, *On the Magistracies*: no book or chapter given by the report. The quality-pass review located it at 2.28 but did not check that section.
