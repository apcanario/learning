# Rome 0.1: How we know anything: the sources: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Appian, *Civil Wars* (surveyed for 133 BC onwards; "Civil Wars 133–35 BC"; no passage cited). Standard edition: Loeb.
- Augustine, *City of God* 6.3; 6.5. Standard edition: Corpus Christianorum Series Latina 47–48 (Dombart and Kalb); Loeb.
- Augustus, *Res Gestae* 1.1. Suetonius, *Augustus* 101.4 is cited for its setting up before the Mausoleum.
- Aulus Gellius, *Attic Nights* 2.28.6 (surveyed generally as quoting Cato, the annalists and the Twelve Tables). Standard edition: Loeb; Oxford Classical Texts (Marshall).
- Cassius Dio, *Roman History* (surveyed; surviving books 36–60, "69 BC to AD 46"; no passage cited). Standard edition: Loeb.
- Cato the Elder, *Origines* (fragments only; the report cites the work generally and through Nepos, Pliny and Gellius). Fragments in Cornell (ed.), *The Fragments of the Roman Historians* (below).
- Cicero, *Brutus* 41–43; 62; 75.
- Cicero, *On Divination* 1.107–108.
- Cicero, *On the Laws* 1.6; 2.59.
- Cicero, *On the Orator* 2.52.
- Cicero, *On the Republic* 1.25.
- The annalists and early historians, known only in fragments: Fabius Pictor, Cincius Alimentus, Cassius Hemina, Calpurnius Piso Frugi, Claudius Quadrigarius, Valerius Antias, Licinius Macer, Aelius Tubero, and the poets Naevius (*Punic War*) and Ennius (*Annales*). Fragments in Cornell (ed.), *The Fragments of the Roman Historians* (2013) (see Modern works).
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 1.3.4; 1.5.1; 1.6.2; 1.7.2; 1.7.3; 1.79.11; 1.89–90; 2.38–40. Standard edition: Loeb (Cary).
- Diodorus Siculus, *Library of History* (surveyed: Roman notices in books 11–20; no passage cited). Standard edition: Loeb.
- Festus, *On the Meaning of Words* (abridgement of Verrius Flaccus; with Paul the Deacon's epitome; surveyed, no passage cited). Standard edition: Lindsay (Teubner, 1913).
- Herodotus, *Histories* 3.153–158; 5.92. Standard edition: Oxford Classical Texts; Loeb.
- Livy, *History of Rome* Preface 6; 7; 10; 1.4.7; 1.13.5; 1.18.2; 1.19.3; 1.54.6; 2.48–50; 4.7.12; 4.20.8; 4.23.1–3; 6.1.2; 7.2; 7.6.1–6; 7.9.5; 8.40.4–5; 10.12; 21.38.3; 22.57.5; 23.11.1–6; 33.10. Standard edition: Oxford Classical Texts; Loeb.
- Macrobius, *Saturnalia* (book 1 on calendar and festivals; surveyed, no passage cited).
- Nepos, *Cato* 3.4 (the report relies on 3.3–4).
- Orosius, *Histories against the Pagans* 4.13.6.
- Ovid, *Metamorphoses* (books 14 and 15 cited generally); *Fasti* (surveyed as covering January to June). No passages given.
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 8.11; 17.244.
- Plutarch, *Camillus* 22.4; *Demosthenes* 2.2–4; *Romulus* 3.1; 8. The report also surveys the *Lives* of Numa, Publicola and Coriolanus and *Roman Questions* without passages. Standard edition: Loeb (Perrin).
- Polybius, *Histories* 1.1.5; 1.14.1–3; 3.8–9; 3.22; 3.22–26; 6.53–54; 39.1. Standard edition: Loeb (Paton, rev. Walbank and Habicht).
- Propertius book 4 (named in the closing list of Roman myth sources; no passage cited).
- Servius, commentary on Virgil, *Aeneid* 1.273; 1.373.
- Suetonius, *On Grammarians* 17; *Augustus* 101.4; *Lives of the Caesars* (surveyed generally). Standard edition: Loeb.
- Tacitus, *Annals* 11.24; *Histories* and *Annals* (surveyed generally). Standard edition: Oxford Classical Texts; Loeb.
- Varro, *Antiquities Human and Divine* (lost; known through Augustine, *City of God* 6.3 and 6.5).
- Varro, *On the Latin Language* 5.148–150 (books 5–10 surveyed generally). Standard edition: Loeb (Kent).
- Velleius Paterculus, *Roman History* (surveyed; no passage cited).
- Verrius Flaccus, *On the Meaning of Words* and his calendar at Praeneste (the *Fasti Praenestini*); surveyed through Suetonius, *On Grammarians* 17 and Festus.
- Virgil, *Aeneid* (cited with Servius at 1.273, 1.373; surveyed as a source of Roman myth). 
- *Historia Augusta* (surveyed; no passage cited). Standard edition: Loeb (Magie).
- Ammianus Marcellinus, *Res Gestae* (surveyed: books 14–31 survive; no passage cited). Standard edition: Loeb (Rolfe).
