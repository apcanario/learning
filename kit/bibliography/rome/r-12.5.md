# Rome 12.5: Book 6: the underworld and the parade of heroes: sources

Curated 1 November 2026 by the writer (Opus 5.5). Editions are those relied on where known; passages are those the report cites. English renderings in the report are the writer's own.

- *Little Iliad* (Epic Cycle), Deiphobus' marriage to Helen: through Proclus' summary, no fragment number.
- Bacchylides 5 (Heracles and the shades like leaves). Edition: Maehler.

- Lucretius, *On the Nature of Things* book 3 (Acheron as the torment of the living): named through Greek module 8.4, no line.
- Horace, *Carmen Saeculare* 51–52. Edition: Oxford Classical Texts (Wickham and Garrod).
- Propertius 4.11.39 (Achilles as Perseus' ancestor). Edition: Oxford Classical Texts (Heyworth).
- Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 14 (the Sibyl's story) and 15 (Pythagoras' speech). Edition: Oxford Classical Texts (Tarrant).
- Lucan, *Civil War* 6 (Erictho): through module 10.2, no line.
- Silius Italicus, *Punica* 13 (Scipio's visit to the dead). Edition: Loeb (Duff).
- Ennius, *Annales*, the line on Fabius Maximus: through module 12.1.
- Livy 1.3.6 and Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 1.70 (Silvius' parentage): through module 1.3.
- Servius, *Commentary on the Aeneid*: on 6.136 (the bough and the grove at Aricia), on 6.211 (against reading *cunctantem* as a bad sign), on 6.609 (the Twelve Tables' curse on the patron who defrauds a client), on 6.893 (the false gate; horn as eyes, ivory as mouth). Edition: Thilo–Hagen.
- Twelve Tables, *patronus si clienti fraudem fecerit, sacer esto*: through Servius on 6.609.
- Augustine, *City of God* 14.5 (6.719–721); 21.13 (the purgations). Edition: Loeb; checked in the Dods translation.
- Paul's rapture (2 Corinthians 12), as Dante's second precedent: named, no passage.
- Dante, *Inferno* 2.13–15, 20–24, 32; 3.91–93, 109, 112–117, 124–126; 4.106–111, 122; 5.4–12, 61–62; 6.25–27; 8.13–24; 9.37–48; 12 (the Centaurs); 13.10; 17 (Geryon); 31.98. Edition: Petrocchi.
