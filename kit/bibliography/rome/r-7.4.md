## Corpora and inscriptions
- P.Giss. 40 col. I (the Giessen papyrus usually taken as a copy of the edict of 212); in O. Eger, E. Kornemann and P. M. Meyer, *Griechische Papyri im Museum des Oberhessischen Geschichtsvereins zu Giessen* (Leipzig, 1910–12) (not verified).
- The report also refers, without numbers, to the Severan arch in the Forum (203) and to the Severan tondo in Berlin (see Not resolved).

## Not resolved
- "Olympiodorus": the fifth-century historian is named for the count of 1,600 marble seats in Caracalla's baths; no passage or fragment number is given.
- The inscription of the arch of Septimius Severus, whose fourth line was recut: described, but no CIL number is given by the report.
- "The Law of Citations of 426": cited without a source reference.
- The Vatican manuscript that preserves Dio's own text for 217–219: named without a shelf mark.
