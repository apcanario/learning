# Rome 8.2: Diocletian and the Tetrarchy: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Ammianus Marcellinus, *Res gestae* 14.11.10 (Galerius walking before Diocletian's carriage).
- Aurelius Victor, *Caesares* 39 (cited as a whole for Aper's death, the Margus and the prefect kept in office); 39.1; 39.2–4; 39.29; 39.31. The writer's note records that the section numbers were given from memory.
- *Collatio Mosaicarum et Romanarum legum* 6.4 (incest edict of 295); 15.3 (rescript on the Manichaeans).
- *Epitome de Caesaribus* 38.8; 39 (as a whole); 39.6; 40.10; 40.16. Section numbers and the wording of 40.10 from memory, per the writer's note.
- Eusebius, *Church History* 8.1.7–8; 8.2.4; 8.2.5; 8.4; 8.6.8–10; 8.17. Standard edition: Loeb (Lake and Oulton). Also *Life of Constantine* 2.50–51 and *Martyrs of Palestine* 3.1.
- Eutropius, *Breviarium* 9.19; 9.19–28 (as a whole); 9.20; 9.21–22; 9.24; 9.26 (quoting the Latin "regiae consuetudinis formam ..."); 9.27; 9.28.
- *Gesta apud Zenophilum* (minutes of the search at Cirta, 19 May 303, preserved in the record of the hearing of 320), cited without a section.
- *Acts of the Abitinian Martyrs* (cited without a section; source of "sine dominico non possumus").
- *Historia Augusta*, *Carus* 14–15 (the druidess and the boar). Chapter numbers from memory, per the writer's note.
- Jerome, *On Illustrious Men* 80.
- John the Lydian, *On the Months* 1.27. The army figure of 389,704 is confirmed in secondary sources; the fleet figure and section number are from memory (writer's note).
- Lactantius, *On the Deaths of the Persecutors* 7.2; 7.3; 7.3–4; 7.4; 7.6–7; 7.6–8; 10; 10–11; 11; 12; 13.1; 13.2–3; 15.7; 16.1; 16.4; 17.1–3; 17–18; 19; 26; 33; 34–35; 42. Edition with translation and commentary: J. L. Creed (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1984).
- *Panegyrici Latini* 10(2) (of 289); 11(3).11 (of 291); 7(6) (of 307). The numbering in the form Arabic(Roman) is that of the standard editions of the twelve Latin panegyrics. Section numbers for 11(3) and 7(6) from memory (writer's note).
- Pliny the Younger, *Letters* book 10 (addressing Trajan as *domine*).
- Suetonius, *Domitian* 13.2.
- The *Syro-Roman Law Book* (named for its equivalences of one *iugum*), cited without a passage.

## Modern works
- Adam, Robert, *Ruins of the Palace of the Emperor Diocletian at Spalatro in Dalmatia* (London, 1764).
- Alföldi, Andreas, "Die Ausgestaltung des monarchischen Zeremoniells am römischen Kaiserhofe", *Mitteilungen des Deutschen Archäologischen Instituts, Römische Abteilung* 49 (1934) 1–118, and "Insignien und Tracht der römischen Kaiser", the same journal 50 (1935) 1–171 (identified from the report's "studies of imperial ceremonial (1934–1935)"; titles, volumes and pages from memory, not verified).
- Bleicken, Jochen, *Prinzipat und Dominat: Gedanken zur Periodisierung der römischen Kaiserzeit* (Wiesbaden: Steiner, 1978) (subtitle, place and series [*Frankfurter Historische Vorträge*] from memory, not verified). The writer's note says Bleicken's argument was taken from an encyclopaedia summary.
- Corcoran, Simon, *The Empire of the Tetrarchs: Imperial Pronouncements and Government AD 284–324* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1996; revised edition 2000).
- Gibbon, Edward, *The History of the Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire* (London: Strahan and Cadell, 1776–1788), chapter 16 (on the martyrs of Palestine and the number of executions).
- Kolb, Frank, *Herrscherideologie in der Spätantike* (Berlin: Akademie Verlag, 2001) (publisher not verified).
- Ste. Croix, G. E. M. de, "Aspects of the 'Great' Persecution", *Harvard Theological Review* 47 (1954) 75–113.
- Zuckerman, Constantin, "Sur la liste de Vérone et la province de Grande Arménie, la division de l'empire et la date de création des diocèses", *Travaux et Mémoires* 14 (2002) 617–637 (title and pages from memory, not verified; the report gives only the journal and year).

## Corpora and inscriptions
- *Edictum de pretiis rerum venalium* (the Edict on Maximum Prices, 301), cited as a whole. Standard edition: M. Giacchero, *Edictum Diocletiani et collegarum de pretiis rerum venalium* (Genoa, 1974) (not verified).
- *P.Cair.Isid.* 1 (edict of Aristius Optatus, prefect of Egypt, 297), in *The Archive of Aurelius Isidorus in the Egyptian Museum, Cairo* (not verified).
- The Verona List (*Laterculus Veronensis*, about 314), cited without an edition; printed in O. Seeck's *Notitia Dignitatum* (Berlin, 1876) (not verified).
- The Treasury of St Mark's porphyry Tetrarchs (Venice), cited as a monument only.

## Not resolved
- "William Seston dated them to 297, Timothy Barnes to 293" (the dioceses): no works or years are given in the report.
- "Theodor Mommsen made the change a divide, setting the Principate ... against the Dominate": no work named.
- "most scholars, Corcoran and Samuel Lieu among them, prefer 302; others have argued for 297": Lieu's work is not named, and Corcoran's is presumably the 1996 book above, which the report does not tie to this point.
- "Andreas Alföldi's studies of imperial ceremonial (1934–1935)": no titles given; the two papers above are identified from memory.
- "collected by Gregorius about 292 and Hermogenianus about 295": the two codes are named with no edition or passage.
