## Corpora and inscriptions

- *Lex Irnitana* (the municipal law of Irni, chapters 21, 84 and 85 cited); standard edition J. González and M. Crawford, "The Lex Irnitana: a new copy of the Flavian municipal law", *Journal of Roman Studies* 76 (1986) 147–243 (not verified; the report names no edition).
- *Tabulae Vindolandenses* (Tab. Vindol.) II 154; 164; 343; 344, ed. A. K. Bowman and J. D. Thomas, *The Vindolanda Writing-Tablets (Tabulae Vindolandenses II)* (London: British Museum Press, 1994) (not verified).
- P.Yadin 16 (Babatha's census declaration, AD 127), in N. Lewis (ed.), *The Documents from the Bar Kokhba Period in the Cave of Letters: Greek Papyri* (Jerusalem: Israel Exploration Society, 1989) (not verified).
