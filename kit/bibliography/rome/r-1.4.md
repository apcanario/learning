# Rome 1.4: Romulus and Remus: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 1.32.3–5; 1.72–73; 1.76–90; 1.77.1; 1.78–79; 1.79.4; 1.79.6–8; 1.79.8; 1.79.11; 1.79–83; 1.84 and 1.84.4; 1.85–87; 2.1–56; 2.38–40 (Calpurnius Piso on Tarpeia); 2.56; 4.2. Loeb (Cary).

## Corpora and inscriptions
- Michael H. Crawford, *Roman Republican Coinage* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1974): RRC 20/1 (the ROMANO didrachm); RRC 235/1 (the denarius of Sextus Pompeius Fostlus).

## Not resolved
- "Adriano La Regina", "Fausto Zevi", "Henner von Hesberg", "Irene Iacopi", "Filippo Coarelli", "Adam Ziółkowski", "Winckelmann" (judging the wolf Etruscan): named without any work or year.
- "Mary Beard" (objecting to Mommsen's consuls and to Wiseman's lost plays): no work cited.
- The radiocarbon and thermoluminescence dating of the Capitoline wolf at the University of Salento: the report names no author or year. Work by Calcagnile and others (2019) and Martini and Galli (2021) appears in a Wikipedia bibliography, but the match to the report is not confirmed.
- "Antonio del Pollaiuolo" (attribution of the twins, a passing remark) and the Bolsena mirror, Capitoline wolf and Ogulnii group as objects: no scholarly edition cited.
