# Rome 8.1: The third-century crisis: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aurelius Victor, *Caesares* 33.8; 33.34.
- Cyprian, *Letters* 80. Also *On the Lapsed* (named, no passage) and *On Mortality* 14; *To Demetrianus* 3.
- Dexippus of Athens, *Chronicle* and *Scythica* (named, no passage; known from fragments and the Vienna palimpsest). Collected fragments: *FGrH* 100 (Jacoby). The new Vienna pages are in Martin and Grusková (see Modern works).
- Dionysius of Alexandria, known through Eusebius, *Church History* 6.41; 7.21–22 (see Eusebius).
- *Epitome de Caesaribus* (named, no passage).
- Eusebius, *Church History* 6.39; 6.41; 7.13; 7.21–22. Standard edition: Loeb (Lake and Oulton).
- Eutropius, *Breviarium* 9.2; 9.3; 9.4; 9.7; 9.8; 9.13; 9.14; 9.15.
- Herodian, *History of the Empire* 7.1.1–2; 7.3; 7.4–5; 7.9; 7.10; 8.5; 8.8. Standard edition: Loeb (Whittaker).
- *Historia Augusta*: *Maximini duo* 12; *Gallienus* 5.5; 13.8; *Tyranni triginta* 30.24–27; *Aurelian* 25; 27; 38.2; 39.2. The writer's note records that all of these locators were given from memory.
- Jordanes, *Getica* 101–103. Standard edition: Mommsen, MGH *Auctores antiquissimi* 5.1.
- Lactantius, *On the Deaths of the Persecutors* 4; 5. Edition with translation and commentary: J. L. Creed (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1984).
- Malalas and Zonaras (named as Byzantine chroniclers on Gordian III's death, no passage).
- Porphyry, *Life of Plotinus* 12.
- *Sibylline Oracles*, book 13 (named among the contemporary controls, no passage).
- Zosimus, *New History* 1.18–19; 1.21–22; 1.23; 1.24; 1.36; 1.38; 1.39; 1.40; 1.42; 1.44; 1.49; 1.50–55; 1.56; 1.59; 1.60–61; 1.61; 1.62. Standard edition: F. Paschoud (Budé, 1971–1989). The writer's note says the chapter numbers follow the divisions used on livius.org; 1.46, 1.50 and 1.60–62 were not checked individually.

## Corpora and inscriptions
- *P.Oxy.* 12.1411 (*The Oxyrhynchus Papyri*, vol. 12, ed. B. P. Grenfell and A. S. Hunt, 1916): the strategos's order of 260 to the bankers.
- Shapur I's trilingual inscription on the Ka'ba-ye Zardosht at Naqsh-e Rustam, the *Res Gestae Divi Saporis*. The report gives no edition; the standard one is P. Huyse, *Die dreisprachige Inschrift Šābuhrs I. an der Ka'ba-i Zardušt (ŠKZ)*, Corpus Inscriptionum Iranicarum III.1.1 (London, 1999) (not verified).
- The Augsburg altar of 260 (found 1992), cited without a publication reference. Usually quoted as *L'Année épigraphique* 1993, 1231 (not verified).
- The Egyptian libelli of 250 (some four dozen), cited as a group without an edition or numbers.
