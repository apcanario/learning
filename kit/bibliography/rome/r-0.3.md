# Rome 0.3: The timeline spine, 1000 BC to AD 476: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Appian, *Civil Wars* (cited as starting the Civil Wars narrative at 133 BC; no passage). Standard edition: Loeb.
- Atticus, *Liber annalis* (lost; used Varro's foundation date in 47 BC; known only as the report gives it).
- Augustus, *Res Gestae divi Augusti* 4; 34.
- Bede, *Ecclesiastical History of the English People* (731; cited for the spread of Anno Domini; no passage). Standard edition: Oxford Medieval Texts (Colgrave and Mynors).
- Cassius Dio, *Roman History* 53.17. Standard edition: Loeb.
- Censorinus, *De die natali* 21.
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 1.74 (including 1.74.1–4); 4.6–7. Standard edition: Loeb (Cary).
- Ennius, *Annales* (quoted in Varro, *On Agriculture* 3.1.2; "speaker and date uncertain"). Fragments: Skutsch (ed.), *The Annals of Q. Ennius* (Oxford, 1985) is the standard edition.
- Livy, *History of Rome* 2.1.1; 2.8.6–8; 3.33.1; 6.1.2; 6.35.10; 7.3.5–7. Standard edition: Oxford Classical Texts; Loeb.
- Luke 3:1.
- Malchus (cited for the message of Odoacer's senate to Zeno; fragment, no reference given).
- Marcellinus Comes, *Chronicle*, s.a. 476. Standard edition: Mommsen, *Chronica Minora* II (MGH AA 11, 1894).
- Justinian, *Novel* 47.
- Orosius, *Histories against the Pagans* (cited as building a history on "from the founding"; no passage).
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 33.19. Standard edition: Loeb.
- Plutarch, *Camillus* 22.4; *Romulus* 12; *Tiberius Gracchus* 20.1. Standard edition: Loeb (Perrin).
- Polybius, *Histories* 1.5.1; 1.6.1–2; 3.22.1–2. Standard edition: Loeb (Paton, rev. Walbank and Habicht).
- Sallust, *Catiline* 10. Standard edition: Loeb (Rolfe, rev. Ramsey).
- Tacitus, *Annals* 1.1; 1.3; *Histories* 3.72. Standard edition: Oxford Classical Texts; Loeb.
- Timaeus of Tauromenium, Fabius Pictor, Cincius Alimentus and Cato the Elder (*Origines*): fragments, cited through Dionysius, *Roman Antiquities* 1.74.
- Varro, *On Agriculture* 3.1.2. Standard edition: Loeb (Hooper and Ash).
- Velleius Paterculus, *Roman History* 2.1. Standard edition: Loeb (Shipley).
- Dionysius Exiguus (Anno Domini, 525): cited by name only.
