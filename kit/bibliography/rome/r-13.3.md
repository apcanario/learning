# Rome 13.3: The rival schools at Rome: sources

Written 1 November 2026 (Opus 5.5) with the report. Editions are the standard ones (Oxford Classical Texts, Teubner, Loeb) unless named; "(not verified)" marks a reference given from memory that could not be confirmed in this session.

## Ancient sources
- Ammianus Marcellinus 29.1.39 (Peregrinus among those who chose death by fire).
- Asconius, commentary on Cicero's *Against Piso*, on section 68 (identifies Philodemus; checked against the quotation in the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Philodemus").
- Athenagoras, *Embassy for the Christians* 26 (oracular statue of Peregrinus at Parium).
- Augustine, *City of God* 19.1–3 (Varro's 288 schools and the Old Academy).
- Cicero, *Academica* 2.8; 2.11–12; 2.119.
- Cicero, *Against Piso* 37 (not verified: the section of "Epicure noster, ex hara producte, non ex schola"); 68–72.
- Cicero, *Against Vatinius* 14.
- Cicero, *Brutus* 315.
- Cicero, *Letters to Atticus* 13.12–19 (the recasting of the *Academica*).
- Cicero, *Letters to Friends* 4.13 (to Nigidius); 7.12 (to Trebatius); 15.16.1 (Catius' spectres); 15.19.2–3 (Cassius to Cicero, January 45; checked against the University of Vermont commentary); 16.21.3 (Marcus Cicero to Tiro on Cratippus).
- Cicero, *On Duties* 1.1; 2.7–8; 3.5–6; 3.20.
- Cicero, *On Ends* 1.8; 2.119; 5.3.
- Cicero, *On the Laws* 1.21; 1.39.
- Cicero, *On the Nature of the Gods* 1.10; 3.95.
- Cicero, *Timaeus* 1 (Nigidius).
- Cicero, *Tusculan Disputations* 2.9.
- Claudianus Mamertus, *On the State of the Soul* 2.8 (the Sextii on the incorporeal soul).
- Dio 72(71).31.3 (Loeb numbering, Boissevain's in brackets; the endowment of 176, as cited in Greek module 7.4).
- Gellius, *Attic Nights* 4.9.1 (Nigidius' learning next to Varro's; not verified); 12.11 (Peregrinus).
- Horace, *Epistles* 1.4.16; *Satires* 1.5.101.
- Jerome, *Chronicle* (Nigidius' death in exile, 45; Anaxilaus expelled, 28; cited by entry, without page).
- Juvenal 13.121–122.
- Livy 1.18.
- Lucan 1.639–672.
- Lucian, *Alexander* 25, 38, 47 (and its dedication to Celsus); *The Eunuch* 3 (the salary and the Peripatetic vacancy; checked in the Perseus translation, which has no section numbers, so the section is not verified); *On the Death of Peregrinus* 10–20, 35–36, 39–40 (checked in the Loeb translation on Perseus by page; section numbers from the Wikipedia summary).
- *Palatine Anthology* 11.44 (Philodemus' invitation to Piso).

- Philostratus, *Lives of the Sophists* 566.
