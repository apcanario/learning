# Rome 1.3: Before Romulus: Evander, Hercules and Aeneas: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aristotle, on the Achaeans blown off course to "Latinion", reported in Dionysius 1.72.3–4.
- Cato the Elder, *Origines* (the Latin geography of Aeneas, Latinus, Turnus and Mezentius, as reported by Servius; the origin of the Vinalia, in Macrobius, *Saturnalia* 3.5.10; the Aborigines as Greeks, in Dionysius 1.11.1; Rome 432 years after Troy, in Dionysius 1.74.2).
- Cicero, *On Divination* 1.43 (Fabius Pictor's dream); *Verrines* 2.4.72 (Segesta).
- Diodorus Siculus 4.21.2 (Cacius and Pinarius).
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 1.11.1; 1.31.1; 1.31.2; 1.32–33; 1.39–40; 1.41–42; 1.43; 1.45–71; 1.46–48; 1.48.3; 1.61–62; 1.64–65; 1.64.4–5; 1.67.4; 1.70–71; 1.70.3–4; 1.71.3; 1.72–73; 1.72.2; 1.72.3–4; 1.72.5; 1.74.1; 1.74.2; 1.89.1. This is the source of the Greek fragments the report quotes second-hand: Hellanicus, Damastes, Aristotle, Callias, Timaeus, Xenagoras and Menecrates of Xanthus. Loeb (Cary).
- Ennius, *Euhemerus* (Saturn as a deified mortal) and, with Naevius, the descent of Romulus from Aeneas through a daughter (reported by Servius on *Aeneid* 1.273).
- The Epic Cycle, *Sack of Ilion*, as summarised by Proclus (Aeneas withdraws to Ida).
- Heraclides Ponticus, reported in Plutarch, *Camillus* 22.2.
- Hesiod, *Theogony* 1011–1016 (with line 1014 bracketed by editors); *Works and Days* 109–126.
- Homer, *Iliad* 20.307–308.
- *Homeric Hymn to Aphrodite* 196–199.
- Hyginus, *Fabulae* 127.
- Justin 28.1–2; 43.1; 43.1.3.
- Livy 1.1–3; 1.1.6–7; 1.2.6; 1.3.2; 1.3.3–4; 1.3.4; 1.3.6; 1.3.6–10; 1.3.8–9; 1.5.1; 1.5.1–2; 1.7.3; 1.7.4–11; 1.7.8; 1.7.12–14; 1.49.9; 8.11.15; 9.29.9–11; 22.9–10; 23.31; 29.12.14; 37.37.1–3; 38.39.10.
- Macrobius, *Saturnalia* 1.7.19–24; 3.4.11; 3.5.10.
- Ovid, *Fasti* 1.90; 1.229–240; 1.238; 4.39–56; *Metamorphoses* 14 (Picus; no line numbers in the report).
- Pausanias 1.12.1.
- Plutarch, *Camillus* 22.2; *Flamininus* 12; *Romulus* 1–2 and 2.1; *Roman Questions* 18 and 60. Loeb (Perrin for the Lives).
- Polybius 12.4b–c (Timaeus and the October Horse).
- Propertius 4.9.
- Servius on Virgil, *Aeneid* 1.273 and the Cato material noted above.
- Strabo 13.1.53.
- Suetonius, *Claudius* 25.3; *Julius* 6.1.
- Tacitus, *Annals* 11.24.
- Thucydides 6.2.3.
- Varro, *On the Latin Language* 5.42; 5.64; 5.144; *On Agriculture* 2.4.18; *De familiis Troianis* (lost; named only).
- Virgil, *Aeneid* 7.45–49; 8.185–275.
- Zonaras 8.9.
- The Tauromenium library catalogue (an inscription summarising Fabius Pictor) and the *Tabula Iliaca Capitolina* ("after Stesichorus") are cited as documents; see below.

## Not resolved
- "M. L. West" (dating of the catalogue containing *Theogony* 1011–1016): named without any work or year. His edition of the *Theogony* and his study of the *Catalogue of Women* are the likely sources, but the report does not say which.
- "Martinelli and Paolucci (2006)" on the Veii statuettes of Aeneas and Anchises: no title; not identified.
- "Paolo Sommella" (excavations at Lavinium from 1968): named without any work or year.
- "Dionysius" on Hellanicus, Damastes, Callias, Xenagoras, Menecrates and Timaeus: the Greek authors are known only through the intermediaries cited above.
