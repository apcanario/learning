# Rome 13.7: Marcus Aurelius: a life: sources

Curated 1 November 2026 (Opus 5.5, the writer) from the report's inline citations. Dio is cited by the Loeb book numbers with Boissevain's in brackets; the Dio and *Historia Augusta* passages were checked against the Loeb translations (Cary; Magie) as given on LacusCurtius, and Eusebius 5.1 and 5.5 against the NPNF translation (McGiffert). "(not verified)" marks a reference given from memory.

## Ancient sources
- Acts of Justin (the trial of Justin Martyr before Junius Rusticus as prefect of the city); cited by title only.
- Apollinaris of Hierapolis, lost work, as reported by Eusebius, *Church History* 5.5.4 (the Thundering Legion).
- Athenagoras, *Embassy for the Christians* (address to Marcus and Commodus); cited by title only.
- Cassius Dio, *Roman History* (Loeb, tr. E. Cary, vol. 9), epitome of Xiphilinus: 71.1 (Lucilla married to Lucius); 71.1.2 (Marcus hears Sextus the Boeotian as emperor); 72(71).6 (frailty, eating at night, theriac); 72(71).8–10 (rain miracle, Arnuphis, Hermes; 9 is Xiphilinus' Christian insertion); 72(71).17 (terms with the Iazyges); 72(71).22 (Commodus summoned and given the toga of manhood; 22.3 Faustina and Cassius); 72(71).22–23 (Cassius from Cyrrhus; the false report and proclamation); 72(71).24–26 (speech to the soldiers); 72(71).27 (the centurion Antonius; three months and six days); 72(71).28 (the head); 72(71).28–30 (papers destroyed, pardons, no senator killed); 72(71).29 (Faustina's death, gout or suicide); 72(71).31 (honours for Faustina); 72(71).33 (Crispina; the spear from the temple of Bellona; death on 17 March and the physicians); 72(71).34 (watchword "go to the rising sun"; age and length of reign; tolerance of his wife's offences); 72(71).35 (teachers: Fronto, Herodes, Rusticus, Apollonius); 72(71).36.1 (ivory shoulders); 72(71).36.3–4 (verdict; gold to iron and rust).
- Claudian, *On the Sixth Consulship of Honorius* (the rain miracle; lines not given) (not verified).
- Eusebius, *Church History* (NPNF tr. A. C. McGiffert): 4.26.5–6 (Melito's petition); 5, preface 1 (the seventeenth year of the emperor); 5.1–2 (the letter of the churches of Vienne and Lyon): 5.1.9–10 (Vettius Epagathus), 5.1.20 (Sanctus), 5.1.25–26 (Biblis), 5.1.29–31 (Pothinus), 5.1.44 (Attalus), 5.1.47 (the emperor's reply; the festival), 5.1.49–51 (Alexander), 5.1.53–56 (Ponticus, Blandina), 5.1.62 (the Rhône); 5.5.1–6 (the Melitene legion; Apollinaris; Tertullian).
- Eusebius, *Chronicle* (the persecution in the seventh year of Marcus, 167); cited by title only.
- Fronto, *Ad M. Caesarem* 4.13 (Marcus' letter on reading Aristo, at twenty-five); the correspondence in general (ed. M. P. J. van den Hout, Teubner, 1988; tr. C. R. Haines, Loeb, 1919–20).
- Galen, *On Prognosis* (Galen excused from the northern campaign to attend Commodus); cited by title only (not verified for the passage).
- Herodian, *History of the Empire after Marcus*, book 1: 1.3–4 (Marcus' last illness and deathbed speech).
- *Historia Augusta* (Loeb, tr. D. Magie, vol. 1): *Marcus* 1.1–2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, 1.9–10, 2.2–3.6, 2.6, 3.2, 3.3–5, 4.1, 4.2, 4.5, 5.1, 5.2, 6.2, 6.3–4, 6.6, 7.2, 7.5, 9.4–5, 14.8, 18.3–6, 19.1–9, 23.7, 24.4, 24.6, 24.9, 25.3, 25.6, 26.4, 26.9, 27.1, 27.5, 28.3–4, 28.4–6, 28.10; *Commodus* 1.2 (birth at Lanuvium with his twin), 12.4–5 (imperator, triumph of 23 December 176); *Verus* 10–11 (poisoning rumours) (not verified for the chapters); *Avidius Cassius* (the letters of Faustina), cited by title only.
- Justin Martyr, *First Apology* 1 (address to Antoninus and Verissimus); the forged letter of Marcus appended to the *First Apology*.
- Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* 1.6 (Diognetus); 1.7 (Rusticus, Epictetus' notes); 1.16 (Antoninus); 1.17 (the gods: his brother, his wife); 11.3 (the Christians' obstinacy).
- Melito of Sardis, petition to Marcus, as quoted by Eusebius, *Church History* 4.26.5–6.
- Tertullian, *Apology* 5 (letters of Marcus; Marcus as protector of the Christians).
