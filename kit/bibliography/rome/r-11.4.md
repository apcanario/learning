# Rome 11.4: Women and the late Republic: sources

Curated 1 November 2026 by the writer (Opus 5.5). "(not verified)" marks a reference given from memory and not checked during writing.

## Ancient sources
- Apuleius, *Apology* 10 (Lesbia's real name Clodia), via module 5.6.
- Appian, *Civil Wars* 4.32–34 (Hortensia; 1,400 women cut to 400; levy on men worth over 100,000 drachmas), checked against the Perseus translation (H. White); 5.19 (Manius and Fulvia) (not verified).
- Asconius, commentary on Cicero's *Pro Milone* (Fulvia and her mother at Clodius' funeral and at the trial), via the RE article on Fulvia.
- Catullus, poems 58, 77, 79, 83 (Lesbia, Caelius, Rufus, Lesbius est pulcher, Lesbia's husband).
- Cassius Dio 47.8.4 (Fulvia and Cicero's tongue) (Loeb numbering, not verified); 48.4–10 (Perusine war; Fulvia armed at Praeneste); 48.44 (Livia's marriage, the pontiffs, the three-month joke); 49.38.1 (sacrosanctity and statues for Octavia and Livia, 35 BC).
- Cicero, *Letters to Atticus* 2.9.1 (Clodia ox-eyed); book 12 (Clodia's gardens, 45 BC); 15.11 (council at Antium, 8 June 44), checked against an English translation.
- Cicero, *Letters to Brutus* 1.18 (council at Servilia's house, July 43), checked against an English translation.
- Cicero, *On the Orator* 2.44 (Catulus' speech for his mother Popilia).
- Cicero, *Pro Caelio* 18, 33–34, 36, 59–60, 62.
- Cicero, *Philippics* 3.4, 5.22, 13.18 (centurions killed at Brundisium before Antony's wife); 5.22 checked in Yonge's translation, 3.4 and 13.18 via the RE article on Fulvia.
- Cicero, *Brutus* 211 (Cornelia), via module 5.1.
- Cornelius Nepos, fragments (Cornelia's letter to Gaius), via module 5.1.
- Martial 11.20 (epigram attributed to Octavian on Fulvia and Glaphyra).
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 7.158 (Terentia's age) (not verified); 34.31 (Cornelia's seated statue, the portico of Metellus, the works of Octavia).
- Plutarch, *Antony* 10, 30, 31, 35, 53, 54, 57, 87.
- Plutarch, *Brutus* 5; *Cato Minor* 24 (Servilia's note to Caesar; Brutus thought Caesar's son).
- Plutarch, *Cicero* 20 (Terentia's character; the Vestals' sign) (not verified), 29 (Terentia's jealousy of Clodia; quadrantaria), 41 (the divorce).
- Plutarch, *Gaius Gracchus* 4.2–3 (Octavius spared; the bronze statue inscribed "Cornelia, mother of the Gracchi"), 19 (Cornelia at Misenum).
- Quintilian, *Institutio oratoria* 1.1.6 (Cornelia's letters; Hortensia's speech still read).
- Sallust, *Catiline* 23.3–4, 24.3–4, 25.1–5, 26.3, 28.2, 40.5.
- Suetonius, *Augustus* 62.1–2 (Claudia; Livia taken while pregnant); *Julius* 50.2 (Servilia, the pearl, the auctions, Cicero's pun); *Tiberius* 6 (Livia's flight).
- Tacitus, *Annals* 5.1 (Livia's marriage).
- Valerius Maximus 4.4 preface (Cornelia's jewels, from Pomponius Rufus) (not verified); 8.3.1–3 (Maesia, Afrania, Hortensia).
- Velleius Paterculus 2.71 (Livius Drusus Claudianus' suicide), 2.74.3 (Fulvia), 2.75 (Livia's flight).

## Inscriptions
- CIL VI 10043 = 31610; ILS 68: base of Cornelia's statue, *Cornelia Africani f. Gracchorum* (Capitoline Museums).
- CIL XI 6721: sling bullets from Perusia (texts on Fulvia, Lucius Antonius and Octavian), checked against a modern list that also gives the ILLRP numbers.

## Coins
- *Roman Provincial Coinage* I, 3140: Eumeneia as Fulvia, bronze with a winged female head, c. 41–40 BC.
