Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Not resolved

- "the list of consuls for 23 names a Varro Murena as Augustus' colleague": the consular *Fasti* are not identified (no edition or number given).
- "a papyrus of Augustus' funeral speech for Agrippa": no publication given (presumably the Cologne papyrus; not verified).
- "the copy from Pisidian Antioch": no edition or article given for the discovery of the Antioch fragments.
