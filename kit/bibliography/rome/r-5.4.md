# Rome 5.4: Caesar: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Appian, *Civil Wars* 2.30; 2.110. Standard edition: Loeb (White).
- Appian, *Gallic History* 1 (the fragmentary Celtic book).
- [Caesar's officers], *Spanish War* 31. The *Alexandrian War* and *African War* are named as continuations of the *Civil War* but cited by no passage.
- Caesar, *Civil War* 1.5; 1.7.8; 1.8; 1.9; 1.16–23; 1.22; 1.37–87; 2.23–44; 3.48; 3.88–99 (3.88–89, 3.99).
- Caesar, *Gallic War* 1.29; 2.28; 2.33; 2.35; 3.16; 4.15; 4.17–19; 4.38; 5.24–37; 7.28; 7.68–89; 7.76; 7.90; 8.44 (book 8 by Aulus Hirtius). Standard edition: Oxford Classical Texts (du Pontet).
- Caesar, *On Analogy* and *Anticato*: lost; named only (via Suetonius, *Julius* 56.5; Plutarch, *Caesar* 54).
- Cassius Dio, *Roman History* 38.6; 41.36; 43.19; 43.47; 43.51 (43.51.3–4); 44.11. Standard edition: Loeb (Cary); the 2026 quality pass checked 43.47 and 43.51 in Cary's translation.
- Cicero, *De domo sua* 39–40.
- Cicero, *Letters to Atticus* 8.11.2; 9.7C.
- Cicero, *On Duties* 1.26.
- Cicero, *Philippics* 2.34; 2.84–87; 2.110.
- Lucan, *Civil War* 1.98–120; 1.125–126; 1.129–157.
- Menander, the line behind "the die is cast" (quoted in Greek by Plutarch; no fragment number given in the report).
- Nicolaus of Damascus, on the Lupercalia of 44 (the report gives no work or fragment number; see Not resolved).
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 7.91; 7.92.
- Plutarch, *Caesar* 15; 21; 22; 32; 35; 39; 46; 48; 49; 50; 56; 57; 61; 66; 67.
- Plutarch, *Cato Minor* 66–70; 72.
- Plutarch, *Crassus* 31.
- Plutarch, *Pompey* 48; 53; 54; 57; 59; 60; 77–80.
- Suetonius, *Julius* 20.1–4; 21; 22.1; 23.1; 24.1; 25.1; 26.1; 27.1; 28.3; 30.3; 30.4; 31–33; 37 (37.2); 41.3; 42.1–3; 52 (52.1, 52.2); 56.5; 76.1; 78.1; 79 (79.1–3); 80.2; 80.4; 81; 82.2–3; 86.1; 88. Standard edition: Loeb (Rolfe).
- Velleius Paterculus, *Roman History* 2.41; 2.47.1.
- Asinius Pollio and Oppius, lost writers named as the sources of Caesar's friends' accounts (via Plutarch and Suetonius); no fragments cited.

## Modern works
- Hans Delbrück: the report names him only for cutting Caesar's Helvetian and German figures; the work is not given (see Not resolved).
- Theodor Mommsen, *Die Rechtsfrage zwischen Caesar und dem Senat* (Breslau: Trewendt, 1857).
- Robert Morstein-Marx, *Julius Caesar and the Roman People* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2021).
- J. A. North, "Praesens divus" (review of Weinstock, *Divus Julius*), *Journal of Roman Studies* 65 (1975) 171–177 (title, journal and year confirmed from the Cambridge Core record; volume and pages from memory, not verified).
- Ronald Syme, "Caesar, the Senate and Italy", *Papers of the British School at Rome* 14 (1938) 1–31 (volume and pages from memory, not verified).
- Stefan Weinstock, *Divus Julius* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1971).

## Corpora and inscriptions
- The charter of Urso (Colonia Genetiva Iulia) on bronze tablets: the report gives no number. Usually *CIL* II² 5/1, 1022 = ILS 6087, and M. H. Crawford (ed.), *Roman Statutes* (1996), no. 25 (from memory, not verified).

## Not resolved
- "Hans Delbrück": no work given; presumably *Geschichte der Kriegskunst im Rahmen der politischen Geschichte*, vol. 1 (Berlin, 1900), which treats Caesar's numbers (not verified, so not listed).
- "Nicolaus of Damascus, writing in Augustus' circle, has a Licinius and Cassius present the diadem": no work or locator. Probably the *Life of Augustus* (FGrH 90 F 130), from memory, not verified.
- "The Greek tag is a line of Menander": no play or fragment given; the report does not say which line.
- The Urso charter is mentioned without a number; the identification above is from memory.
