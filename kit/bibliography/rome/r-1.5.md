# Rome 1.5: The seven kings: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aristotle, *Politics* 5.1313b (tyrants' devices).
- Arnobius, *Against the Pagans* 6.7 (the head of Olus).
- Claudius, speech on the Gauls: see Corpora (CIL XIII 1668) and Tacitus, *Annals* 11.24.
- Cicero, *On the Republic* 2 (cited generally for the kings and the curiate law); 2.28–29 (Numa and Pythagoras); 2.34 (Demaratus); 2.39–40 (the Servian census).
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 3.46–47; 4.2; 4.6–7; 4.16–18; 4.16–21; 4.27; 4.61 (temple of 200 feet a side). Loeb (Cary).
- Fabius Pictor and Calpurnius Piso: known only through Livy 1.44.2 and Dionysius 4.6–7 as cited.
- Aulus Gellius, *Attic Nights* 6.13 (*classis* and *infra classem*).
- Herodotus 1.82 (Thyrea).
- Livy 1.8.2–3; 1.8.7; 1.13.6–8; 1.15.8; 1.17.5–6; 1.18.2; 1.18.6–10; 1.19.2; 1.19.6–7; 1.20; 1.21.3; 1.22.2; 1.24–25; 1.24.1; 1.26; 1.28; 1.29–30; 1.31.8; 1.32; 1.32.5–14; 1.33.1–8; 1.33.9; 1.34.2; 1.34.8–9; 1.35.2; 1.35.6–9; 1.38.6; 1.38.6–7; 1.39.1–4; 1.39.5–6; 1.40–41; 1.41.6; 1.42–43; 1.43.10–11; 1.44.2; 1.45.2; 1.46.4; 1.48.7; 1.49.3; 1.55–56; 1.56.2; 1.60; 2.2.1–2; 2.8.6–8; 2.16.4; 2.21.5; 2.21.7; 5.19.6; 6.1.2; 9.38.15; 40.29.3–14.
- Ovid, *Fasti* 6.263–264; 6.627–636.
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 34.26; 35.152; 35.157 (citing Varro).
- Plutarch, *Numa* 1; 4; 8; 14.
- Suetonius, *Claudius* 42.2; *Julius* 6.1.
- Tacitus, *Annals* 4.65; 11.24.
- Varro, *On the Latin Language* 5.46; 5.55.

## Corpora and inscriptions
- *Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum* XIII 1668 (the Lyon tablet of Claudius' speech, found in 1528); a reworked version is in Tacitus, *Annals* 11.24.
- *Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum* I² 1 (the Lapis Niger cippus, found by Giacomo Boni in 1899). Standard edition of the early Latin inscriptions: *Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum* I², 2nd edn (Berlin: de Gruyter, 1893–1986).
- The François Tomb inscriptions (Vulci) and the Vibenna dedication at Portonaccio (Veii) are named without numbers.

## Not resolved
- "Giorgio Pasquali's phrase of 1936, 'la grande Roma dei Tarquini'": the article or book in which Pasquali used it was not confirmed. A 1936 piece in *Nuova Antologia* is the likely source, but this is from memory only.
- "Momigliano" (objection to Dumézil, weighed in module 2.1; and on the Mastarna equation): no work or year given.
- "Carmine Ampolo" (archaic social mobility), "Frank Brown" (Regia excavations, 1960s), "Francesca Fulminante" (gradual urbanisation), "Georges Dumézil" (later work): named without any title or year.
- "Fabius Pictor" and "Calpurnius Piso": known only through the later authors cited above.
