# Rome 9.5: Reading a site: Rome on foot: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Augustus, *Res Gestae* 20.3 (the Basilica Julia).
- Cassius Dio 43.49.1; 53.27.3.
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 1.79.11.
- Livy 8.14.12.
- Plutarch, *Marius* 26.
- Procopius (Gaiseric's removal of half the gilded roof of the Capitoline temple in 455; the report gives no passage).
- Statius, *Silvae* 4.2.
- Suetonius, *Augustus* 72.1; 101.4; *Julius* 88; *Tiberius* 20.

## Modern works
- Maria Letizia Conforto, *Adriano e Costantino: le due fasi dell'arco nella valle del Colosseo* (Milan: Electa, 2001). (the subtitle is as given in Wikipedia's bibliography for the arch; the report gives only the main title)
- Filippo Coarelli, "Substructio et Tabularium", *Papers of the British School at Rome* 78 (2010). (title, journal and volume confirmed via Wikipedia's reference list; pages not confirmed)
- A. L. Frothingham, "Who Built the Arch of Constantine? Its History from Domitian to Constantine", *American Journal of Archaeology* 16 (1912) 368–386. (confirmed in the AJA table of contents for 16.3 and Wikipedia)
- Patrizio Pensabene and Clementina Panella (eds), *Arco di Costantino tra archeologia e archeometria* (Rome: L'Erma di Bretschneider, 1999). (the report gives 1999; Wikipedia's bibliography lists 2001; the title and publisher are confirmed there)
- Nicholas Purcell, "Atrium Libertatis", *Papers of the British School at Rome* 61 (1993) 125–155. (identified from context as the 1993 article that argues the inscriptions do not name the Tabularium; confirmed via Wikipedia's reference list)
- Pier Luigi Tucci, "Where High Moneta Leads Her Steps Sublime: the 'Tabularium' and the Temple of Juno Moneta", *Journal of Roman Archaeology* 18 (2005) 6–33. (identified from context; confirmed via Wikipedia's reference list)
- Corrado Ricci, *Il mercato di Traiano* (Rome, 1929); publisher not confirmed. (not verified)
- Mark Wilson Jones, "Genesis and Mimesis: the Design of the Arch of Constantine in Rome", *Journal of the Society of Architectural Historians* 59 (2000) 50–77. (confirmed via Wikipedia's reference list)
- T. P. Wiseman, *The House of Augustus: A Historical Detective Story* (Princeton: Princeton University Press, 2019). (not verified)

## Corpora and inscriptions
- *Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum* (CIL) VI: 944 (the Circus Maximus arch of 80–81 for Titus); 945 (the Arch of Titus); 1139 (the Arch of Constantine, *instinctu divinitatis*); 1314 (Catulus' building inscription, lost, read as the Tabularium); 32422 (the Vestal's base of 364 with the name erased).
