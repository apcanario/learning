# Rome 11.3: Politics in the street: sources

Curated 1 November 2026 by the writer (Opus 5.5) from the report's inline citations. No web search was used; passages are given from memory and the writer's notes file lists those least certain.

## Ancient sources
- Appian, *Civil Wars* 1.29–30 (Saturninus' land law of 100; city plebs against countrymen). Loeb (White).
- Asconius Pedianus, commentaries on Cicero: on *Against Piso* (the 64 dissolution of the colleges; masters of colleges giving the Compitalia games; Clodius' grain law abolishing the price of 6⅓ asses); on *Pro Milone* (Bovillae, 18 January 52; the burning of the Senate house; the final decree of 52; Milo's trial and the jury's votes by order; the delivered speech; freedmen and the rural tribes, on *Pro Milone* 87). Clark (Oxford Classical Texts, 1907); Lewis, *Asconius: Commentaries on Speeches of Cicero* (2006).
- Caesar, *Civil War* 1.5 (the final decree of 49); 1.5.3; 1.7.5–6 (Caesar's speech to his soldiers); 3.20–22 (Caelius in 48). Loeb.
- Cassius Dio 36.42 (Manilius' freedmen law); 38.12–17 (Clodius' tribunate; 38.13 his four laws); 39.24 (manumissions and Pompey's distribution, 56); 40.45–46 (delayed elections); 40.49 (the Senate house burned); 40.54.3–4 (Milo and the mullets of Massilia); 42.29–32 (Dolabella in 47). Loeb book numbers.
- Cicero, speeches: *Against Catiline* 1.4; *Against Piso* 9, 14; *De domo sua* 25, 54; *De lege agraria* 2.6–10; *Pro Milone* 10, 11, 28–29, 72–80, 87; *Pro Plancio* (cited as a whole, defence under the law of 55); *Pro Rabirio perduellionis reo* 20; *Pro Sestio* 33, 34, 55, 75–77, 79, 86–90, 103, 104–106; *Verrines* 1.54, 2.3.163. Oxford Classical Texts; Loeb.
- Cicero, letters: *Letters to Atticus* 1.1.2, 1.16.11, 4.1.4, 4.1.6–7, 4.3.2–3; *Letters to Quintus* 2.3.2, 2.3.5. Shackleton Bailey (Cambridge, 1965–70; Loeb 1999–2002).
- Cicero, treatises: *On Duties* 2.72 (Octavius' grain law); *On the Laws* 3.19–26 (the tribunate debated). Loeb.
- [Quintus Cicero], *Commentariolum petitionis* 30–31 (Italy by tribes and towns). Loeb (with *Letters to Quintus*).
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 4.24.5 (slaves freed to draw public grain). Loeb (Cary).
- Granius Licinianus, book 36 (Lepidus' grain law of 78, five modii). Teubner (Criniti).
- Livy, *Periochae* 60, 84, 98, 115. Teubner (Rossbach); Loeb vol. 14.
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 36.104 (Milo's debts of 70 million sesterces). Loeb.
- Plutarch, *Caesar* 8, 55; *Cato Minor* 26; *Cicero* 35; *Pompey* 49; *Sulla* 8. Loeb (Perrin).
- Polybius 6.16.5 (tribunes bound to the people's wishes). Loeb (Paton, rev. Walbank and Habicht).
- *Rhetorica ad Herennium* 1.21 (Saturninus' grain law and Caepio's violence). Loeb (Caplan).
- Sallust, *Catiline* 29.2–3 (the final decree of 63 and its powers); *Histories* 1.77 M (Philippus' speech, 77), 3.48.19 M (Macer on five modii). Maurenbrecher numbering for the *Histories*; Loeb (Ramsey).
- Suetonius, *Julius* 41.3 (the grain list cut from 320,000 to 150,000), 42.3 (colleges dissolved). Loeb.
- Valerius Maximus 6.2.3 (Scipio Aemilianus and the contio). Loeb (Shackleton Bailey).
- Velleius Paterculus 2.4.4 (Scipio's "stepmother" retort); 2.45.1 (Clodius' law against Cicero); 2.47.4 (Cato's vote at Milo's trial). Loeb (Shipley).
