# Rome 12.8: The Aeneid after Rome: sources

Curated 1 November 2026 by the writer (Opus 5.5). Editions are those relied on where known; passages are those the report cites.

## Ancient and medieval sources
- Virgil, *Aeneid* 1.1; 2.1; 2.426–427; 4.9; 4.23; 4.615–620; 6.808–812; 6.851–853; 6.883; 9.447; 9.473; 12.64–69; books 1, 2, 4, 6, 11 named. Edition: Oxford Classical Texts (Mynors).
- Virgil, *Eclogue* 4 (4.6, 4.13–14); *Eclogue* 5 (on the Mantua monument); *Georgics*, praise of Italy (on the Mantua monument). Edition: Oxford Classical Texts (Mynors).
- Suetonius, *On Grammarians* 16 (Caecilius Epirota). Edition: Kaster (Oxford, 1995).
- Quintilian, *Institutio Oratoria* 1.8.5. Edition: Loeb (Russell).
- Juvenal, *Satires* 7.226–227. Edition: Loeb (Braund).
- CIL IV 9131 (Pompeii, the fullers' parody of *Aeneid* 1.1); the count of at least fifteen Pompeian graffiti of the first line, from the Judaism and Rome database record of CIL IV 9131.
- *Tab. Vindol.* 118 (*Aeneid* 9.473, with *seg(niter)*?), as edited in *Tabulae Vindolandenses* II and on Roman Inscriptions of Britain online.
- Masada papyrus of *Aeneid* 4.9: no edition or number named.
- Augustine, *City of God* 1.3; 10.27. Edition: Loeb.
- Aelius Donatus, preface and life of Virgil (pointed to module 12.1); his lost commentary.
- Tiberius Claudius Donatus, *Interpretationes Vergilianae*: named, no passage.
- Servius, commentary on Virgil: preface to book 6 (*totus quidem Vergilius scientia plenus est*); the commentary in general. Edition: Thilo–Hagen. "Servius Danielis" (Pierre Daniel's edition, Paris 1600).
- Macrobius, *Saturnalia* (Servius as a speaker): no passage.
- Fulgentius, *Exposition of the Content of Virgil* (*Expositio Virgilianae continentiae*): named, no section.
- Bernard Silvestris (attributed), commentary on the first six books of the *Aeneid*: named, no passage.
- Lactantius, *Divine Institutes* 7.24. Edition: CSEL (Brandt).
- *Oration to the Assembly of the Saints* (ascribed to Constantine), chapters 19–21. Edition: Heikel, GCS Eusebius I.
- Jerome, *Letters* 53.7 (to Paulinus of Nola). Edition: CSEL (Hilberg).
- Proba, *Cento Vergilianus de laudibus Christi*, line 23. Edition: Schenkl, CSEL 16 (1888).
- Ausonius, *Cento nuptialis*: named (pointed to module 10.2).
- *Decretum Gelasianum*: named, no section.
- Isidore of Seville, *Etymologies* 1.39.26. Edition: Lindsay (OCT).
- *Historia Augusta*, *Hadrian* 2.8; *Severus Alexander* 14.5. Edition: Loeb (Magie).
- John of Salisbury, *Policraticus* 1.4 (the bronze fly).
- Conrad of Querfurt, letter of 1194: no edition named.
- Gervase of Tilbury, *Otia imperialia* (about 1211): no section.
- Alexander Neckam: named, no work.
- Petrarch, *Itinerarium* (1358): the exchange with King Robert of Naples.
- Simone Martini, frontispiece to Petrarch's Virgil (Milan, Biblioteca Ambrosiana).
- Anonymous, *Roman d'Eneas* (about 1160): no edition named.
- Heinrich von Veldeke, *Eneas*: named.
- Dante, *Inferno* 1.70–72, 1.85–87, 4.39–42, 20.113; *Purgatorio* 21.97–98, 22.67–73, 30.21, 30.48, 30.49–51; *Paradiso* 20.67–69. Edition: Petrocchi.
- Sequence for St Paul at Mantua (the apostle at Virgil's tomb): as reported by Comparetti; not seen in an edition.
- Maffeo Vegio, *Aeneid* book 13 (1428): named.
