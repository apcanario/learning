# Seed bibliography: Egyptian history and mythology

Compiled 21 Nov 2026 from the designer's knowledge. **Not checked.** No entry was opened and none was checked for existence in the design chat; S-EG0 checks each against Crossref's or the publisher's record before EG1 is written, corrects it to the record and strikes what it cannot find. Nobody has checked what any entry says. A writer opens an entry before citing it, and an entry that cannot be opened carries no claim.

Kept short on purpose: 70 entries. A module may add a source it finds and opens; it lists the addition in its notes so this file can take it.

"Open" says where a writer is likely to be able to read it: OA (open access at the publisher or an institutional repository), DOI (the journal's page; the abstract at least), Press (a book at its publisher: often the record and contents only, so plan on an opened reporter).
