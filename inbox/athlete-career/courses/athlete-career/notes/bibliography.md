# Seed bibliography · Managing an Athlete's Career

A starting list. **It is checked for existence only and is never an authority on what a source says.** A writer opens a reference before citing it and logs what was read.

Status, as of the design chat (21 Nov 2026):

- **seen**: its record appeared in a web search that day (title, authors, venue matched). Not opened.
- **secondhand**: only reports about it were seen. The original must be found.
- **unchecked**: listed from the designer's knowledge. Existence not confirmed.

Row S-AC0 checks every entry against Crossref's, the publisher's or the issuing body's record and rewrites this column as "exists (where checked, date)" or "not found".
