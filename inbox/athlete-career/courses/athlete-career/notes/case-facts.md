# Case register · Managing an Athlete's Career

The athletes, their coaches and families are those of Sport Psychology for Coaches. **Their facts are in `courses/sport-psychology/notes/case-facts.md`, and that file wins on any conflict.** This file adds a commercial layer only. It was reconciled with that register on 21 Nov 2026 (repo commit 3188250, read only).

Everything here is invented. First names only for people, as in that register; coaches and parents have no names ("Sara's coach", "Dani's parents"). Every amount is in euros and invented. No case company stands for a real one, and none is ever said to deal with a real company.

## How writers use it

- Label each person and company "(fictional)" on first use in a unit.
- A fact a unit invents is listed in that unit's notes, to be added here at filing.
- The case is never evidence for a claim about the world.
- No fact here states a real federation's rule, age category or weight class.
- The country is left unstated outside the "In Portugal" sections, which work the case *as if* the athlete were registered with a Portuguese federation, and say so.

## Carried from Sport Psychology's register (not to be contradicted)

- **Sara**, 20, 400 m. University student, second year, lives away from home. First season as a senior. Eight months back from a hamstring injury that cost a season. Trains in a group of six; her coach, a man with 20 years of coaching, also coaches two other groups, which include athletes under 18. Her group belongs to an athletics club with a welfare officer. Exam periods in January and June. A referral to her university's student health service is under way after six weeks of poor sleep and flat sessions.
- **Dani**, 14, judo. A girl. National title in her age group at 13. Trains five times a week at a club; school on weekdays. Both parents come to every competition. Her coach: a man, 15 years of coaching, a former competitor; the squad is 18 athletes aged 11 to 17. The club has a welfare officer and a second coach. At a review with both parents she chose to move up a weight class. A concern about her eating has been passed to the club's person for medical concerns; that referral continues.
- **Milan**, 17, football. Academy midfielder since 11. The academy decides at the end of this season whether to keep him, in the same weeks as his final school exams.

These states matter here: nothing commercial is added to Sara's or Dani's load without regard to the referrals, and a unit that plans public work for either says so.

## Sara: the commercial layer

- Competes at national championship level; not on a national team; the season's aim is a qualifying standard for an international championship.
- Works about ten hours a week beside her studies.
- No agent and no approach from one.
- **Her coach is part-owner of Corrin Athletic**, a small label that makes running kit. Corrin has given Sara kit for two seasons with nothing in writing. Corrin also supplies two athletes her coach does not coach, and none of the other five in her group.
- Her coach has also handled entries, travel and money matters for her informally and unpaid. He takes no payment from her.
- Other support: a small annual grant from her municipality; her club pays entry fees.
- Season cost about 9,400; income and support in place about 5,100; shortfall about 4,300. (AC5 builds the budget and may revise these, recording the change.)
- Public presence: one account with about 3,100 followers, mostly local; two pieces in the local paper last season.
- Her limits: no supplement, betting or alcohol sponsors; no content from inside her home; nothing in exam weeks.
- Brannoch Mutual's marketing manager has asked whether Sara is "available"; its community programme would want its mark on her kit, where Corrin's mark now is.

## Dani: the commercial layer

- Her parents handle her administration.
- **Her coach owns Hallam Mat & Kit**, a small label that sells training wear and kit bags to combat-sport clubs, hers among them. He wants Dani in the label's photographs and would give her kit.
- Tarnwick Building Supplies has offered to pay a season's travel in exchange for a mark on the club tracksuit and a photograph.
- A supplement shop has offered free product. It is refused in every unit.
- No personal public account; the club's account posts results with her parents' consent.
- In AC15 most commercial lines of Dani's plan read "not yet", each with its reason.
