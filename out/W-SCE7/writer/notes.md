## Contradictions

1. SCE1 · flag F6, TMS row of section 3. Quoted: "| **Transportation management system** (TMS) | Working placement only: carriers, rates, loads and shipments | Plan and tender, carry and track | Gartner lists TMS as execution software [analyst]; what it holds is sourced in SCE7 and SCE8 |". What is wrong: no longer only a working placement. Source: Gartner Peer Insights, "Transportation Management Systems Reviews and Ratings", market definition, features updated April 2026, https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/transportation-management-systems (SCE7 §5). Exact replacement: "| **Transportation management system** (TMS) | Carrier sourcing and rates, the shipment plan (consolidation, mode, route, carrier), carrier communications, tracking events, and the expected charge settlement checks invoices against | Plan and tender, carry and track; the check of the carrier's invoice | Gartner TMS market definition, Peer Insights, features updated April 2026 [analyst] (SCE7 §5); paying the carrier is not in the definition |". Touches SCE1, SCE7, SCE8, SCE10.
2. SCE1 · flag F6, section 3 matrix figure, row "Transport system (TMS)". Quoted cells: "working", "working" under Tender and Track, null under Settle. What is wrong: Tender and Track are now sourced, and settlement's invoice check sits in the TMS on Gartner's definition. Source: as item 1. Exact replacement: cells "holds" under Tender and Track, and "check" under Settle, with the legend line {"mark": "check", "means": "matches expected charges against actual (Gartner TMS definition, April 2026)"}; the ERP's Settle cell stays "working" for payment. Touches SCE1, SCE10.
3. SCE1 · flag F6, section 2 step 10. Quoted: "This unit opened no source on how a carrier is paid; SCE7 sources it." Exact replacement: "SCE7 §6 sources the carrier's side for the US: under 49 CFR Part 377 (2009 edition) a motor carrier presents its freight bill within 7 days and the default credit period is 15 days, a tariff allowing no more than 30 calendar days; Gartner's TMS definition places the check of the invoice against the expected charge in the transport system [analyst]. No European rule was opened." Touches SCE1, SCE7, SCE10.
4. SCE1 · glossary (deliverable B). Quoted rows: "| Transportation management system (TMS) | Software for planning, buying and following freight movements | Gartner SCE glossary [analyst]; contents sourced in SCE7–SCE8 |"; "| Tender | The offer of one load to one carrier | Course term; SCE7 sources it |"; "| Truckload (TL) | One vehicle hired for one shipper's load | Course term; SCE7 sources it |"; "| Lane (freight) | A recurring route from one origin to one destination that a shipper buys carriage for | Course term; SCE7 sources it |". Exact replacements: TMS: "| Transportation management system (TMS) | Software for multimodal sourcing, planning and execution of transport, with visibility, settlement and analytics | Gartner TMS market definition, April 2026 [analyst] (SCE7 §5) |"; Tender: "| Tender | The offer of one load to one carrier, which accepts or rejects it | Acocella, Caplice & Sheffi 2020 [practice] (SCE7 §3) |"; Truckload: "| Truckload (TL; FTL in Europe) | A vehicle dedicated to one shipper's shipment from origin to destination | Sheffi 2004 [practice] (SCE7 §2) |"; Lane: "| Lane (freight) | A one-way movement from an origin to a destination, with its shipments, that a shipper buys carriage for | Sheffi 2004 [practice] (SCE7 §3) |". Touches SCE1, SCE7.
5. SCE1 · section 2, region note, and glossary row "Consignment note (CMR)". Quoted: "requires the contract of carriage to be confirmed by a consignment note made out in three original copies signed by sender and carrier (Arts. 4 and 5(1))". What is wrong: an omission that can mislead a product team; Art. 4 also says the absence, irregularity or loss of the note does not affect the existence or validity of the contract. Source: CMR, Art. 4, https://treaties.un.org/doc/Treaties/1961/07/19610702%2001-56%20AM/Ch_XI_B_11.pdf (SCE7 §7). Exact replacement: "requires the contract of carriage to be confirmed by a consignment note made out in three original copies signed by sender and carrier, though the note's absence, irregularity or loss does not affect the contract's existence or validity (Arts. 4 and 5(1))". Touches SCE1, SCE7.
