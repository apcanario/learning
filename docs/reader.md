# The reader

Every brief in this Project is written for one reader. This profile is extracted from the Brand + AI hub's shared preamble (`PRE`, now the `## Preamble` of `courses/brand-ai/briefs.md`) and from the Personal Mythology kit (`AGENT-SPEC.md`, the course pages and syllabi, `README.md`, `NEXT-SESSION.md`). Nothing is added: each line says where it comes from. A brief copies what it needs from here and nothing else.

## How he reads (Personal Mythology)

- An educated adult reader who wants genuine depth (`AGENT-SPEC.md`).
- Self-directed study, built so that the gaps get filled in the right order and nothing is taken on trust (site home).
- Reads in order; each report assumes the ones before it, and Rome assumes the Greek course before it (syllabus, "How to run it").
- One or two reports a sitting, about fifteen to twenty minutes each; more than two a day and the self-tests stop meaning anything (course page, "How to read it").
- Self-tests before moving on; under 7 of 10, re-read (course page and syllabus).
- Reads on his phone: the pages are mobile-first and progress "is stored on this phone only" (`README.md` design rules; syllabus).
- Treats a report as a study text, not scripture: citations are there so he can check them (course page).

## What he wants from the writing

- Direct, no flattery, no filler, no motivational language, no "not X but Y" constructions (Brand + AI preamble).
- Plain, direct prose; no preamble, no summary of what is about to be said, no closing pleasantries; do not pad (`AGENT-SPEC.md`).
- Length is whatever the topic needs; never padded, never cut to a target (Brand + AI preamble; `AGENT-SPEC.md`, Pedro's rule of 27 October 2026).
- Every unit keeps its sources for future checks (`AGENT-SPEC.md`, Pedro's rule of 27 October 2026).
- In working sessions: direct and terse, one-line progress updates (`NEXT-SESSION.md`, `BACKLOG.md`).
