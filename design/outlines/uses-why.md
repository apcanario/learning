# Which page shows which relief, and why

Written by `kit/p96_uses.py` (P96, 8 Nov 2026) from its `MAP`; `uses.json` is written with it. For each reading page given a relief, the outline and the sentence of the page's own text that decided it: the text discusses that object at length, not in passing ("names" is how often the page's prose names the outline by its keywords). A page with none discusses no object at length; the outline its text names most is given with its count. A unit filed after P96 is added here by its filing (`skills/file-unit`).
