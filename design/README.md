`theme.css` is the only place style is defined in the Learning Project. It is the Personal Mythology visual system (the history site's `shell.css` of 27 October 2026), with three fixes and the few components the hubs need that the history pages lack. Every page in the Project is built from it; no page carries its own stylesheet.

## Why it is inlined

A published claude.ai artifact cannot load an external stylesheet. So the generators read `theme.css` at build time and write it into each page's `<style>`. The consequence: **a change to `theme.css` reaches a page only when that page is rebuilt and republished.** There is no other way to propagate it.

## How to change it and propagate the change

1. Edit `design/theme.css`. Change a token in section 1 before touching a component; most changes are a token.
2. Copy it over the kit: `cp design/theme.css kit/shell.css`. The theme keeps every class name `shell.css` had, so `build.py` and `shell.js` need no change (verified 28 October 2026: no class undefined, `build.py` builds, `verify.py` passes).
3. Rebuild every hub: `cd kit && python3 build.py && python3 verify.py`; from `generator/`, `python3 build_hub.py data/brand-ai` → `hubs/brand-and-ai-courses.html`; any other hub: `python3 build_hub.py data/<slug>`.
4. Check each rebuilt page at the three widths (phone 390 or 400 px, tablet 768 px, desktop 1280 px) in dark and light: no horizontal scroll, no boxes closer than 6 px, every control at least 44 px tall, the drawer a drawer on the tablet and a sidebar on the desktop, light mode legible (the checks are listed below). `kit/verify.py` and `generator/check_page.py` run all of it.
5. Republish each page to its existing artifact (never a new one). The links are in `docs/courses-index.md`.

Dark accents are the house palette and do not change. No grey is a dominant accent. The light accents (6.3, P90, Pedro 4 Nov 2026: e-ink, bright accents within contrast) keep each house hue at 100% saturation and take the highest lightness that holds 4.6:1 on the darkest surface the accent is text on, its own track band (7% of it over the paper); `kit/contrast.py` prints every pair (the accent on the paper, on a card, cream text on the accent, the eyebrow's `.u`, the accent on its band), before and after in `kit/reviews/p90/`. In light a filled button's label (`.hbtn span`) is at full opacity, since at .85 it measured 4.49:1 on red and purple. No pure white is used anywhere in light mode.

## Figures

`kit/viz.py` draws a declared figure (timeline, compare, tree, flow; since V1 sitemap, objects, decision, matrix, states, swimlanes, wireflow; Academy's Figure kinds page shows each) as inline SVG with classes only (and, since P92, the home's topography, its one home kind, `contours`, styled by the `.topo` and `.ct-*` classes above, which a unit does not declare; P91's HTML bookshelf, `shelf`, is gone); section 4i of the theme gives every class its fill and stroke from the tokens, so a figure follows the theme like any text. Since P90 a sitemap node may carry `href` (a panel id: its box is an `<a class="viz-a">` with `data-target`, as the drawer's links are, which `shell.js` follows) and `dashed` (a dashed, untinted box, `.viz-dash`: a planned page), and the block may carry `phone` (`{"stack_from": 1}`: a second drawing of the same root as wide as the phone frame, `svg.viz-alt`, shown below 768 px in place of the wide one). The Academy home's map was such a figure (P90; gone since P91). A page never styles a figure; a new kind is a function in `viz.py` plus its classes in 4i. The house accents are reachable as tones 1 to 6 (`--viz-1` … `--viz-6`).

## Rules for pages

- A page's only `<style>` is `theme.css`, inlined by a generator. No `style` attribute except to set a custom property (`--c` item colour, `--cc` course colour, `--hc` callout colour) or a progress width.
- Every page carries `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover">`. Without it phones lay the page out 980 px wide (the v5.1 Brand + AI hub had no viewport tag, which is why its 44 px targets measured 32 to 40 px on a phone).
- New component: first check section 4 for one that already does the job (most hub needs were met by `.howto`, `.smod`, `.hrow`, `.course`, `.pill`). If none does, add it to section 5 with a one-line comment, using tokens only.

## Checks before republishing

At 390 or 400 px, 768 px and 1280 px wide, in dark and in light, on every page with every `<details>` open: the document is not wider than the viewport; no two boxed elements are closer than 6 px; no button, summary or block link is under 44 px tall; no console error; on the tablet the drawer is off screen until the menu button and 360 px with a backdrop when open; on the desktop it is a 300 px sidebar at the left, the content column starts beside it, the top bar spans the column, the current page is highlighted and report text keeps its 68ch measure; text tokens keep at least 4.5:1 against `--bg` and `--card`. `python3 generator/check_page.py generator/hubs/<page>.html` (hubs) and `python3 kit/verify.py` (Academy) measure all of it for every panel at every width in both themes (`verify.py` measures contrast since P90: every text of the home, the drawer, the changelog, the backlog, each course home and one reading page per course in the light theme, against the background it actually sits on) and end with ALL CHECKS PASSED or FAIL (Playwright with Chromium); `--shots DIR` saves the three widths.

The responsive rules (section 6) add no component and change no token: a breakpoint only widens the column, puts cards side by side or pins the drawer open. Phone layout is checked against the previous build's panel count and tightest gap before and after any theme change (28 October 2026: 144 panels, 6 px, unchanged).
