## What I could not verify

- F121, workarounds in a warehouse: the closest candidate I found is Cheon, E. & Erickson, I., "Fulfillment of the Work Games: Warehouse Workers' Experiences with Algorithmic Management" (arXiv 2508.09438v1, 13 Aug 2025; the arXiv page gives ACM DOI 10.1145/3757409). I read the full text on arXiv. It is two years of ethnography (from Sept 2020) of one large US online retailer's fulfilment-centre workers: 16 interviews with workers at 16 centres in 12 states, plus their online communities. It describes scanner and rate "tricks" (logging out to get an easier pick path, scanning printed barcodes, batch-scanning stows, delaying scans to lengthen breaks). The paper ties them to the rate and to time-off-task tracking, and does not state that dislike of the system drives them. That bears on C14 and on §5's inference that a workaround protects a measure. I could not confirm that it was published or peer-reviewed (the harness refused the Crossref record, and no search returned the ACM page), so it does not meet Part 8 as read, and I give no replacement text. It also names the retailer, which the course's naming rules would need to settle. Proposed: a later row with search on opens the ACM record and, if peer-reviewed, tests C14 against it.
