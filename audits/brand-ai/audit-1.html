7 · The three most consequential errors

  1. The weekly-cadence argument rests on a misread number. §2 says moving from bi-weekly to weekly "roughly doubles the growth rate" in vidIQ's data. The page shows view growth ×1.5 and subscriber growth ×3.9 between the 1–3 and 4–7 uploads-a-month tiers, on channels that already had 1,000 subscribers.
