## Findings

1. [serious] [delay] §7, step 3; Current: "3. **If there is immediate danger, call the emergency number** where you are, and stay with the athlete until help arrives."; Problem: step 3 and step 4 make the coach judge whether danger is immediate, while step 5 tells him "do not decide yourself how serious it is" and section 6 tells him not to ask about method. A coach who follows the letter has no basis for the judgement and may choose the slower "same day" branch; no default for doubt is given. Fix: "3. **If there is immediate danger, or you cannot tell, call the emergency number** where you are, and stay with the athlete until help arrives."; Basis: IOC toolkit 2021 p. 41 emergency pathway as quoted in §7; the unit's own step 5; ISSF page: red flags call for "immediate help" (read 18 Nov 2026).

2. [serious] [delay] §7, step 4; Current: "Stay until the athlete is with someone who can help."; Problem: nothing says what to do if the athlete refuses help or walks away. Section 6 and plan B tell the coach that an adult's decision to seek help is theirs, so a coach following the unit may let an adult who has talked of ending their life leave alone. The IOC toolkit's own exception (immediate danger) is not carried into the steps. Fix: "Stay until the athlete is with someone who can help. If the athlete refuses help or leaves while you fear for their safety, call the emergency number at once and tell them what was said and where the athlete may be going."; Basis: IOC toolkit pp. 39, 41, as quoted in §6 ("aside from when an athlete poses an immediate danger to themselves or others"); grade [practice].

3. [serious] [minors] §7, step 4; Current: "for a minor, the parents or guardians as your plan and the club's welfare officer say."; Problem: the urgent route (and the template C1 and C2 lines for minors) sends the coach to the parents by default. If what the child has said concerns how someone at home treats them, calling the parents first can put the child at greater risk. No caveat anywhere in the unit. Fix: "for a minor, the parents or guardians as your plan and the club's welfare officer say, unless what the child has told you concerns how someone at home treats them: then the welfare officer, or the police or child-protection authority if the child is in danger, and not the parents, until the welfare officer advises."; Basis: NSPCC Sport, *How to respond…* (7 Jan 2026): do not confront an alleged abuser "as this could put the child at greater risk" (said of staff; applied to family as the unit's inference); NSPCC Sport, *Deal with a concern* (read 18 Nov 2026): immediate danger, police "straight away". Grade [practice].

4. [should-fix] [scope/delay] Deliverable A, last row; Current: "| **Distress during skill practice (SP5)** | At once for that exercise | Stop the exercise for her without comment; first conversation after | [The student health service]; a red flag: the rows above | Not seen. |"; Problem: the red-flag table has no row for an athlete telling the coach that someone is hurting them. A coach at a competition looking for that case finds nothing, and nothing tells him not to question further, not to confront anyone, and that immediate danger means the police. Fix: add a row after it: "| **An athlete tells you someone is harming them, or you see signs of it** | At once, once is enough | Listen without leading questions; do not investigate, do not confront anyone; no secrecy; write down their words the same day [practice; NSPCC Sport, 2026] | Immediate danger: [the emergency number]. Otherwise the same day: [the club welfare officer], by [the federation's safeguarding policy] (DD-SP1) | Not seen. |"; Basis: NSPCC Sport pages read 18 Nov 2026 ("without leading or intrusive questions"; "do not confront…"; "you do not need to decide whether abuse has taken place in order to report a concern"; immediate danger, police).

5. [should-fix] [delay] §4, second rule; Current: "goes the same day, without waiting for anything"; Problem: the tool the unit quotes says red flags call for "immediate help"; "the same day" lets a coach finish the session or the event first. Fix: "goes at once, by section 7, without waiting for anything"; Basis: ISSF page, read 18 Nov 2026.

6. [should-fix] [delay] Deliverable A, row "Other highly uncharacteristic behaviours"; Current: "The same day: [the student health service]; if any danger, the emergency number"; Problem: a red flag ("seek immediate help") is routed to a daytime routine service that is closed evenings, weekends and at away competitions. Fix: "At once, with her: [the university's out-of-hours or crisis contact], or [the student health service] if it is open now; if any danger, the emergency number"; Basis: ISSF page; the unit's own urgent route (§7, plan B "Urgent route: same day").

7. [should-fix] [delay] Deliverable A, row "Dramatic weight changes"; Current: "| The same day: [the student health service], [her doctor] |"; Problem: as 6: a red flag sent to routine daytime services, with no out-of-hours contact and no emergency line. Fix: "| At once, with her: [the student health service] or [her doctor] if open now, otherwise [her doctor's out-of-hours service]; if she is unwell, the emergency number |"; Basis: ISSF page; plan B.

8. [should-fix] [delay] §4, second rule; Current: "**Referral when the change continues after the first conversation, spreads beyond sport, or the athlete asks for help.**"; Problem: section 6 step 4 offers referral in the first conversation, but this rule makes referral wait for the change to "continue" after it, with no interval. With the two-week point before the conversation, low mood or short sleep can run for an open-ended time before a referral. Fix: "**Referral offered at the first conversation; made when the athlete agrees, or when the change continues at the follow-up you agreed, spreads beyond sport, or the athlete asks for help.**"; Basis: §6 step 4 and IOC toolkit p. 41 (non-emergency pathway: offer contact; follow up within 24 hours); grade [practice].

9. [should-fix] [delay] §6, "Where and when"; Current: "Choose a time with room to talk: never just before competing, never straight after a loss (SP7, SP8) [practice]."; Problem: read alone, "never just before competing" can hold back a conversation when a red flag shows on competition day. Fix: "Choose a time with room to talk: never just before competing, never straight after a loss (SP7, SP8), unless a red flag is in front of you: then section 7 at once, whatever the timetable [practice]."; Basis: §10 ("a red flag outranks every event on the timetable"); ISSF page.

10. [should-fix] [confidentiality] §6, "What not to promise"; Current: "I'll need to tell [the person on the plan], and I'll tell you first who and why."; Problem: "I'll tell you first" is a promise a coach cannot always keep with a minor: in an emergency, or where the welfare officer advises otherwise, the call comes before the explanation. The unit's own rule is "Promise only what you will keep". Fix: "I'll need to tell [the person on the plan], and I'll tell you who and why, before I do it whenever I can."; Basis: The FA, *Reporting concerns* (read 18 Nov 2026): "do not promise confidentiality or outcomes"; NSPCC Sport, 2026.

11. [should-fix] [harm] §8, "Staying their coach"; Current: "Keep the athlete in the squad and in ordinary training unless the person they were referred to advises otherwise."; Problem: between referral and appointment, ordinary training continues by default even when the concern is low energy availability, disordered eating, overtraining or injury, where the load itself may be part of the harm. Fix: "Keep the athlete in the squad and in ordinary training unless the person they were referred to advises otherwise. Where the concern is eating, low energy, overtraining or injury, ask the medical contact that week what training is safe until the appointment, and add no load meanwhile."; Basis: REDs consensus (Mountjoy et al., 2023, abstract): harm to health from too little energy for training; §4's own "adjust load as the doctor advises"; grade [practice].

12. [should-fix] [urgent] §7, after the steps; Current: "The same steps apply when an athlete talks about harming someone else; then the safety of others comes first, and the emergency number is the first call if anyone is in danger."; Problem: §7 and the table cover only talk. A coach who finds that an athlete has already hurt themselves, or has taken something to do so, has no instruction. Fix: "The same steps apply when an athlete talks about harming someone else; then the safety of others comes first, and the emergency number is the first call if anyone is in danger. If an athlete has already hurt themselves, or says they have taken something to harm themselves, it is a medical emergency: the emergency number and first aid at once."; Basis: IOC toolkit p. 41 ("serious and immediate danger": contact emergency services); grade [practice]. Describes no method.

13. [should-fix] [phone] Deliverable A, header; Current: "| Sign | How long, how much | What to do | Who to call | Sara now |"; Problem: five columns, long cells (the fifth red flag runs to 40 words); on a phone the table scrolls sideways and "Who to call", the cell a coach needs at a competition, sits fourth, off-screen; rows 2 to 4 read "As above". Fix: "| Sign | Who to call | What to do | How long, how much | Sara now |" with each row's cells moved to match, and "As above" replaced in rows 2 to 4 by the row-1 contacts written out; Basis: the brief (the reader reads on a phone).

14. [should-fix] [phone] Deliverable A, opening line; Current: "**Built for Sara.** Red flags first, then concerns. \"How long\" is the unit's own point for acting [practice] unless a cell cites a source."; Problem: the deliverable has no short urgent route a coach can find in seconds; the steps live in §7 with the emergency call at step 3. Fix: prepend: "**Urgent, before anything else.** 1. Danger now, or you cannot tell: [the emergency number where you are]; stay with them. 2. A red flag, no danger now: do not leave them alone; with them, call [the urgent contact] at once. 3. Then tell [the person the plan names] and write it down the same day.\n\n**Built for Sara.** Red flags first, then concerns. \"How long\" is the unit's own point for acting [practice] unless a cell cites a source."; Basis: §7; IOC toolkit p. 41; the brief (phone reader).

15. [minor] [scope] §4, perfectionism; Current: "her coach records whether her talk is about her own standards or about others' judgement, and does not label it."; Problem: sorting her talk into self-oriented and socially prescribed perfectionism is the meta-analysis's construct, an assessment in all but name. Fix: "her coach records her words as she says them, with the date, and does not label or sort them."; Basis: the brief's scope (no assessment); §8 handover ("no label").

16. [minor] [scope] Deliverable A, perfectionism row; Current: "Record whether her talk is about her standards or others' judgement; no label"; Problem: as 15. Fix: "Record her words as she says them, with the date; no label"; Basis: as 15.

17. [minor] [confidentiality] Deliverable C1; Current: "I'd have to get help, and I'd tell you first."; Problem: as 10, for an adult; the same promise may be unkeepable in an emergency, and the template copies it for minors. Fix: "I'd have to get help, and I'd tell you who and why, before I did it whenever I could."; Basis: as 10.

18. [minor] [clarity] Deliverable A, row "Dramatic weight changes"; Current: "No comment on weight, body or food; raise it privately [practice; the course's safety rule]"; Problem: "raise it" reads as raising the weight change, which the same cell forbids. Fix: "No comment on weight, body or food; tell her privately what you have seen in training, in behaviour words, and offer the contact [practice; the course's safety rule]"; Basis: §4 eating paragraph; DD-SP2.

19. [minor] [supervision] §7, step 2; Current: "Ask another adult to cover the squad, or stop the session."; Problem: "stop the session" alone can leave minors unsupervised while the coach stays with one athlete. Fix: "Ask another adult to cover the squad; if there is none, stop the session and keep the squad together where you can see them until they are collected."; Basis: §10 (second adult); NSPCC Sport, away day trips (adequate staff); grade [practice].
