Confirmed:
- Mossman et al. 2024 (abstract, https://informahealthcare.com/doi/abs/10.1080/1750984X.2022.2031252): 1,320 correlations from 131 samples, N = 38,844; ρ = .39, .16, −.01 and −.19; associations with well-being, distress and need satisfaction; competence support, relatedness support, structure and involvement listed; culture and type of sport did not moderate.
- Gu & Cheng 2026 (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1716745/full): 177 studies, 334 effect sizes (313 cross-sectional, 12 longitudinal, 9 experimental); r = 0.27, CI 0.22–0.32 in the table and 0.20–0.32 in the text; I² = 98.45%; the "pattern of association" wording; coding of undesirable outcomes not stated; volume 16, published 8 Jan 2026; adolescents, in PE and physical activity settings.
- Morales-Sánchez et al. 2020 (https://mdpi.com/1660-4601/17/13/4909): 103 male footballers aged 12–17, one club, convenience sample; path 0.53; indirect effect 0.35; the burnout dimensions; thwarting described as "stronger and more threatening" than absence of satisfaction; CCBS developed by Bartholomew et al. 2010; the autonomy-supportive recommendations; citation fields.
- Gómez-López et al. 2020: task climate with fear of failure r = −0.31 (publisher's table); authors, volume, issue and article number.
- Hu et al. 2023 (https://frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1106916/full): 252 athletes aged 10 to 25+, from professional teams in six provinces, in team and individual events; cross-sectional; γ ≈ −0.46; direct path not significant; mediation through needs (0.351) and a chain through constrained commitment.
- Zhang, Du & Tao 2025 (https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1433171/full): 325 athletes aged 12–18 in China; two waves two weeks apart; "causation cannot be established"; resilience, optimism and coach-rated development; volume 15, published 8 Jan 2025.
- Zhang, Wang & Shao 2022 (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.871936/full): 21 studies, 1,199 participants aged 5–19, 9 cross-sectional and 12 longitudinal; the sports listed; enjoyment r = 0.45 [0.42, 0.49], k = 4; coach support r = 0.43 [0.08, 0.69], k = 4; coach support SMD 0.68 [0.27, 1.10], k = 5; intention SMD 1.13; enjoyment and actual persistence not pooled.
- Smoll et al. 2007 (https://jyd.pitt.edu/ojs/jyd/article/view/358/344):
  - Design: 17 intervention and 17 control teams, 34 coaches; 75-minute coach and 60-minute parent workshops, delivered by the first author; quasi-experimental.
  - Sample: 151 athletes aged 9–15 (mean 11.6), 82 and 69 analysed; only athletes whose parents attended were counted in the intervention group.
  - Results: about 12 weeks apart; SAS-2 range 15–60; means 25.44 to 23.98 and 24.12 to 27.01; interactions significant for the total and all three subscales.
  - Not collected: no behavioural data and no effect sizes.
  - Also confirmed: the guideline list; volume 2(1), article 0701FA002.
- Tzioumakis et al. 2026 (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1563931/full):
  - Design: 68 coaches (36 intervention, 32 control) in four countries; MMCOS; three time points; about six hours of workshop; players aged 10–15.
  - Results: disempowering and ego-involving behaviour declined; autonomy and relatedness support rose only temporarily ("short-term, but not sustained").
  - Not reported: no standardised effect sizes and no athlete outcomes.
  - Volume 17, published 5 Aug 2026.
- Adams et al. 2024 (https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fspor.2024.1428682/full): 20 former college athletes aged 19–44, nine sports, qualitative; some running punishment accepted; performance-linked conditioning distinguished from punitive; punishment described as arbitrary, unsafe and cruel; injury; volume 6, article 1428682.
- Lewis et al. 2023 (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.1005705/pdf): non-disabled adults aged 18–40; positive comparative feedback not different from no feedback at retention; perceived competence higher than with time feedback only; published 24 Jan 2023.
