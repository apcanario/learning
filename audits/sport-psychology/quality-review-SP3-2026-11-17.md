## Confirmed

- Mossman et al. 2024 abstract: 1,320 correlations, 131 samples, N = 38,844; ρ = .39 autonomous, .16 introjected, −.01 external, −.19 amotivation; no moderation by culture or type of sport; related supports competence, relatedness, structure, involvement. https://informahealthcare.com/doi/abs/10.1080/1750984X.2022.2031252
- Gu & Cheng 2026: 177 studies, 334 effect sizes; 313 cross-sectional, 12 longitudinal, 9 experimental; task/mastery r = 0.27 [0.22, 0.32] (table); quote 'a pattern of association rather than a confirmed causal mechanism'; anxiety coding not stated. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1716745/full
- Gómez-López et al. 2020: task climate with fear of failure r = −0.31. https://www2.mdpi.com/1660-4601/17/2/592
- Morales-Sánchez et al. 2020: 103 male players aged 12–17, one club, convenience sample, one session; β = 0.53; indirect effect 0.35; thwarting 'a stronger and more threatening experience than the absence of their satisfaction'; choice, perspective, meaning of activities, involvement in decisions; ABQ with exhaustion, devaluation, reduced accomplishment; CCBS from Bartholomew et al. 2010. https://mdpi.com/1660-4601/17/13/4909
- Smoll et al. 2007: 75- and 60-minute workshops; 17 v 17 teams; 34 coaches; 82 and 69 athletes; ages 9–15, mean 11.6; ~12 weeks; SAS-2 15–60; means 25.44→23.98 and 24.12→27.01; interactions for total, somatic, worry, concentration disruption; quasi-experimental; only athletes with attending parents; no behavioural data; first author delivered; no effect sizes; every guideline listed in sections 2 and 4. https://jyd.pitt.edu/ojs/jyd/article/view/358/344
- Tzioumakis et al. 2026: 68 coaches (36/32), four countries, MMCOS, three time points, ~6-hour workshop, players 10–15, reduction in disempowering and ego-involving behaviour, 'short-term, but not sustained', no standardised effect sizes, no athlete outcomes. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1563931/full
- Hu et al. 2023: 252 athletes, 10 to 25+, six provinces, team and individual sports, cross-sectional; γ = −0.455; chain indirect effects; 'had no significant impact on FF'. https://frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1106916/full
- Zhang, Du & Tao 2025: 325 athletes aged 12–18 in China, two waves about two weeks apart, coach-rated development, 'causation cannot be established'. https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1433171/full
- Zhang, Wang & Shao 2022: 21 studies, N = 1,199, ages 5–19, 9 cross-sectional and 12 longitudinal; enjoyment r = 0.45 [0.42, 0.49] k = 4 ('high'); coach support r = 0.43 [0.08, 0.69] k = 4 and needs 'moderate'; coach support SMD 0.68 [0.27, 1.10] k = 5; intention SMD 1.13; enjoyment with persistence not pooled; sports as listed. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.871936/full
- Monteiro et al. 2017: six studies, ages 5–30, four Canadian, 1982 and 1985 among them, mixed designs, reasons as listed, 'controllable by the athletes'; 727 in text v about 955 in table. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5968952
- Adams et al. 2024: 20 former US college athletes, 19–44, nine sports, qualitative; most accepted some physical punishment; punitive conditioning arbitrary, unsafe, cruel; injury and fear accounts. https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fspor.2024.1428682/full
- Bergeron et al. 2015: all ten quoted phrases in sections 5, 7, 8, 9 verbatim. https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/49/13/843.full
- Fletcher & Sarkar 2016 notes: 'the world's best athletes and teams', 'extreme challenge-no support environments', 'the unethical and inappropriate imposition of adversity'; 2012 and 2016 Games; no test of effects. https://informahealthcare.com/doi/full/10.1080/21520704.2016.1255496
- Gustafsson et al. 2011 abstract: multifaceted; process and state perspectives; antecedents, early signs, consequences, personality, coping, environment; not a definitive model. https://informahealthcare.com/doi/full/10.1080/1750984X.2010.541927
- Gao et al. 2024: 29 studies (26 quantitative, 3 qualitative), 9,185 athletes, 2,191 parents, 10–19, 82.8% cross-sectional, no pooled estimate, 'the manner and extent of their involvement are key'; autonomy support with self-determined motivation; support with enjoyment; parents v coaches mixed. https://frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1291711/full
