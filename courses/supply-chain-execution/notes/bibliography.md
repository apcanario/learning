# Seed bibliography: Supply Chain Execution for Product Teams

Compiled 19 Nov 2026. **Checked for existence only.** Nobody has checked what any entry says. A writer opens an entry before citing it, and an entry that cannot be opened carries no claim (preamble, sourcing standard).

Kept short on purpose: 41 entries. A unit may add a source it finds and opens; it lists the addition in its notes so this file can take it.

How each entry was checked:
- **Crossref**: the record at `api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>` was read on 19 Nov 2026 and the authors, title, journal, year, volume, issue and pages below are as it gives them.
- **Not opened**: named here as a starting point only; no page was read.
- **Search**: found by web search on 18 or 19 Nov 2026 at the address given or on the publisher's or issuing body's page; details as that page gives them.
- **Abstract**: the analyst firm's own abstract page was seen; the report itself was not read.
- **Secondhand**: seen only as cited by another page, which is named. The writer finds the original or does not use it.
