<h2 class="hsec">Frame</h2>
<section class="howto edge" style="--hc:var(--red)"><h2>Pick</h2>
  <p><strong>The decision first, the technique second, the claim third.</strong></p>
  <ul><li>Every AI feature in this software makes or supports one operational decision.</li><li>Four technique families fail in four different ways; the label hides which.</li><li>Most evidence is simulation or vendor-told; the course grades and tags it.</li></ul>
  <p class="over"><strong>Override if:</strong> you want a course about generative AI and agents only; then AIE2 and AIE3 shrink to one unit and AIE4 grows to three.</p>
</section>
<div class="report"><ul>
  <li><strong>What the course is for.</strong> Being able to look at a claimed AI feature and say what it decides, what kind of technique does the work, what a person still decides, who pays when it is wrong, and how good the evidence is. It is the third course of the Supply Chain track. No unit is interview preparation.</li>
  <li><strong>Stands on its own files.</strong> It reuses the track's fictional case and assumes the ground of Supply Chain Execution for Product Teams, and it never points to that course's units by code, so it does not wait for that course to be finished.</li>
  <li><strong>Out of scope.</strong> Demand forecasting and inventory planning (the planning course). Robots and their control software (the automation course). How to build a model, write a prompt or design an interface.</li>
  <li><strong>No real vendor is assessed.</strong> The first course's rule, unchanged, extended to companies that provide AI models. The fictional vendors gain fictional AI features and six fictional claims.</li>
  <li><strong>Where the evidence pushes back.</strong> The solid work is decades of optimisation research; it was not called AI when it was written. Machine learning for batching, slotting and routing is published mostly as simulation on synthetic or historical data; field experiments in execution work are few. The human-factors research comes from cockpits, control rooms and clinics, and its transfer to a dispatch desk is an inference, which the units say. For generative features and agents in execution software no independent field study was found when the course was designed; expect "vendor-reported" and "no independent source found" often. The statistics that circulate lead back to interviews, polls and predictions. The law is moving, so units read the texts and date them.</li>
</ul></div>
