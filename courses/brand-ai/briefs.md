## Preamble

Write one unit of a self-study course in the same long-form report format as his history course: a long-form report for one reader. Length is whatever the topic needs (2,000–6,000 words); do not pad and do not cut to a target. Reader: Pedro, 35, Porto. Senior Director of Product Management (UX/AI) at a PE-backed software company; ADHD; ended competitive karate after the WIKF Global Cup (Oct 2026), still trains Wado-Ryū and kumite and coaches; next journeys are a sub-1:45 half marathon in spring/summer 2027, −15 kg, and a self-taught history course; owns Shōmen, a small karate-apparel brand; publishes in English with some Portuguese; has 5–7 h/week. Brand thesis (working name to be decided) — he gets consumed by a journey, builds a syllabus, records the process so he does not forget it, and ships the artefact; plurality is the register, each piece leads with one journey; ADHD is public under his name; failures stay in the record. Tone: direct, no flattery, no filler, no motivational language, no 'not X but Y' constructions. Course goal: {GOAL} Sourcing standard: every claim that carries a number, a mechanism or a recommendation is sourced; acceptable sources are peer-reviewed work, platform-official documentation or statements, large-sample vendor datasets (named, with sample size and date), and documented creator cases with verifiable numbers; creator anecdotes are labelled as such; correlational and vendor data is labelled as such; no uncited figures; no invented handles, names or numbers; if a claim cannot be sourced, say so and leave it out of the deliverable. A unit that reads as advice without evidence is a failed unit. Where the unit depends on current facts (prices, platform rules, model lineups), search before writing and date the facts. End with the unit's deliverable, built out in full, not described.
