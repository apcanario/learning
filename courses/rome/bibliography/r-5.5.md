# Rome 5.5: Antony, Octavian and Cleopatra: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources

## Corpora and inscriptions
- *Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum* XI 6721 (sling bullets from the siege of Perusia).
- The papyrus of 33 BC granting tax privileges to Publius Canidius, with the Greek subscription taken as Cleopatra's autograph: the report gives no number. Usually *BGU* XIV 2376 (from memory, not verified).

## Not resolved
- "Augustus' memoirs, cited by Plutarch": the report gives no title and cites no fragment collection (the lost autobiography is usually called *De vita sua*; not verified).
- The papyrus number under Corpora is not given by the report.
- Lange (2011), Murray and Petsas (1989), van Minnen (2000) and Johnson (1978) appear in the report as author and year only; each is completed above as noted.
