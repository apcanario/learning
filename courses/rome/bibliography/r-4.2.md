# Rome 4.2: The First Punic War: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Appian, *Punica* 4.
- Augustine, *City of God* 1.15.
- Cassius Dio, *Roman History*, as excerpted in Zonaras, *Epitome of Histories* 8 (8.13 on the terms offered by Regulus). Dio's text for this period survives only in Zonaras and fragments.
- Cicero, *On Duties* 3.99–111.
- Cicero, *On Old Age* 44.
- Cicero, *Against Verres* (*Verrines*) 2.2.2.
- Diodorus Siculus, *Library of History* 23.4.1; 23.8; 23.9.1; 23.18.1; 24.1.5; 24.1.9; 24.11; 24.12; 24.13 (books 23–24 survive in Byzantine excerpts).
- Eutropius, *Breviarium* 2.20.
- Gellius, *Attic Nights* 7.3; 7.4; 17.21.45 (on Naevius). Gellius 7.3–4 is the report's route to Tubero and Tuditanus on Regulus.
- Horace, *Odes* 3.5.13–56.
- Livy, *Periochae* 14; 16; 17; 19; 20 (books 16–19 of Livy are lost). Standard edition: Jal, Budé (1984).
- Naevius, *Punic War* (lost; known through Gellius 17.21.45 for his service in the war).
- Nepos, *Hamilcar* 1.
- Philinus of Agrigentum and Fabius Pictor, the lost partisan histories Polybius uses (known through Polybius 1.14–15 and Diodorus 23.8). Fragments: Jacoby, FGrH 174 (Philinus) and 809 (Fabius Pictor).
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 35.22.
- Polybius, *Histories* 1.1–1.88 (cited passim; locators cited: 1.5; 1.7.2–4; 1.9.7–8; 1.10.1–2; 1.10.3–4; 1.10.5–9; 1.11.1–3; 1.11.4–5; 1.11.9–12.4; 1.12.5–9; 1.14; 1.15; 1.16.1–3; 1.16.6–8; 1.16.9; 1.19.15; 1.20.1–2; 1.20.5–8; 1.20.9–21.2; 1.20.13–14; 1.20.15–16; 1.21.4–8; 1.22; 1.23; 1.25.7–26.9; 1.26.7; 1.26.10–16; 1.28; 1.28.14; 1.29.9; 1.30; 1.31; 1.31.4–8; 1.32; 1.34; 1.35; 1.36; 1.36.11; 1.37; 1.37.1–4; 1.37.7–10; 1.38; 1.39; 1.39.6–8; 1.40–48; 1.49–51; 1.51–54; 1.52.2–3; 1.54; 1.56; 1.56.1–2; 1.57–58; 1.58; 1.59.6–8; 1.60–61; 1.61; 1.62.8–9; 1.63.1–3; 1.63.4–6; 1.63.9; 1.64.6; 1.65; 1.66–67; 1.72; 1.79–80; 1.83.3–4; 1.83.5–11; 1.84–85; 1.88.6; 1.88.7; 1.88.8–12); 3.10; 3.26; 3.26.2–5; 3.27.2–6; 3.28; 3.28.1–4; 6.19. Standard edition: Loeb (Paton, rev. Walbank and Habicht).
- Servius, commentary on Virgil, *Aeneid* 4.628 (a treaty keeping each power off the other's shores).
- Valerius Maximus 9.6 ext. 1.
- Ancient authors for the Regulus tradition, cited at second hand through Gellius: Aelius Tubero and Claudius Tuditanus (consul 129), both lost.

## Corpora and inscriptions
- *Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum* I², 2nd ed. (Berlin): CIL I² 25 (the column of C. Duilius, preserved in an archaising imperial copy); CIL I² 9 (epitaph of Lucius Cornelius Scipio, consul 259).
- *Die Fragmente der griechischen Historiker*, ed. F. Jacoby: see Philinus and Fabius Pictor above.
