# Rome 8.4: Julian, Theodosius and the end of the old gods: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Ambrose, *Letters* 17; 18; 40–41; 51 (the report numbers them as in the Maurist order; it notes that 17 and 18 are 72 and 73 in the CSEL edition, ed. Zelzer). Also *On the Death of Theodosius* 34 and *Explanation of Psalm 36* 25.
- Ammianus Marcellinus, *Res gestae* 14.11; 16.10; 16.12; 19 (book 19, Amida); 20.4; 22.5.2; 22.5.3–4; 22.10.7; 22.13.1–2; 22.16.12–13; 23.2.6; 23–25 (as the eyewitness books); 24.7.4; 25.3.3–6; 25.3.6; 25.3.23; 25.4; 25.4.17; 25.4.20; 25.7.9–11; 26.4.3; 27.3.13–14; 31.2–3; 31.4.6; 31.4.11; 31.5.5–7; 31.12.1; 31.12.3; 31.12.6–7; 31.12.11–17; 31.13.12–16; 31.13.18–19; 31.16.9. Standard edition: Loeb (Rolfe). Section numbers for 31.4.6 and 25.3.3 from memory, per the writer's note.
- Augustine, *Confessions* 8.2.4–5 (Marius Victorinus); *City of God* 5.26.
- Cassius Dio 51.22.1–2.
- Claudian, *On the Third Consulship of Honorius* 96–98 (the Latin quoted).
- *Codex Theodosianus* 13.3.5; 16.10.2; 16.10.10; 16.10.11; 16.10.12. Standard edition: Mommsen and Meyer; translation by C. Pharr.
- Eunapius, *Lives of the Sophists* 472; 474–475. Cited by page number; the writer's note records the pages as 474–475 (Maximus and Julian) and 472 (the monks).
- Eutropius, *Breviarium* 10.9.
- Jerome, *Chronicle* under the year 354 (statue of Marius Victorinus); *Against John of Jerusalem* 8 (Praetextatus and Damasus).
- Julian, *Letter* 22 (to Arsacius, high priest of Galatia) and *Letter* 36 (the rescript on teachers), in the numbering of Wright's Loeb edition; *Misopogon*; *Letter to the Athenians*.
- Libanius, *Oration* 18.274–275 (funeral speech for Julian); *Oration* 30 (cited without a section, for Cynegius and the monks).
- Orosius, *Seven Books of History against the Pagans* 6.15.32; 7.33; 7.35.19.
- Paulinus of Milan, *Life of Ambrose* 26.
- Prudentius, *Against Symmachus* 1.
- Rufinus, *Church History* 11.22; 11.23; 11.33.
- Sozomen, *Church History* 5.16; 6.1–2; 7.15; 7.25.
- Symmachus, *Relatio* 3 (3.9; 3.10; 3.11–15); *Letters* 2.36. Edition: O. Seeck, MGH *Auctores antiquissimi* 6.1 (1883).
- Themistius, *Oration* 16.
- Theodoret, *Church History* 3 (the dying cry "You have won, Galilean"); 5.17; 5.17–18; 5.22; 5.24.
- Zosimus, *New History* 2.40; 5.38. Standard edition: F. Paschoud (Budé).

## Corpora and inscriptions
- CIL VI 2145 (*Corpus Inscriptionum Latinarum*, vol. 6): the statue of Praetextatus set up by the chief Vestal Coelia Concordia.
- The *Calendar of 354* (cited in the report as a luxury codex, with no edition). Standard edition: Th. Mommsen, *Chronica minora* 1 (MGH *Auctores antiquissimi* 9, 1892) (not verified).
- The consular list of Constantinople (*Consularia Constantinopolitana*), cited for the treaty of 3 October 382, no edition given.
