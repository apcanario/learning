# Rome 1.6: The fall of the monarchy: Lucretia, Brutus and Porsenna: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aristotle, *Politics* 1311a (tyrannies overthrown through outrage; Peisistratids); 1313b (building works as a tyrant's device).
- Augustine, *City of God* 1.18; 1.19.
- Aulus Gellius, *Attic Nights* 1.19 (the Sibylline Books); 4.5 (Horatius' statue).
- Cicero, *On the Nature of the Gods* 2.6.
- Diodorus Siculus 16.69.
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 4.53–58; 4.55; 4.58.4; 4.62; 4.66–67; 4.69; 4.85; 5.9–12; 5.18; 5.24–25; 5.35; 5.36; 6.2; 6.13; 7.3–12; 7.3–4; 7.5–6. Loeb (Cary).
- Herodotus 1.166–167; 3.80.5; 3.153–158; 5.63; 5.92.
- Justin 20.3.
- Lactantius, *Divine Institutes* 1.6 (quoting Varro on the Sibyl).
- Livy, preface 10; 1.49; 1.50–53; 1.53.4–54.10; 1.54.6; 1.55–56 (via 1.56.1–3); 1.56.4–8; 1.56.9–12; 1.57; 1.58.1–5; 1.58.6–12; 1.59.1–2; 1.59.7–60.4; 2.1.7; 2.2.1–2; 2.2.3–11; 2.3–4; 2.5.8; 2.6–7.2; 2.7.5–8.2; 2.8.4–8; 2.9; 2.10; 2.12; 2.13; 2.14.1; 2.14.5–9; 2.19–20; 2.20.12; 2.21.3–4; 2.21.5; 2.34.4; 2.42.5; 2.48–50; 3.44–58; 7.3.5–8; 7.27.2; 10.9.3–6. Oxford Classical Texts (Books 1–5, ed. Ogilvie) is the standard edition for the books cited.
- Ovid, *Fasti* 2.685–852.
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 33.19 (shrine of Concord and the 204 years); 34.17 (tyrannicide statues); 34.139 (Porsenna's treaty).
- Plutarch, *Brutus* 1 (citing Posidonius); *Coriolanus* 3; *Publicola* 7; 19. Loeb (Perrin).
- Polybius 3.22; 3.26.1; 6.55.
- Tacitus, *Histories* 3.72.
- Thucydides 6.56.
- Posidonius: known only through Plutarch, *Brutus* 1.

- Sandra R. Joshel, "The Body Female and the Body Politic: Livy's Lucretia and Verginia", in Amy Richlin (ed.), *Pornography and Representation in Greece and Rome* (New York and Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1992) 112–130 (not verified).
- Theodor Mommsen, *Römische Chronologie bis auf Caesar*, 2nd edn (Berlin: Weidmann, 1859) (not verified).
- R. M. Ogilvie, *A Commentary on Livy Books 1–5* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1965) (not verified).

## Corpora and inscriptions
- *Fasti Capitolini*, the Augustan consular lists (cited generally; no edition in the report). Standard edition: A. Degrassi, *Inscriptiones Italiae* XIII.1 (Rome, 1947) (not verified).
- Named documents without numbers: the first treaty with Carthage (in Polybius 3.22); Cn. Flavius' dedication of the shrine of Concord (in Pliny, *Natural History* 33.19); the Satricum stone (*Lapis Satricanus*; see Stibbe and others above); the Pyrgi tablets (module 1.2); the bronze plaque to Castor and Pollux from Lavinium (module 1.1).

## Not resolved
