# Rome 5.3: Pompey, Crassus, Cicero and Catiline: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Appian, *Civil Wars* 1.105; 1.107; 1.116–118; 1.120; 2.5–6; 2.9 (the "three-headed monster" attributed to Varro). Standard edition: Loeb (White).
- Appian, *Mithridatic Wars* 94; 95.
- Asconius, commentary on Cicero's *In toga candida* (on Catiline; no passage number given in the report). Standard edition: Oxford Classical Texts (Clark).
- Cassius Dio, *Roman History* 37.26–28; 37.49–50; 37.54–58. Standard edition: Loeb (Cary).
- Cicero, *Against Catiline* 4.7–10; 4.10 (the four speeches are tabulated; individual speeches otherwise not cited by passage).
- Cicero, *De imperio Cn. Pompei* 14–19; 28; 32–33; 35; 62.
- Cicero, *De lege agraria* 2.3.
- Cicero, *Letters to Atticus* 1.16.5; 2.1.3; 2.1.8; 2.3.3.
- Cicero, *Philippics* 11.18.
- Cicero, *Pro Murena* 52.
- Cicero, *Verrines* 1.56; 2.2; 2.3; 2.4; 2.5 (2.5.162).
- Florus and Orosius: cited as descending from Livy for the Spartacan war, with no passage.

- Josephus, *Jewish Antiquities* 14.72.
- Livy, *Periochae* 97; 98; 99; 103.

- Sallust, *Catiline* 18–19; 20; 28; 31.6; 35; 40–41; 44–45; 48; 49; 51 (51.27; 51.43); 52; 53–54; 61.
- Sallust, *Histories* 1.55; 1.77 (Maurenbrecher numbering, "M"); 3.48 M (speeches of Lepidus, Philippus and Licinius Macer). Standard edition of the fragments: B. Maurenbrecher, *C. Sallusti Crispi Historiarum reliquiae* (Leipzig: Teubner, 1891–93).
- Suetonius, *Julius* 1; 9; 18; 19; 74.
- Velleius Paterculus, *Roman History* 2.30.5; 2.30.6; 2.31.2; 2.40.3; 2.44.1.
