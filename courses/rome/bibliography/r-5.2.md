## Not resolved
- "Alberto Campana, La monetazione degli insorti italici durante la guerra sociale, 1987": the report gives author, title and year; place and publisher could not be confirmed, so it is not listed under Modern works with an imprint.
- "Plutarch ... names Posidonius and the historian Gaius Piso (Marius 45)": the Piso is identified by Plutarch only as "Caius Piso, an historian" (quality-pass review, finding 2); the report does not say which Piso.
- Sulla's *Memoirs*, Rutilius Rufus, Catulus and Sisenna: lost works known only through Plutarch, Cicero and Sallust; no fragment collection is cited by the report.
- Sallust, *Histories* 1 "speech of Lepidus": no fragment number given in this report (see 5.3, where it is Maurenbrecher 1.55).
