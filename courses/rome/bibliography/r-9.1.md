# Rome 9.1: Roman law: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- *Acts of the Apostles* 22.25–29; 25.10–12 (Paul's appeal to his citizenship).
- Asconius, commentary on Cicero's *Pro Cornelio* (for the tribune's law of 67 binding praetors to their edicts; the report gives no passage number).
- Augustine, *City of God* 2.21 (preserving Cicero, *On the Republic* 5.1, with Ennius' line on ancient customs).
- Aurelius Victor, *Caesares* 41.4.
- Callistratus, in Justinian, *Digest* 48.19.28 pr. (the ranking of capital penalties). Digest passages generally: standard edition Mommsen and Krüger, *Digesta Iustiniani* (Berlin, 1868–70).
- Celsus, quoted by Ulpian, in *Digest* 1.1.1 pr. (ius as "the art of the good and the fair").
- Cicero, *Verrines* 2.1.109 ("annual law"); 2.5.165.
- Cicero, *On Duties* 3.60 (Aquillius Gallus and formulas against deceit).
- Cicero, *On the Orator* 1.212.
- Cicero, *On the Republic* 5.1 (as preserved by Augustine).
- Cicero, *Pro Roscio Amerino* 70–72.
- Eutropius, *Breviarium* 8.17 (Salvius Julianus and the edict).
- Florentinus, in *Digest* 1.5.4.1.
- Gaius, *Institutes* 1.7; 1.8; 1.9–11; 1.13–15; 1.17; 1.18; 1.22; 1.29; 1.37; 1.38; 1.42–46; 1.48; 1.53; 1.78; 1.96; 1.99; 1.119; 1.134; 1.159–162; 2.2–9; 2.14a–22; 2.79; 2.87; 2.102–104; 3.56; 3.88; 3.89; 3.92; 3.182; 3.223; 4.2–3; 4.11; 4.12; 4.30; 4.39–43; 4.48; 4.69–74; 4.75. The text rests on the Verona palimpsest; a standard edition is Seckel and Kuebler (Teubner, 1935).
- Gaius, in *Digest* 41.1.7.7; 47.9.9.
- Gellius, *Attic Nights* 5.19 (adrogation); 20.1.13 (Labeo and Lucius Veratius); 20.1.53 (the Tarpeian Rock and the false witness).
- Isidore, *Etymologies* 5.2.2.
- Julian, in *Digest* 1.3.32.1.
- Justinian, *Deo auctore* (530; cited without section); *Tanta* 1; *Institutes* 1.5.3; 2.1.25; *Code of Justinian* 2.57.1; *Novel* 118 (543).
- Juvenal, *Satires* 8.213–214.
- Livy 6.42.11; 7.16.7; *Periochae* 68 (the epitome, for Publicius Malleolus).
- Marcian, in *Digest* 48.9.1.
- Modestinus, in *Digest* 48.9.9 pr.
- *Digest* 29.5 (the Silanian decree; no jurist named in the report).
- *Passion of Perpetua* 6.
- Papinian, in *Digest* 1.1.7.1.
- Polybius 6.14.7.
- Pomponius, in *Digest* 1.2.2.6; 1.2.2.7; 1.2.2.35; 1.2.2.38; 1.2.2.41; 1.2.2.47; 1.2.2.49.
- *Rhetorica ad Herennium* 1.23.
- Suetonius, *Augustus* 40.4; *Vespasian* 11.
- Tacitus, *Annals* 3.75; *Histories* 4.11.
- *Theodosian Code* 1.1.5; 1.4.3 (the Law of Citations); 9.15.1.
- Ulpian, in *Digest* 1.1.10 pr.–1; 14.6.1; 50.17.32.
- Varro, *On the Latin Language* 6.29–30.
