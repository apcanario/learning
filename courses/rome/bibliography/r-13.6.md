# Rome 13.6: Musonius Rufus and Epictetus: sources

Written 1 November 2026 by the report's writer (Opus 5.5). Passages are those the report cites. "(not verified)" marks a reference given from memory and not confirmed online.

## Ancient sources
- Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* 1113a10–11 (prohairesis as deliberate desire of what is up to us). (not verified: line numbers)
- Arrian, prefatory letter to Lucius Gellius, prefixed to Epictetus' *Discourses* (text: Schenkl, Teubner, 2nd ed. 1916; trans. Oldfather, Loeb, 1925–1928).
- Cassius Dio, *Roman History* 65(66).13.2 (Loeb 65.13.2, traditional 66.13: Vespasian expels all philosophers except Musonius). Loeb text and numbering checked on LacusCurtius (Cary, Loeb vol. 8).
- Epictetus, *Discourses* (Arrian): 1.1 (1.1.10–12, 1.1.23, 1.1.26–27); 1.2.8–18, 1.2.17–18, 1.2.19–24; 1.7.32; 1.9.29; 1.16.20; 1.19.19–22; 1.28; 2.10; 2.18.24; 3.1.40; 3.2.1–5, 3.2.6–8; 3.22 (3.22.10, 3.22.23–25, 3.22.54, 3.22.67–72, 3.22.76, 3.22.81–82, 3.22.93–96); 3.23.29; 3.24. Text Schenkl; translation Oldfather (Loeb). Section numbers within 3.22 from memory (not verified).
- Epictetus, *Handbook* (*Encheiridion*): 1 (1.1–3, 1.5); 3; 5; 9; 17; 30; 53.
- Gellius, *Attic Nights* 1.2 (Herodes and the *Discourses*); 2.18 (Epictetus a slave, recent memory); 15.11 (Epictetus leaves Rome for Nicopolis under Domitian's decree); 17.19 (Favorinus: bear and forbear); 19.1 (the lost fifth book; pointer to module 13.2).
- *Historia Augusta*, *Hadrian* 16.10 (friendship with Epictetus).
- Juvenal, *Satires* 3.116–117 (the Stoic informer who killed Barea, his friend and pupil). (not verified: line numbers)
- Lucian, *Against the Ignorant Book-Collector* 13 (Epictetus' lamp, 3,000 drachmas); *Demonax* 55 (Epictetus urges Demonax to marry).
- Pseudo-Lucian, *Nero* (Musonius at the Isthmus canal); cited without section.
- Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* 1.7 (pointer to module 13.7); 4.41 (a little soul carrying a corpse, as Epictetus said).
- Musonius Rufus, *Lectures* (Lucius' record, excerpted by Stobaeus), by Lutz's numbering: 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11, 12, 13A, 13B, 14, 15, 18A, 18B, 19, 20, 21. Edition and translation: Cora E. Lutz, *Musonius Rufus: "The Roman Socrates"*, Yale Classical Studies 10 (New Haven, 1947).
- Origen, *Against Celsus* 6.2 (Epictetus read by ordinary people); 7.53 (Celsus' story of the broken leg).
- Philostratus, *Life of Apollonius* 5.19 (Musonius digging at the Isthmus); 7.16 (Gyaros and the spring). Checked against the Livius translation.
- Photius, *Library*, codex 58 (Arrian's eight books of *Discourses* and twelve of *Conversations*). (not verified: codex number)
- Pliny the Younger, *Letters* 3.11 (Artemidorus, Musonius' son-in-law; Pliny's admiration).
- Simplicius, *Commentary on Epictetus' Handbook*, preface (Arrian's selection) and the biographical remarks (lame from youth; the adopted child); cited without passage. Translation: T. Brennan and C. Brittain, *Simplicius: On Epictetus' Handbook 1–26* and T. Brennan, *27–53* (Ithaca, 2002).
- Stobaeus, *Anthology* (the source of the Musonius lectures); cited without passage.
- Suda, s.v. Musonius (Volsinii) and s.v. Epictetus (Hierapolis; rheumatism).
- Suetonius, *Nero* 49.3 (Epaphroditus helps Nero die); *Domitian* 14.4 (Epaphroditus executed).
- Tacitus, *Annals* 4.30 (Tiberius refuses waterless Gyaros); 14.22 (Rubellius Plautus sent to Asia, 60); 14.59 (Coeranus and Musonius counsel Plautus, 62); 15.71 (Verginius Flavus and Musonius exiled, 65; the Latin sentence quoted); 16.32 (Egnatius Celer and Barea Soranus).
- Tacitus, *Histories* 3.81 (Musonius, equestrian, lectures the Flavian soldiers); 4.10, 4.40 (prosecution and condemnation of Celer; Demetrius defends him).
