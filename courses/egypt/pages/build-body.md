  <h2>Procedure</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>One fresh writer per module, in code order, four modules to a chat. The chat stages a packet for the writer: the module's brief from this course's Briefs page, never reworded; the sourcing standard; the seed bibliography, <code>courses/egypt/notes/bibliography.md</code>; every module of this course already filed; and, where a brief points back, the Greek or Roman module named. The course has no source units and no case register.</li>
    <li>The writer opens every reference before citing it and keeps a source log with the address opened, what was read and the date; cites an Egyptian text by its standard designation and the translation opened; gives every date before 664 BC as approximate with its chronology; grades every claim by EG1's key; puts every disagreement in a dispute box with a named source or scholar on each side; and reports its contradictions with a filed module or a brief, and the needs the syllabus does not meet.</li>
    <li>A fresh checker that has not seen the writing runs seven passes: every Sources entry against its publisher's or Crossref's record, every cited claim against its source, the grades, time and number, names and positions, lane, pointers and tone, and the closing material and figures. Its report is filed as <code>audits/egypt/fresh-check-&lt;code&gt;.md</code> before any fix; findings are applied as exact replacements.</li>
    <li>Anything found wrong in a filed module goes into the next module's prompt and is fixed there first; anything that bears on a module not yet written is appended to <code>courses/egypt/notes/syllabus-flags.md</code> and printed in that module's prompt. A flag that would change a brief is proposed, never applied by the chat that raised it; a writer never rewords a brief.</li>
    <li>The module is filed at <code>courses/egypt/&lt;code&gt;-&lt;slug&gt;.md</code> with the front matter of method.md §7; this data file is updated; Academy is rebuilt, verified and published, the artifact and then GitHub Pages, before the next module begins.</li>
    <li>A read by the Project's owner sits on a bundle boundary (after bundle 1 and after bundle 15); starting the next writing chat is his go. There is no gate row.</li>
  </ol>
  <h2>Models</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Syllabus: Fable 5.2</strong>, as the Project sets for course design.</li>
    <li><strong>Writing: Opus 5.5</strong> for every module. The deliverable is written in the same context as its module.</li>
    <li><strong>Fresh check: Opus 5.5</strong>, in a separate context.</li>
    <li><strong>Not Sonnet or Haiku</strong> for modules or checks.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
<section class="howto edge" style="--hc:var(--gold)"><h2>Review rule</h2>
  <p>Every new module gets its fresh check before filing: every reference against its record, every cited claim against its source, the deliverable against the brief, every figure against the text. Modules with the search flag on (EG18, EG46, EG55, EG56, DD-EG5) state facts that can change and are re-checked for staleness six months after writing.</p>
