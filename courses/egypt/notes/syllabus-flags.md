# Syllabus flags: Egyptian history and mythology

Raised by the chats that write this course's modules. A flag is a contradiction between modules or briefs, or a need the syllabus does not meet. A brief is never reworded by the chat that raises a flag: a flag that would change a brief is proposed and listed in that chat's reply for the Project's owner to veto (`docs/egypt-prompts.md` section 4, point 3, and section 0, rule 7). `kit/next_prompt.py` passes every open or carried flag whose Affects cell names a module to that module's writer; an Affects cell names module codes or ranges of them (`EG1 to EG57`), never "every module" (P-SCP1; override 10 of row S-EG0).

Status: open (not yet acted on), carried (the named module's writer was given it and its module deals with it), resolved (the row that worked it, or a brief change, settled it), dropped (with the reason).

Flags F1 to F5 open the file as the syllabus (`docs/egypt-syllabus.md` section 9) set them, filed by row S-EG0 on 23 Nov 2026; F2's Affects cell, "every module" in the syllabus, names the codes, and F5's "Part 9" is Part 12 (override 1 of row S-EG0).
