# Seed bibliography: Egyptian history and mythology

Compiled 21 Nov 2026 from the designer's knowledge; **checked for existence only** by row S-EG0 on 23 Nov 2026 (syllabus decision D14). Nobody has checked what any entry says. A writer opens an entry before citing it, and an entry that cannot be opened carries no claim (preamble; Part 12 of `docs/sourcing-standard.md`; flag F2).

Kept short on purpose: 70 entries. A module may add a source it finds and opens; it lists the addition in its notes so this file can take it.

"Open" says where a writer is likely to be able to read it, as the design judged it: OA (open access at the publisher or an institutional repository), DOI (the journal's page; the abstract at least), Press (a book at its publisher: often the record and contents only, so plan on an opened reporter).

How each entry was checked (23 Nov 2026; WebSearch first, then WebFetch only of an address a search had returned):
- **In full**: the entry's own record was read on its publisher's page, the publisher's own front matter or PDF, or the issuing body's page, and it gave the author or issuer, the title and the year; an entry naming several works counts in full only if every one was read so. The entry below is corrected to that record.
- **In part** (not counted as checked): only search listings were seen (a publisher's page that appeared in a search but could not be fetched, a library catalogue, a bookseller, a review whose title carries the record), or a third party's page was read. The entry is corrected only where a listing agreed with itself; the writer finds the record before any use.
- **Not opened**: no page of the entry was seen. None.
- **Struck**: not found anywhere. None.
- **Crossref**: no Crossref record was read. The session refused the fetches of `api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>` (approval was not given), so each article was checked at its publisher's page instead, where that page could be fetched.

Counts: **checked in full 30**; checked in part 40 (not counted as checked); not opened 0; struck 0.
