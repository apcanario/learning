# Seed bibliography · Managing an Athlete's Career

A starting list. **It is checked for existence only and is never an authority on what a source says.** A writer opens a reference before citing it and logs what was read.

Compiled in the design chat (21 Nov 2026). Existence check of 23 Nov 2026 (row S-AC0, a fresh Opus 5.5 agent): every one of the 38 entries was looked for again against Crossref's, the publisher's or the issuing body's own record. Nothing was read about what any source says.

Counts, 23 Nov 2026: **checked in full 19; checked in part 17 (not counted as checked); not opened 2.** An entry opened in part is not counted as checked.

How the Status column reads (each rule applied to the whole entry; an entry naming several documents is checked in full only if every one was):

- **exists (where checked, 23 Nov 2026), checked in full**: the entry's own record was read at Crossref (`api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>`, the DOI taken from a web search's result), or on the publisher's or issuing body's own page or document, and it gave the author or issuer, the title and the year or date. The reference is as that record gives it.
- **exists (where checked, 23 Nov 2026), checked in part**: only a search listing, a third party's page (a university repository, an index, another body's copy, a reference list) or a page that did not give the record was read; or only some of the documents an entry names were read. The cell says what was and was not opened.
- **not opened**: no page of the entry was opened; the cell says what the search returned.
- **not found**: no record of the entry was returned by the search.
