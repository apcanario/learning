# Syllabus flags: Managing an Athlete's Career

Raised by the chats that write this course's units. A flag is a contradiction between units or briefs, or a need the syllabus does not meet. A brief is never reworded by the chat that raises a flag: a flag that would change a brief is proposed and listed in that chat's reply for the Project's owner to veto (`docs/athlete-career-prompts.md` section 4, point 3, and section 5, point 7). `kit/next_prompt.py` passes every open or carried flag whose Affects cell names a unit to that unit's writer; an Affects cell names unit codes or ranges of them (`AC1 to AC15`), never "every unit".

Status: open (not yet acted on), carried (the named unit's writer was given it and its unit deals with it), resolved (the row that worked it, or a brief change, settled it), dropped (with the reason).
