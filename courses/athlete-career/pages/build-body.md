  <h2>Procedure</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>One fresh writer per unit, in writing order, up to four units in one chat. The chat stages a packet for the writer: the unit's brief from this course's Briefs page, never reworded; the sourcing standard; the fictional case register, <code>courses/athlete-career/notes/case-facts.md</code>, which agrees with Sport Psychology for Coaches' register; the seed bibliography, <code>courses/athlete-career/notes/bibliography.md</code>; and every unit of this course already filed. The course has no source units.</li>
    <li>The writer opens every reference before citing it and keeps a source log with the address opened, what was read and the date, grades every claim by AC1's key, gives every rule as its issuing body's dated text followed by the question for the reader's own body, names the conflict between the reader's roles wherever the coach or the coach's business has a stake, and reports three lists with the unit: facts it invented about the case, contradictions with a filed unit or with the brief of an unwritten one, and needs the syllabus does not meet.</li>
    <li>A fresh checker that has not seen the writing runs seven passes: every Sources entry against its publisher's, Crossref's or its issuing body's record, every cited claim against its source, the grades, the rules and the Portugal sections, safety (minors, anti-doping, real people, the roles conflict, money figures), the case, scope and tone, and the deliverable and figures against the brief and the text. Its report is filed as <code>audits/athlete-career/fresh-check-&lt;code&gt;.md</code> before any fix; findings are applied as exact replacements.</li>
    <li>Anything found wrong in a filed unit goes into the next unit's prompt and is fixed there first; anything that bears on a unit not yet written is appended to <code>courses/athlete-career/notes/syllabus-flags.md</code> and printed in that unit's prompt. A flag that would change a brief is proposed and listed in the chat's reply; a writer never rewords a brief. The case register gains the facts a unit invented, with the unit's code.</li>
    <li>The unit is filed at <code>courses/athlete-career/&lt;code&gt;-&lt;slug&gt;.md</code> with the front matter of method.md §7; this data file is updated; Academy is rebuilt, verified and published, the artifact and then GitHub Pages, before the next unit begins.</li>
    <li>A read by the Project's owner sits on a bundle boundary; starting the next writing chat is his go. There is no gate row.</li>
  </ol>
  <h2>Models</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Syllabus: Fable 5.2</strong>, as the Project sets for course design.</li>
    <li><strong>Writing: Opus 5.5</strong> for every unit. The deliverable is written in the same context as its unit.</li>
    <li><strong>Fresh check: Opus 5.5</strong>, in a separate context.</li>
    <li><strong>Not Sonnet or Haiku</strong> for units or checks.</li>
  </ul>
