# Greece 9.1: Reading Greek art: temples, sculpture, vases: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

- Euripides, *Helen* 262–3.
- Galen, *De placitis Hippocratis et Platonis* 5.
- Herodotus 1.31; 2.53; 3.57–58.
- Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 1.8.5; 1.24.5; 1.24.5–7; 5.10.6–8; 5.11; 5.17.3.
- Philo Mechanicus (Philo of Byzantium), quoted for Polykleitos' "Perfection comes about little by little through many numbers"; no locator given in the report.

## Modern works
- Barbara A. Barletta, *The Origins of the Greek Architectural Orders* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2001) (confirmed by a library review record).
- John Boardman, "The Parthenon Frieze: Another View", in U. Höckmann and A. Krug (eds.), *Festschrift für Frank Brommer* (Mainz: Philipp von Zabern, 1977) 39–49 (identified from context; not verified).

- Francis C. Penrose, *An Investigation of the Principles of Athenian Architecture* (London: Society of Dilettanti, 1851) (not verified).
- Brunilde S. Ridgway, *Roman Copies of Greek Sculpture: The Problem of the Originals* (Ann Arbor: University of Michigan Press, 1984) (not verified).
