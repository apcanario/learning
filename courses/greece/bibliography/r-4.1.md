# Greece 4.1: Causes of the war and the gathering at Aulis: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aeschylus, *Agamemnon* 114–138 (portent of the eagles and the hare); 184–247 (Iphigenia's sacrifice); 1202–1212 (Cassandra).
- Alcman fr. 21 (Theseus carries off Helen; no edition named in the report).
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Library* 2.6.4 (Priam and Hesione); 3.10.7 (Helen's egg); 3.10.9 (oath of Tyndareus); 3.12.1–3 (Trojan genealogy); 3.12.3 (Ilus and the Palladium); 3.12.5 (Hecuba's children, birth of Paris); 3.13.5 (Thetis, Prometheus); 3.13.8 (Achilles on Scyros). *Epitome* 3.2 (apple of Eris); 3.3 (Menelaus in Crete); 3.7 (Odysseus' feigned madness); 3.8 (Palamedes); 3.11–14 (size of the fleet); 3.16 (Achilles aged fifteen); 3.17 (Telephus); 3.18 (interval between the musters); 3.20 (Achilles cures Telephus); 3.21 (Iphigenia, Agamemnon's boast, Atreus' golden lamb); 3.26 (Tennes); 3.29 (Protesilaus); 3.32 (Troilus).
- Aristophanes, *Acharnians* (parody of Euripides' *Telephus*; no lines given).
- Athenaeus, *Deipnosophistae* 8.334b–d (quoting *Cypria* fr. 10 West).
- Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *Roman Antiquities* 1.61 (Dardanus from Corythus). The report gives only "Dionysius of Halicarnassus 1.61".
- Euripides, *Alexandros* (lost, 415 BC; no fragment numbers given); *Electra* 1280–1283 (Zeus sends the phantom); *Hecuba* 3 (Hecuba's parentage) and *Hecuba* (cited generally for Polyxena and Polydorus); *Helen* 17–21 (Leda and the swan); *Iphigenia at Aulis* 49–71 (the oath); 1532–1629 (the deer ending, disputed); *Iphigenia among the Taurians* 20–24 (Agamemnon's vow); *Palamedes* (lost; no fragments given); *Protesilaus* (lost; no fragments given); *Telephus* (lost, 438 BC; no fragments given); *Trojan Women* 924–931 (the bribes at the Judgement).
- Euphorion (cited via Pausanias 2.22.6 for Iphigenia as Helen's daughter by Theseus; no fragment given).
- Gorgias, *Encomium of Helen* (cited generally; no section numbers given).
- Hellanicus (cited with Alcman and Stesichorus for Theseus' abduction of Helen; no work or FGrH number given).
- Herodotus, *Histories* 2.113–120 (Helen in Egypt); 2.117 (the *Cypria* and Homer); 7.33 (tomb of Protesilaus at Elaeus); 9.116–120 (Artayctes and the Elaeus sanctuary).
- Hesiod, *Catalogue of Women* frr. 23a (Iphimede made immortal); 24 (Helen's Oceanid mother); 196–204 (the suitors); 204.78–85 (the oath, damaged); 204.87–92 (Achilles with Chiron); 204.95–119 (Zeus plans strife) (all numbered as in Merkelbach–West).
- Homer, *Iliad* 1.5; 1.366–369; 2.134; 2.295; 2.299–332; 2.494–759; 2.690–693; 2.695–710; 2.721–725; 3.122; 3.144; 3.173–176; 3.199; 3.205–224; 3.236–244; 3.399–412; 3.443–445; 5.265–267; 5.638–651; 6.248; 6.289–292; 7.362–364; 9.144–145; 9.252–259; 9.328–329; 9.668; 11.138–142; 11.765–790; 13.626–627; 16.140–144; 16.718; 16.866–867; 18.432–434; 19.326; 20.89–96; 20.215–241; 20.302–308; 20.407–418; 21.34–135; 21.40–41; 21.441–457; 23.746–747; 24.25–30; 24.29; 24.59–63; 24.257; 24.495–497; 24.765–766. *Odyssey* 4.12–14; 8.517; 11.298–300; 11.519–521.
- Hyginus, *Fabulae* 91 (Paris' recognition); 92 (Eris); 95 (Odysseus' madness); 96 (Achilles on Scyros); 101 (Telephus); 102 (Philoctetes); 103–104 (Protesilaus and Laodamia); 105 (Palamedes).
- Isocrates, *Helen* 64 (Stesichorus blinded).
- Lucian, *Dialogues of the Gods* 20 (the inscription on the apple); *On the Dance* 46 (Odysseus' feigned madness).
- Lucretius, *De rerum natura* 1.84–101 (Iphigenia).
- Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 12.72–145 (Cycnus); 13.162–170 (Achilles on Scyros).
- Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 1.22.6 (Polygnotus' Achilles among the maidens); 2.22.6 (Iphigenia, daughter of Helen); 3.19.11–13 (Stesichorus and Helen); 10.31.2 (death of Palamedes, citing *Cypria* fr. 27 West).
- Pindar, *Isthmian* 8.26–48 (Thetis); 8.49–50 (Telephus); *Nemean* 10.55–90 (the Dioscuri); *Paean* 8a (Paris exposed on Ida); *Pythian* 11.22–23 (Iphigenia).
- Plato, *Phaedrus* 243a (Stesichorus' Palinode, fr. 192 PMG).
- Proclus, *Chrestomathy* (summaries of the Epic Cycle, as preserved in Photius, *Bibliotheca* cod. 239 and in the Venetus A manuscript of the *Iliad*), and the Epic Cycle itself: *Cypria* (frr. 1, 10, 27 West cited), *Aethiopis*, *Little Iliad*, *Sack of Ilion*, *Returns*, *Telegony*, with the scholia to *Iliad* 1.5 (source of *Cypria* fr. 1). The standard editions are those the report names (West 2003 and 2013, below).
- Sophocles, *Electra* 157 (Iphianassa and Iphigenia); *Philoctetes* 5–11; 1326–1328.
- Statius, *Achilleid* (cited generally for Achilles on Scyros; no lines given).
- Stesichorus, Helen Palinode (fr. 192 PMG); *Oresteia* (cited for Iphigenia as Hecate); Stesichorus on Theseus and Helen (no fragment numbers given).
- Thucydides, *History* 1.9 (the oath rejected); 1.10 (the fleet's size); 1.11 (the nine years).
- Virgil, *Aeneid* 3.167–168 (Dardanus from Italy).

## Modern works

## Corpora and inscriptions
- Merkelbach–West: R. Merkelbach and M. L. West (eds), *Fragmenta Hesiodea* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1967), for the *Catalogue of Women* fragments cited above (standard edition; the report cites "M–W").
- PMG: D. L. Page (ed.), *Poetae Melici Graeci* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1962), for Stesichorus fr. 192 (the report's "PMG"). The report gives Alcman fr. 21 without naming an edition.
- West fragment numbers for the *Cypria*: as in West 2003 above.

## Not resolved
- "Most scholars since Wilamowitz prefer a second-century AD grammarian" (Proclus): no Wilamowitz work named.
- "Hesiod fr. 24 M–W", "Alcman (fr. 21)", "Hellanicus", "Euphorion": cited without edition or FGrH/PMG/SH number; only the fragment numbers the report gives are listed.
- "Apollodorus 3.12.3" and other bare "Apollodorus" locators: read as the *Library* (Bibliotheke), since the report names Epitome separately.
- "Statius, Achilleid" and "Gorgias' Encomium": no passages given.
