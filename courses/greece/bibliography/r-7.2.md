# Greece 7.2: Alexandria: science, scholarship and the new philosophies: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Apollonius of Rhodes, *Argonautica*, with Book 3 singled out (Medea).
- Archimedes, *Sand-Reckoner* 1.4–7; *On the Sphere and Cylinder*; *Measurement of a Circle*; *On Floating Bodies*; *Method*.
- Aristarchus of Samos, *On the Sizes and Distances of the Sun and Moon*.
- Aristocles, quoted in Eusebius, *Preparation for the Gospel* 14.18 (Timon's summary of Pyrrho).
- Aulus Gellius, *Attic Nights* 7.17.3.
- Callimachus, *Aetia* fr. 1 Pfeiffer (prologue, "Reply to the Telchines"); fr. 110 ("Lock of Berenice"); *Epigram* 28 Pf.; *Pinakes* (lost); *Ibis* (lost). Standard edition: R. Pfeiffer, *Callimachus*, 2 vols (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1949–53).
- Catullus 66.
- Celsus, *On Medicine*, preface 23–26.
- Cicero, *Academica* 2.99–104; *On Ends* 3; *On Fate* 22; 42–43; *On the Nature of the Gods* 2.88; *On the Republic* 1.21–22; 3 (the embassy of Carneades, via Lactantius).
- Cleomedes, *On the Heavens* (*Caelestia*) 1.7.
- Cassius Dio 42.38.
- Diogenes Laertius, *Lives of the Philosophers* 6.63; book 7 (including 7.183); book 10 (including 10.136, the three letters and the *Principal Doctrines*). Standard edition: M. Marcovich's Teubner (1999) or T. Dorandi's Cambridge edition (2013).
- Epicurus, *Letter to Menoeceus* 124–127; *Principal Doctrines* (KD) 1, 2, 4; *Letter to Herodotus*; *Letter to Pythocles*; *On Nature* (Herculaneum papyri).
- Euclid, *Elements*.
- Galen, *Commentary on Hippocrates' Epidemics* 3; *On the Doctrines of Hippocrates and Plato* 4–5.
- Hero of Alexandria, *Dioptra* 35; *Pneumatica*; *Metrica*.
- Herophilus (fragments, in von Staden: see Modern works).
- Josephus, *Jewish Antiquities* 12.11–118.
- Letter of Aristeas.
- Livy 25.31.
- Lucretius, *On the Nature of Things* 2.216–293; 3.830–1094.
- Philo, *Life of Moses* 2.25–44.
- Philodemus (the "fourfold remedy"; no work or locator given).
- Plutarch, *Caesar* 49; *Marcellus* 19; *On the Face in the Moon* 6; *On the Fortune of Alexander* 329a–b.
- Porphyry, on Aristarchus' method "clarifying Homer from Homer" (no work or locator given; see Not resolved).
- Proclus, *Commentary on Euclid I*, prologue 2.
- Ptolemy, *Almagest* 7.1–3.
- Seneca, *On Tranquillity* 9.5.
- Sextus Empiricus, *Against the Mathematicians* 7.158.
- Strabo, *Geography* 17.1.8.
- Tertullian, *On the Soul* 10.
- Theocritus, *Idylls* 1, 7, 11, 15.
- Tzetzes (the 490,000-roll figure; no work or locator given; see Not resolved).
- Also named as sources for reconstruction without locators: Stobaeus; Zeno's *Republic*; the Septuagint; Ezekiel the Tragedian.

## Modern works
- Richard Bett, *Pyrrho, His Antecedents, and His Legacy* (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2000). The report writes "Richard Bett reads Timon's summary of Pyrrho..." with no title or year (identified from context; not verified).
- Donald R. Dudley, *A History of Cynicism from Diogenes to the 6th Century A.D.* (London: Methuen, 1937). The report gives "Dudley (1937)" only (identified from context; not verified).
- Tony Freeth, Yanis Bitsakis, Xenophon Moussas and others, "Decoding the ancient Greek astronomical calculator known as the Antikythera Mechanism", *Nature* 444 (2006) 587–591 (identified from context from "Freeth and colleagues, Nature 2006"; pages not verified).
- Tony Freeth, Alexander Jones, John M. Steele and Yanis Bitsakis, "Calendars with Olympiad display and eclipse prediction on the Antikythera Mechanism", *Nature* 454 (2008) 614–617 (identified from context; title and volume confirmed by a catalogue record, pages not verified).
- Tony Freeth and Alexander Jones, "The Cosmos in the Antikythera Mechanism", ISAW Papers 4 (New York: Institute for the Study of the Ancient World, 2012). The report gives "Freeth and Jones 2012". Title and series confirmed by search.
- Tony Freeth, Graeme Higgon, Aris Dacanalis, Lindsay MacDonald, Myrto Georgakopoulou and Adam Wojcik, "A Model of the Cosmos in the ancient Greek Antikythera Mechanism", *Scientific Reports* 11 (2021), article 5821 (identified from context: the report names only "the 2021 reconstruction of a full planetary display"; title and journal confirmed by search, article number not verified).
- Mary Lefkowitz, *The Lives of the Greek Poets* (London: Duckworth; Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press, 1981). (not verified)
- A. A. Long and D. N. Sedley, *The Hellenistic Philosophers*, 2 vols (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1987). The report cites only "Long and Sedley" for the idealist reading of Epicurus' gods (identified from context; not verified).
- Heinrich von Staden, *Herophilus: The Art of Medicine in Early Alexandria* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1989). The report cites "fragments in von Staden" (identified from context; not verified).
- Martin L. West, *Studies in the Text and Transmission of the Iliad* (Munich and Leipzig: K. G. Saur, 2001). (publisher not verified)

## Corpora and inscriptions
- P.Oxy. 1241: *The Oxyrhynchus Papyri*, ed. B. P. Grenfell and A. S. Hunt (London: Egypt Exploration Society), the list of Alexandrian librarians; volume 10 (1914) (volume not verified).
- Callimachus fr. 1, fr. 110 and *Epigram* 28: numbering of R. Pfeiffer (1949–53).
- KD: Epicurus, *Kyriai Doxai* (*Principal Doctrines*), numbered as in Diogenes Laertius 10.
- Rosetta Stone (196 BC), named without edition.
- Venetus A (Marcianus gr. Z. 454), tenth-century manuscript of the *Iliad* with scholia; Qumran fragment 4QJer-b (agrees with the Greek Jeremiah). Both named without further reference.
