## Not resolved
- "Habicht argued the cities offered cult spontaneously": no work or year given. Probably C. Habicht, *Gottmenschentum und griechische Städte* (Munich: Beck, 1956), but this is a guess from context and not verified.
- "Eratosthenes" (Olympias' secret; Nysa): cited only via Arrian 5.3.1–4 and Plutarch; no fragment given.
- "Ptolemy" and "Aristobulus" on the snakes, ravens and Callisthenes' death: cited only through Arrian (FGrH 138 and 139 not named by the report).
- "Chares of Mytilene" (proskynesis): cited via Plutarch 54.3–6 and Arrian 4.12.3–5 only; no fragment number.
- "Hyperides, Against Demosthenes" and "Duris": cited by name without passage.
- Plutarch passages written as bare numbers in the report (for example "Plutarch 33.1", "27") are taken to be the *Alexander*.
