# Greece 10.8: The other Greeks at Troy: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aeneas Tacticus, 31.24 (the Locrian maidens).
- Aeschylus, *Agamemnon* 104–257; *Myrmidons* (cited for Achilles and Patroclus as lovers; no lines given); lost *Philoctetes* (via Dio, below).
- [Apollodorus], *Epitome* 6.9; 6.10; *Library* 1.8.6.
- [Aristotle], *On Marvellous Things Heard* 107.
- Cypria, *Little Iliad*, *Aethiopis*, *Iliupersis*: cited through Proclus' summaries (Proclus, *Chrestomathy*); no editor named. Hesiod, *Melampodia*, as cited by Strabo 14.1.27.
- Dio Chrysostom, *Oration* 52.
- Euripides, *Andromache* 1085–165; 1243–9; *Helen* 87–150; *Trojan Women* (cited generally).
- Herodotus, *Histories* 5.66; 5.94–5; 6.35; 6.61; 7.159–61; 8.64; 8.121.

- Horace, *Odes* 1.7.21–32.
- Isocrates, *Evagoras* 18.
- Lycophron, *Alexandra* 1141–73, with scholia (including the scholiast citing Mimnermus); Tzetzes on Lycophron 1268 (quoting the *Little Iliad*).

## Modern works
- Malkin, Irad, *The Returns of Odysseus: Colonization and Ethnicity* (Berkeley and Los Angeles: University of California Press, 1998).

## Corpora and inscriptions
- *Inscriptiones Graecae* IX 1² 3, 706 (Locrian inscription, early third century BC); the report cites it as "IG IX 1² 3, 706".

## Not resolved
- "Kakridis and Schadewaldt" (neoanalysis; Patroclus plot modelled on Memnon–Antilochus): named without titles or years. Works not identified.
- "Carl Blegen" (Pylos excavation from 1939; Palace of Nestor): no publication cited.
- The Pithekoussai "Nestor's cup" (grave no. 168, c. 730–720 BC): the inscription and grave are named but no publication or corpus number is given in the report (the standard publication would be Buchner and Ridgway, *Pithekoussai I*, not cited by the report; not verified).
- Timaeus (who "collected" the western nostoi), Mimnermus (via a scholiast on Lycophron) and the Heneti sacrifice: cited without fragment numbers.
