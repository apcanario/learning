# Greece 3.2: Heracles: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Library* 1.6.1–2 (Gigantomachy); 1.9.19 (Heracles and the Argo; Herodorus, Demaratus); 2.4.6; 2.4.8; 2.4.9; 2.4.12; 2.5.1–12 (the labours; 2.5.4 Chiron and Prometheus; 2.5.11 Hydra count, apples, Antaeus, Busiris; 2.5.12 Eleusis); 2.6.1; 2.6.1–2 (Iphitus); 2.6.3 (Omphale); 2.6.4 (Troy); 2.7.3 (Hippocoon); 2.7.6 (Nessus); 2.7.7 (Oechalia, Deianira, pyre, Hebe); 2.8.1; 2.8.2–5 (Heracleidae, the Return). Loeb (Frazer) is the standard edition.
- Apollonius of Rhodes, *Argonautica* 1.1207–1357 (Hylas).
- Arrian, *Anabasis* 2.15.7–16.8 (Tyre); 4.28.1–4 (Aornos).
- *Catasterisms* (Pseudo-Eratosthenes), cited for the Milky Way, no passage given.
- Diodorus Siculus 4.9.6; 4.10.1; 4.11.1–2; 4.11–26; 4.14.3; 4.18.5; 4.27.2–3; 4.37.3–4; 4.38.3; 4.38.4–5; 4.39.1; 4.39.2; 4.57–58.
- Euripides, *Heracles* 394–399; 822–873 (Iris and Lyssa); 1322–1337 (Theseus' offer). Euripides, *Children of Heracles* (no lines given).
- Herodotus, *Histories* 1.7; 1.60 (Peisistratus and the false Athena); 2.43–44 (two Heracleses); 2.44; 2.45 (Busiris); 5.22 (Alexander I at Olympia); 5.72; 6.108; 6.116; 7.193; 7.204 (Leonidas' pedigree); 8.131 (Leotychides' pedigree); 8.137–139; 9.26; 9.27.
- Hesiod, *Theogony* 287–294 (Geryon); 313–318 (Hydra); 526–534 (Prometheus' eagle); 950–955 (Heracles and Hebe). Hesiod (Pseudo-), *Shield of Heracles* 1–56.
- Homer, *Iliad* 2.730 (Oechalia); 5.638–642; 11.690–693; 14.249–256; 18.117–119; 19.95–133. Homer, *Odyssey* 11.601–627 (Heracles' shade and the immortal); 21.22–30 (Iphitus).
- Lucian, *Dialogues of the Gods* 13.
- Ovid, *Heroides* 9.
- Panyassis, *Heracleia*, and Peisander of Camirus, *Heracleia*: named as the lost epics that fixed the saga; no fragment numbers given.
- Pausanias 2.37.4 (Hydra's heads); 3.1.5–6; 4.2.2–3 (Oechalia).
- Pherecydes of Athens, cited via Apollodorus for the serpents, the apples and the number of children; no fragment number given.
- Pindar, *Isthmian* 4.52–55 (Antaeus); 4.63–64. Pindar, *Nemean* 1 (1.33–72, serpents; 1.67–69, Gigantomachy); 3.21–23; 3.22 (heros theos). Pindar, *Olympian* 3.26–32 (Ceryneian hind); 3.43–45; 10.24–59.
- Plutarch, *Theseus* 35; 35.2–3.
- Sophocles, *Philoctetes* 801–803. Sophocles, *Women of Trachis* 9–27; 74, 237; 248–253; 270–273; 572–577; 695–704; 930–931; 1099–1100.
- Theocritus, *Idyll* 17.26–27; 24 (the infant Heracles).
- Thucydides 1.12.3.
- Tyrtaeus, fr. 2 West.
- Herodorus and Demaratus are cited only through Apollodorus 1.9.19.
