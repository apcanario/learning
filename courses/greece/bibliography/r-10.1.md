# Greece 10.1: Achilles: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

Verification note: web search was available for part of the session only. Items marked "(partly verified)" had their journal or series and title confirmed by a search hit but not volume, pages or publisher; everything else completed from memory is marked "(not verified)".
