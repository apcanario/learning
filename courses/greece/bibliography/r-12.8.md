## Not resolved
- "Zeller emended 'for this reason' to 'because'" (Stopper 1983 defended the change): no work by Zeller named.
- "Aenesidemus, Pyrrhonian Discourses": lost; known through Photius, *Bibliotheca* 212.
- "Philo of Larissa, Roman Books": lost; recovered from Cicero, *Academica* and one sentence of Sextus, as the report says.
- "Cameron and Watts" on the closure of the school in 529: the dispute is pointed to module 7.4; only Watts (*Hypatia*, 2017) is cited here, and Cameron is not cited as a work.
- "Paul the Persian dedicated a treatise on Aristotle's logic to him": no edition or passage given.
- "Olympiodorus still lecturing after 565" and "Ammonius son of Hermias": no works or passages given.
