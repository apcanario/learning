# Greece 6.3: The Peloponnesian War: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aristophanes, *Acharnians* 515–539; *Peace* 605–611 (the Megarian Decree as cause).
- Aristotle (attributed), *Constitution of the Athenians* 29–33 (the constitutional documents of 411); 34–35; 35.4 (the dead under the Thirty).
- Diodorus Siculus 13.19 (the Syracusan debate); 13.101–103 (Arginusae, following Ephorus); 13.105–106 (Aegospotami).
- Lysias 12.71–76 (*Against Eratosthenes*, on Theramenes).
- Marcellinus, *Life of Thucydides*: cited for the claim that Cleon secured Thucydides' exile; no section number given.
- Plato, *Apology* 32b (Socrates and the Arginusae motion).
- Plutarch, *Lysander* 11; 13 (the Athenian prisoners at Aegospotami); *Nicias* 19–28.
- Thucydides: 1.20–22 (method), with 1.22.1 and 1.22.4; 1.23.6 (the truest cause); 1.24–55 (Corcyra); 1.56–65 (Potidaea); 1.67.4; 1.68–71; 1.76.2; 1.80–85; 1.86; 1.88; 1.89–118; 1.118.2; 1.139.1–2; 1.140–144; 2.2–6; 2.13; 2.35–46 (Funeral Oration), with 2.37, 2.43.1, 2.43.3; 2.47–54 (plague); 2.60–64; 2.65, with 2.65.11; 3.2–6; 3.27–28; 3.36.6; 3.37–48 (Mytilenean Debate), with 3.42–48, 3.45; 3.49; 3.50 (3.50.1); 3.52–68; 3.82–83 (stasis), with 3.82.2; 3.87; 4.2–6; 4.8–23; 4.17–22; 4.27–28; 4.29–41; 4.38–40; 4.78–88; 4.102–108; 5.6–11; 5.16; 5.16–19; 5.18–19; 5.23; 5.26; 5.27–48; 5.43; 5.44–46; 5.64–74; 5.84–116 (Melian Dialogue), with 5.89, 5.103–105, 5.105, 5.116, 5.116.4; 6.1; 6.6; 6.9–14; 6.15; 6.16–18; 6.20–26; 6.27–29; 6.43; 6.46; 6.47–49; 6.53; 6.61; 6.88–93; 6.96–103; 7.1–7; 7.8–16; 7.19; 7.27–28; 7.42; 7.43–45; 7.47–49; 7.50; 7.69–71; 7.75–85; 7.86–87; 8.18; 8.37; 8.45–56; 8.47–54; 8.58; 8.63–70; 8.66; 8.73–82; 8.86; 8.89–96; 8.97.2; 8.104–109.
