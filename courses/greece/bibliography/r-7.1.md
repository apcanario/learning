# Greece 7.1: The Successors and the kingdoms: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aelian, *Varia Historia* 2.20 (Antigonus Gonatas: kingship as "a noble servitude").
- Appian, *Syriaca* (*Syrian Wars*) 55; 57; 62–64.
- Arrian, *Anabasis* 7.26. Arrian, *Events after Alexander* (*Ta meta Alexandron*), cited through Photius' summary; no locator given in the report.
- Athenaeus, *Deipnosophistae* 6.253e (hymn for Demetrius, 290; the report cites 6.253 and 6.253e).
- Curtius Rufus, *History of Alexander* 10.5; 10.6–10.
- Diodorus Siculus, *Library of History* 17.117; 18.2–4; 18.4; 18.9–18; 18.26–28; 18.30–32; 18.33–36; 18.39; 18.55–56; 18.60–61; 19.11; 19.26–44; 19.35–36; 19.49–51; 19.55–57; 19.80–85; 19.105; 20.20; 20.28; 20.37; 20.46; 20.47–52; 20.81–99; 20.100. The report treats books 18–20 as derived largely from Hieronymus of Cardia.
- Duris of Samos, cited in Plutarch, *Lysander* 18 (Samos honours Lysander).
- Hypereides, *Against Demosthenes* 31.
- Josephus, *Jewish Antiquities* 12.237–264.
- Justin (Epitome of Pompeius Trogus), 13.2–4; 15.4; 17.1–2; 41.1–6; 41.4.
- Livy 38.38; 44.40–42.
- Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 1.8.6.
- Pliny the Elder, *Natural History* 34.41.
- Plutarch, *Aemilius Paullus* 16–22. *Agis* 5–20. *Aratus* 18–24. *Cleomenes* 7–11. *Demetrius* 10–13; 17–18; 28; 28–29; 29; 36–37; 38; 42; 44–52. *Eumenes* 5–7; 13; 16–19. *Lysander* 18. *Precepts of Statecraft* (*Praecepta gerendae reipublicae*) 821d. *Pyrrhus* 34. *On Isis and Osiris* 28.
- Polybius, *Histories* 2.37–44; 2.56–63; 5.27–29; 5.35–39; 5.65; 5.67; 5.88–90; 5.104; 5.107; 7.9; 10.27–31; 10.49; 11.34; 18.11; 18.18–27; 21.42; 26.1; 29.27; 30.25; 30.31; 34.14 (via Strabo 17.1.12). Standard edition: Loeb, trans. W. R. Paton, rev. F. W. Walbank and C. Habicht (2010–12).
- Strabo, *Geography* 11.9.2–3; 11.11.1; 13.4.2; 15.2.9; 17.1.8; 17.1.12.
- Tacitus, *Histories* 5.8.
- Theocritus, *Idyll* 17.
- Memnon of Heraclea, *On Heraclea*, FGrH 434 F 5–8 (see Corpora).
- Hellenistic Jewish and biblical texts cited: 1 Maccabees 1; 13.41. 2 Maccabees 4–6. Daniel 11.31.
- Other ancient texts named without locator: the Letter of Aristeas (on the Museum and Library); the Pali *Milindapanha* (Menander I and the monk); the Suda, entry *basileia* (see Not resolved); the treatises *On Kingship* (unspecified).

## Modern works
- A. K. Narain, *The Indo-Greeks* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1957). Title, place, publisher and year confirmed through a review record in *Classical Review*.
- W. W. Tarn, *The Greeks in Bactria and India* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1938). The report writes the title as *Greeks in Bactria and India*; the published title carries "The". Date and publisher confirmed through catalogue listings found in search; a second edition appeared in 1951.

## Corpora and inscriptions
- BCHP 3: the Diadochi Chronicle, in *Babylonian Chronicles of the Hellenistic Period* (BCHP), ed. I. Finkel and R. J. van der Spek. (not verified; the report gives only "BCHP 3")
- FGrH 434: Memnon of Heraclea, in F. Jacoby, *Die Fragmente der griechischen Historiker*, F 5–8.
- Named monuments cited without edition: the Satrap Stele of 311; the Rosetta Stone (196); the Zenon archive (Fayum); the Besnagar pillar of Heliodorus (c. 110); the Ai Khanoum finds (Clearchus of Soli's copy of Delphic maxims). The report gives no corpus number for any of them.
