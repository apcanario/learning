# Greece 2.4: Prometheus, Pandora, the Five Ages and the Flood: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aeschylus (attributed), *Prometheus Bound* 231–233; 250; 506. *Prometheus Unbound* (lost), cited for its sequel plot only.
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Library* 1.7.1; 1.7.2; 1.7.3; 2.5.11.
- Atrahasis, and *Gilgamesh* Tablet XI (flood parallels); cited without lines, no edition named.
- Babrius, *Fable* 58.
- Daniel 2:31–45; Genesis 6–9.
- Euripides, *Ion* 1571–1594.
- Herodotus 1.56.
- Hesiod, *Theogony* 507–616; 521–525; 526–534; 535–560; 551; 556–557; 563; 566–567; 570–612; 585; 590–602; 603–612; 901–903. *Works and Days* 11–26; 42–105; 57–105; 80–82; 90–92; 94–99; 106–201 (109–126; 127–142; 143–155; 156–173; 174–201; 174–175); 202–212; 213–218; 225–237; 238–247; 256; 267; 276–280.
- Hesiod (attributed), *Catalogue of Women* fr. 9 Merkelbach–West (stemma of Hellen); the flood is also placed in the *Catalogue*, no fragment given.
- Homer, *Iliad* 2.684.
- Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 1.313–415.
- Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 9.31.4; 10.4.4.
- Pindar, *Olympian* 9.41–56.
- Plato, *Protagoras* 320c–322d (321d; 322a; 322d); *Timaeus* 22a–b.
- Thucydides 1.3.
- Tyrtaeus fr. 2.
- *Zand-i Wahman Yasn* (Zoroastrian); cited without chapter or edition.
