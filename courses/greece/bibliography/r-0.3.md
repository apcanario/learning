# Greece 0.3: The timeline spine, 3000 BC to AD 529: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Agathias, *Histories* 2.30–31 (Damascius, Simplicius and the journey to Persia).
- Censorinus, *De die natali* 21.
- Cicero, *De oratore* 3.137.
- Clement of Alexandria, *Stromateis* 1.21.
- *Codex Justinianus* 1.11.10.
- Eratosthenes of Cyrene, *Chronographiai* (fall of Troy at 1184/3); FGrH 241 (standard number, not given in the report).
- Ephorus (fall of Troy at 1135) and Duris of Samos (1334): reported by the report without a source passage; FGrH 70 and 76 (standard numbers, not given in the report).
- Herodotus, *Histories* 1.56; 2.53; 2.142; 2.145.
- Hesiod, *Works and Days* 654–659 (funeral games for Amphidamas).
- Hippias of Elis (victor list, c. 400 BC) and Timaeus of Tauromenium (dating by Olympiads): known only through later writers; FGrH 6 and 566 (standard numbers, not given in the report).
- John Malalas, *Chronographia*, cited for the ban on teaching philosophy at Athens in 529; no book or chapter given.
- Plato (pseudo-), *Hipparchus* 228b.
- Plutarch, *Numa* 1.4.
- Thucydides, *Histories* 1.12; 2.2; 5.20; 6.3–5.
- Xenophon, *Hellenica* (cited for the narrative to 362; no passages given).
- Apollodorus of Athens, chronological poem (second century BC): named only; FGrH 244 (standard number, not given in the report).

Pearson and others, "Annual radiocarbon record indicates 16th century BCE date for the Thera eruption", *Science Advances* 4 (2018) eaar8241 (the report gives "Pearson and others, 2018 onwards"; identified from context, and the report's phrase "onwards" implies further papers that are not identified).
- Anthony M. Snodgrass, *The Dark Age of Greece: An Archaeological Survey of the Eleventh to the Eighth Centuries BC* (Edinburgh: Edinburgh University Press, 1971).
