# Greece 1.3: The collapse of 1200 and the Dark Age: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Library* 2.8.2–5 (return of the Heracleidae).
- Diodorus Siculus 4.57–58.
- Eratosthenes (fall of Troy at 1184/3): cited as a date, no passage; see 0.3 for the sources that preserve it.
- Herodotus, *Histories* 1.142–148 (with 1.146 on the mixed settlers of Ionia); 2.53; 9.26 (Hyllus and Echemus).
- Hesiod, *Works and Days* 150–151 (bronze men and iron); 156–173 (the heroes).
- Homer, *Iliad* 2.494–759 (Catalogue of Ships); 6.168–169 (Bellerophon's tablet); 10.261–265 (boar's-tusk helmet); 11.632–637 (Nestor's cup); 23 (funeral of Patroclus); 23.826–835 (iron as a prize).
- Mimnermus, fr. 9 West (Colophon founded from Pylos); Tyrtaeus, fr. 2 West (Erineos; the Spartans and the Heracleidae). Numbering of M. L. West, *Iambi et Elegi Graeci*, as the report gives it.
- Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 2.16.5 (Cyclopean walls); 2.18.7–9; 4.3.3–8; 7.2–5.
- Strabo, *Geography* 14.1.3.
- Thucydides, *Histories* 1.12; 1.12.3.
