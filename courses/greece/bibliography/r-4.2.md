# Greece 4.2: The Iliad: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aeschylus, *Myrmidons* (lost; cited for the erotic reading of Achilles and Patroclus; no fragments given).
- Aristotle, *Poetics* 23 (Homer's restraint, contrasted with the Cyclic poets).
- Homer, *Iliad* (cited by book and line): 1.5 (*Dios boulē*); 3.125–128 (Helen weaving); 4.164–165; 5.855–861; 6.129–141; 6.146–149; 6.441–446; 6.447–449; 6.521–523; 8.69–74; 9.144–145; 9.158–161; 9.182–198 (dual verb forms); 9.381–384 (Egyptian Thebes); 9.401–409; 9.410–416; 9.413; 9.624–642; 10.261–265 (boar's-tusk helmet); 11.794–803; 12.310–328; 15.59–71; 15.69–71; 15.674–746; 16.431–461; 17.670; 18.95–96; 18.249–313; 18.478–608 (the Shield); 19.67–68; 19.74–75; 19.86–138; 19.416–417; 20.30; 21.106–113; 21.166–167; 21.462–466; 21.517; 22.66–76; 22.99–110; 22.167–185; 22.209–213; 22.358–360; 24.25–30; 24.44; 24.503–504; 24.525–533; 24.734–738; 24.761–775. The report also summarises all 24 books without locators. *Odyssey* 4.271–289; 8.492–520 (the Wooden Horse).
- Hesiod (Moirai, cited only as a comparison; no passage given).
- Plato, *Symposium* 180a (Phaedrus on Achilles and Patroclus).
- Proclus, *Chrestomathy*, for the Epic Cycle poems (*Aethiopis*, *Little Iliad*, *Sack of Ilion*), cited generally in the last section.
- Xenophon, *Symposium* 8.31.

## Modern works
- Walter Arend, *Die typischen Scenen bei Homer*, Problemata 7 (Berlin: Weidmann, 1933).
- Richard Janko, *Homer, Hesiod and the Hymns: Diachronic Development in Epic Diction* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1982). The report cites "Richard Janko's statistical study of the language (1982)".
- Albert B. Lord, *The Singer of Tales* (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1960).
- Leonard Muellner, *The Anger of Achilles: Mênis in Greek Epic* (Ithaca, NY: Cornell University Press, 1996).
- Milman Parry, *L'Épithète traditionnelle dans Homère: essai sur un problème de style homérique* and *Les Formules et la métrique d'Homère* (both Paris: Société d'édition "Les Belles Lettres", 1928) (identified from the report's "Paris theses of 1928"; not verified).
- Wolfgang Schadewaldt, *Iliasstudien*, Abhandlungen der Sächsischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, phil.-hist. Klasse 43.6 (Leipzig: Hirzel, 1938) (series number and publisher from memory; not verified).
- Cedric H. Whitman, *Homer and the Heroic Tradition* (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1958) (not verified).
- Friedrich August Wolf, *Prolegomena ad Homerum* (Halle: Libraria Orphanotrophei, 1795) (imprint from memory; not verified).
- M. L. West, *The Making of the Iliad: Disquisition and Analytical Commentary* (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2011).
