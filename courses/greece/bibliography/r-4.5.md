# Greece 4.5: The house of Atreus after Troy: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Aelian, *Varia Historia* 4.26 (Xanthus renames Laodice "Electra").
- Aeschylus, *Agamemnon* 1372–1398; 1528 (the sword). *Choephori* 164–234; 269–296; 734–765; 889; 896–898; 1048–1062. *Eumenides* 280–283; 470–489; 657–666; 681–710; 696; 704–706; 734–741; 752–753; 762–774. The *Oresteia* is also discussed as a whole, without locators.
- Antiphon 5.51 (*On the Murder of Herodes*; the equal-vote rule).
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Epitome* 6.14 (Machaereus); 6.25 (Orestes' accusers); 6.28 (Orestes' death).
- Aristotle, *Constitution of the Athenians* 25 (Ephialtes); *Problems* 29.13 (equal vote).
- Demosthenes 23.66 (the Areopagus and Ares).
- Epic Cycle, *Cypria* (as summarised by Proclus, for Iphigenia among the Taurians).
- Euripides, *Andromache* 993–1008; 1085–1165. *Electra* 86–87; 524–544; 1244; 1347–1348. *Iphigenia at Aulis* (cited generally). *Iphigenia among the Taurians* 940–986; 970–978; 1446–1467. *Orestes* 491–525; 1653–1659.
- Herodotus, *Histories* 1.67–68 (bones of Orestes); 4.103 (the Tauri).
- Hesiod, *Catalogue of Women* fr. 23a Merkelbach–West.
- Homer, *Iliad* 9.144–145. *Odyssey* 1.29–43; 3.254–312; 3.306–310; 3.309–310; 4.3–9; 4.512–537; 11.405–434; 11.421–423.
- Hyginus, *Fabulae* 122 (Erigone).
- Pausanias 1.28.5 (the Areopagus and Ares); 1.43.1 (tomb of Iphigenia at Megara); 3.16.7–11 (Artemis Orthia).
- Pindar, *Nemean* 7.34–47; *Paean* 6 (generally, and at 6.117–120); *Pythian* 11.17–37 (including 11.17 and 11.22–25).
- Simonides (cited via the scholia on Euripides' *Orestes* for the palace's location; no fragment given).
- Sophocles, *Electra* 32–37; 97–99; 680–763; 1098–1231; 1415.
- Stesichorus, *Oresteia* (frr. 210–219 PMG).
- Xanthus the lyric poet (via Aelian, above; no fragment given).
- Zenodotus (read "Phocis" for Orestes' return in the passage at *Odyssey* 3.306–310; the report gives no further locator).
