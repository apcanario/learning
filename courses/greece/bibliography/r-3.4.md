# Greece 3.4: Jason, the Argonauts and Medea: sources

Curated 27 October 2026 (Sonnet 5.5) from the report's inline citations. Edition notes are given where the report or the standard edition makes them clear; "(not verified)" marks a reference completed from memory that could not be confirmed.

## Ancient sources
- Apollodorus (Pseudo-), *Library* 1.9.1 (Athamas, Phrixus); 1.9.16 (Jason, Pelias, Polymede; the crew; also cited as 1.9.16–28 for the Argonaut narrative); 1.9.19 (Heracles' departure); 1.9.21 and 3.15.3 (Phineus); 1.9.24 (Apsyrtus); 1.9.27 (Pelias' death); 1.9.28 (Corinth, Glauce, Medus). Apollodorus, *Epitome* 1.5–6 (Medea and Theseus).
- Apollonius of Rhodes, *Argonautica* 1.5–17; 1.18–19; 1.23–233 (the catalogue); 1.47 and 1.233 (Alcimede); 1.341–349; 1.494–515; 1.609–909 (Lemnos); 1.769–773 (Atalanta); 1.936–1077 (Cyzicus); 1.1153–1362 (Hylas; also cited as 1.1207–1362); 2.1–97 (Amycus); 2.178–530 (Phineus); 2.181–184; 2.549–610 (Clashing Rocks); 2.815–898; 2.1093–1230; 2.1140–1156; 2.1144–1147; 2.1246–1259; 3.6–166; 3.401–421; 3.616–824; 3.844–866; 3.1013–1062; 3.1278–1407; 4.123–182; 4.259 (scholia, for Hesiod fr. 241); 4.410–481 (Apsyrtus); 4.580–591; 4.659–752; 4.783–832; 4.811–815; 4.891–919 and 4.891–921 (Sirens); 4.1098–1169; 4.1228–1764; 4.1638–1693 (Talos); 4.1731–1764.
- Diodorus Siculus 4.41; 4.47 (rationalised ram, after Dionysius Scytobrachion); 4.52 (Alcestis); 4.56 (return route, Medus; citing Timaeus).
- Euripides, *Medea* 663–758 (Aegeus); 1136–1230; 1334; 1378–1383 (cult at Hera Akraia); scholia to *Medea* 264 (Creophylus, Parmeniscus, the five talents). Euripides, lost *Peliades* (455 BC) and *Aegeus*. Euripides' ancient hypothesis to the *Medea* (Neophron).
- Eumelus, *Corinthiaca*, cited through Pausanias 2.3.10–11.
- Herodotus, *Histories* 7.62 (Medes named from Medea); 7.193; 7.197 (Alus, Zeus Laphystius).
- Hesiod, *Theogony* 992–1002 (Jason and Medea); 1001 (Medeius). Hesiod, fr. 241, via the scholia to Apollonius 4.259 (return route).
- Homer, *Iliad* 7.467–469 (Euneus). Homer, *Odyssey* 12.59–72, esp. 12.69–72 (Argo and the Planctae).
- Hyginus, cited for Creusa as Jason's bride; no *Fabula* number given.
- Ovid, *Metamorphoses* 7.159–293 (Aeson).
- Pausanias 2.3.6–7 (tomb of Medea's children, Deima); 2.3.10–11; 5.17.9–11 (Chest of Cypselus).
- Pindar, *Pythian* 4: 4.13–56; 4.25–27; 4.71–120; 4.156–167; 4.171–183; 4.251–254; 4.251–262.
- Plutarch, *Theseus* 12.
- Theocritus, *Idyll* 13.73–75; *Idyll* 22 (Amycus).
- Valerius Flaccus, *Argonautica* 1.22–90; 5.154–176; Books 3, 4 (Hylas) and 6 (Colchian civil war) without lines.
- Seneca, cited for Creusa; no passage given.
- Fragmentary authors reported at second hand, with no fragment numbers except where stated: Creophylus; Parmeniscus; Neophron of Sicyon; Staphylus; Simonides; Pherecydes; Hecataeus; Herodorus of Heracleia; Ibycus; *Nostoi* fr. 6; Sophocles' lost *Colchian Women* and *Phineus* plays.

## Modern works
- Denys L. Page (ed.), *Euripides: Medea* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1938). The report cites "Page's 1938 edition"; identified from context and confirmed by journal reviews (lxviii + 190 pp.).

## Corpora and inscriptions
