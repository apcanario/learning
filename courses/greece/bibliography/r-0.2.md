## Modern works
- Robert Bittlestone, with James Diggle and John Underhill, *Odysseus Unbound: The Search for Homer's Ithaca* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2005). Confirmed by catalogue records.
- John Chadwick, "Who were the Dorians?", *La Parola del Passato* 31 (1976) 103–117 (identified from context: the report gives "John Chadwick, 1976" for the view that the Dorians were already in the Peloponnese as a subject population; not verified, and the same scholar's *The Mycenaean World*, Cambridge University Press, 1976, is the other candidate).
- M. I. Finley, "The Trojan War", *Journal of Hellenic Studies* 84 (1964) 1–9 (identified from context: the report gives "Moses Finley (1964)" for the view that nothing in Homer can be used as history; the article's existence is confirmed, its pages are from memory).
- Jonathan M. Hall, *Ethnic Identity in Greek Antiquity* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1997) (identified from context: the report gives "Jonathan Hall (1997)"; the same book is cited with title in 1.3).
- Mogens Herman Hansen and Thomas Heine Nielsen (eds), *An Inventory of Archaic and Classical Poleis* (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2004).
- Peregrine Horden and Nicholas Purcell, *The Corrupting Sea: A Study of Mediterranean History* (Oxford: Blackwell, 2000).
- Joachim Latacz, *Troy and Homer: Towards a Solution of an Old Mystery*, trans. Kevan Windle and Rosh Ireland (Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2004); German original *Troia und Homer* (Munich: Koehler & Amelang, 2001) (identified from context: the report gives "Joachim Latacz (2004)").
