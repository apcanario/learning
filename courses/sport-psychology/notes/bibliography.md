# Seed bibliography: Sport Psychology for Coaches

A starting list for the writers, by unit. It is not an authority.

**What was checked, and what was not.** On 16 Nov 2026 every journal entry below was looked up at Crossref by its DOI (two by a bibliographic query): authors, title, journal, volume, issue, pages and DOI are as Crossref returned them. The two books and the two reports were found by web search on the same day. **Nobody has checked what any of these sources says.** A writer opens each reference before citing it and takes the claim from the source, never from this list or from the note beside an entry. The "Why it is here" notes say why an entry was chosen; they are leads, not findings.

Where Crossref's year is the year of first online publication and the volume is later, the entry says so; the writer takes the print year from the publisher's page.

Authors are given by family name, as Crossref returned them; the writer takes initials from the publisher's page.

Units: the codes after each entry say which units are expected to open it.
