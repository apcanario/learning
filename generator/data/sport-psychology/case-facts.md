# Case facts: Sport Psychology for Coaches

The register of the course's running case. Everything here is invented. A unit takes facts about the case only from this file, labels each athlete "(fictional)" on first use, and never uses the case as evidence for a claim about the world. A writer who needs a fact this file lacks invents it, lists it in its notes, and the filing chat appends it here with the unit's code. No fact here states a real federation's rule, age category or weight class.

First names only, so that no real person is named.

## Dani (fictional) · 14 · judo

- Girl, 14. Started judo at 8. Trains five times a week at a club; school on weekdays.
- Won a national title in her age group at 13.
- Grew 9 cm in the past twelve months. She is now at the top of the weight class she won in.
- Her coach wants her to stay in that class one more season. Dani has said once, quietly, that she wants to move up.
- Has started skipping the snack after school on training days. Nobody has asked her about it.
- Trains hard, says little, looks to her coach after every exchange.
- Both parents come to every competition. Her father stands at the edge of the mat.
- Her coach: 15 years of coaching, a former competitor, direct, raises his voice in training, proud of her.
- The squad: 18 athletes aged 11 to 17, mixed.

## Milan (fictional) · 17 · football

- Boy, 17. Central midfielder in a club academy squad. Joined the academy at 11.
- Since about 12, plays below his training level in matches: in the first fifteen minutes he hides from the ball and passes backwards.
- Feels sick on match mornings. Sleeps badly the night before.
- In training he is one of the two best passers in the squad.
- Has been told "just relax" and "play like you train" for five years.
- Asks his coach before most matches whether he is starting, and whether the coach thinks he will play well.
- A decision on whether the academy keeps him comes at the end of this season.
- His mother drives him to every match and talks through the game on the way home.
- His coach: 6 years of coaching, autonomy-minded in training, anxious and loud on the touchline.
- The squad: 20 players aged 16 to 18.

## Sara (fictional) · 20 · 400 metres

- Woman, 20. 400 m runner. University student, second year. First season as a senior.
- Eight months back from a hamstring injury that cost her a full season.
- Trains with a group of six; her coach also coaches two other groups.
- Describes herself as a perfectionist. Keeps a training log to the tenth of a second.
- For six weeks: sleeping about five hours a night, flat in sessions, has stopped staying to talk after training.
- Ran a personal best in her second race back, and has been slower in each race since.
- Lives away from home. Her parents follow results online.
- Her coach: 20 years of coaching, technical, sees her four times a week, has not asked about the sleep.
