# Mara Venn: the register of invented facts

The running example of the course Building a Personal Brand on Instagram and YouTube. Mara is fictional; any resemblance to a real person or account is unintended. Every fact below was invented for the course. A unit labels her "(Mara, fictional)" on first use, agrees with this register, and never uses her as evidence about the world. The chat that files a unit appends every fact the unit set, with its section, and lists what the unit left free. A fact a later unit must change is a fix to the earlier unit, made in the same chat.

## Set by the shared preamble (11 Nov 2026)

- Mara Venn, 38, a full-time accountant.
- Restores mid-century furniture at weekends; wants to teach restoration to people who have no workshop.
- Starts with no YouTube subscribers and 140 Instagram followers.
- Films on a phone. Has 6 hours a week.

## Set by PB1, What to expect (12 Nov 2026)

- Day one of her first year is Monday 4 January 2027 (§1, D3).
- Year-one cadence, a planning assumption: one 10-minute YouTube long-form video every two weeks (26 a year), and three Instagram feed posts a week (six a fortnight) cut from the same footage (§1, D1, D2).
- Hours: 12 a fortnight (6 a week, 312 a year), split capture 2.0, long-form edit 4.0, packaging 1.0, Instagram derivatives 3.0, publishing and replies 1.25 (1.5 until PB3), email 0.25 (PB3), log 0.5; a week over 7 hours is logged as over budget (D2). These are her planning figures, to be replaced by her logged hours.
- Money: a year-one cap of 150 USD: phone already owned; no paid editing app in year one; a clip-on microphone (99.00 USD, one product's list price on 12 Nov 2026) only if PB5's audio check fails; a tripod or clamp up to 40 USD (her estimate); 11 USD contingency (D2).
- Her stop rule: she stops, or rebuilds the system before uploading again, if she publishes fewer than 3 YouTube videos in each of two consecutive quarters; her verdicts in order: stop or rebuild; change one thing (cut the load: over 7 hours in 3 or more weeks); change the format (from Q2, click-through under 2% or percentage viewed under its band on 3 or more uploads); continue (D3).
- Her checkpoint reviews: Monday 5 April 2027, 5 July 2027, 4 October 2027 and 3 January 2028 (the twelve-month review), each with the seven reads of D3 (D3).

Left free by PB1: her country (USD is the source currency of the one price checked, not hers); her niche's sub-topics; her actual numbers in any quarter; year two's dates (PB10). PB3 may move hours between the platforms and PB4 turns them into a calendar, inside the same 12 hours a fortnight.

## Set by PB2, Positioning (12 Nov 2026)

- She restores indoors, on a table, with hand tools that fit in one box; she has no garage, shed or workshop (§1, D1, D4).
- Her pieces: mid-century chairs, side tables and small cabinets (D1).
- Each YouTube video is one piece, start to finish, with what was wrong, what she did, what went wrong and what it cost; she presents every video herself (D1, D4).
- When a job needs a workshop she says so and shows a workaround or refers it to a professional (D4).
- Positioning sentence: "Mara shows people who have no workshop how to restore a mid-century chair, table or small cabinet at home, start to finish, with tools that fit in one box." Its 60-word expansion and the audience are as in PB2's D1 (PB3 tests the audience).
- Account name: One Box Restoration; handle @oneboxrestoration on both platforms, provisional until she claims it in both apps on the same day; fallbacks @onebox.restoration, then @oneboxrestore; override: Mara Venn (@mara.venn), with One Box Restoration as the series name, if she moves beyond restoration or sells teaching under her own name (D2). None of these handles was checked for use on either platform (flag F13).
- Instagram name field "Mara · One Box Restoration"; her Instagram bio, YouTube description and keywords are as written in PB2's D3 and D4. Her job as an accountant stays out of public copy until PB8 decides.
- Repeat-back test: run 1 on the profile in the week of 7 December 2026; run 2 on one video at the Q1 review, Monday 5 April 2027 (D5).

Left free by PB2: her country, city, household and income; how long she has restored; whether her job is disclosed (PB8).

## Set by PB3, Audience and the two platforms (12 Nov 2026)

- Her audience: PB2's audience tested and kept unchanged. Profile sentence: "People who own, or want to buy, a mid-century chair, side table or small cabinet, have no workshop, garage or shed, and learn practical jobs from video; most of them can be reached on YouTube at any age, and on Instagram mainly under 50." Ten traits with basis and label, as in PB3's D1; her own audience's age, gender and country (trait 8) and what it watches (trait 9) are unknown until her own data shows them, read at each checkpoint from the Q1 review (D1).
- Platform roles: YouTube long-form is the home (one piece per video); Instagram is the shop window, mostly Reels for reach to non-followers, carousels or images for followers, the mix left to PB7; email is the list she owns (§3, D2).
- No YouTube Shorts hours at 6 hours a week; PB6 may revisit (§4, D2).
- Email: one short email per video, 0.25 hours a fortnight, on beehiiv's Free plan; nothing is collected until PB8 answers the data-protection question (§5, D2).
- Hours at 6 a week, per fortnight: capture 2.0, long-form edit 4.0, packaging 1.0, Instagram derivatives 3.0, publishing and replies 1.25, email 0.25, log 0.5 (24.0 per four weeks); posting rates unchanged from PB1 (D2).
- Alternative plans if a checkpoint changes her budget: at 3 hours a week, one video every four weeks and one Instagram post a week (11.75 hours per four weeks); at 10 hours a week, one video a week and three Instagram posts a week (39.0 hours per four weeks), or five Instagram posts a week if she cannot finish a piece weekly; on such a move PB1's posting-rate rows and stop rule are re-read that day (D2).
- Her cut order in a bad week: Instagram posts from three to one; replies deferred; the video edited to about 8 minutes; the upload moved by up to seven days, once; the log is never cut (D2).

Left free by PB3: her audience's actual demographics (her own data decides), her email list's size, her Instagram format mix (PB7).

## Left free (a unit that needs one sets it and it is appended here)

- Her country, city, household and income. PB8 works its register for the EU, the UK and the US without fixing her country unless it must.
- Her account name and handle (PB2 chooses them, after checking that the handle is not in use on either platform on the day; if it is, PB2 picks another).
- Her calendar, her numbers in any quarter, her products and prices.
