# Mara Venn: the register of invented facts

The running example of the course Building a Personal Brand on Instagram and YouTube. Mara is fictional; any resemblance to a real person or account is unintended. Every fact below was invented for the course. A unit labels her "(Mara, fictional)" on first use, agrees with this register, and never uses her as evidence about the world. The chat that files a unit appends every fact the unit set, with its section, and lists what the unit left free. A fact a later unit must change is a fix to the earlier unit, made in the same chat.

## Set by the shared preamble (11 Nov 2026)

- Mara Venn, 38, a full-time accountant.
- Restores mid-century furniture at weekends; wants to teach restoration to people who have no workshop.
- Starts with no YouTube subscribers and 140 Instagram followers.
- Films on a phone. Has 6 hours a week.

- Day one of her first year is Monday 4 January 2027 (§1, D3).

- Hours: 12 a fortnight (6 a week, 312 a year), split capture 2.0, long-form edit 4.0, packaging 1.0, Instagram derivatives 3.0, publishing and replies 1.25 (1.5 until PB3), email 0.25 (PB3), log 0.5; a week over 7 hours is logged as over budget (D2). These are her planning figures, to be replaced by her logged hours.
- Money: a year-one cap of 150 USD: phone already owned; no paid editing app in year one; a clip-on microphone (99.00 USD, one product's list price on 12 Nov 2026) only if PB5's audio check fails; a tripod or clamp up to 40 USD (her estimate); 11 USD contingency (D2).
- Her stop rule: she stops, or rebuilds the system before uploading again, if she publishes fewer than 3 YouTube videos in each of two consecutive quarters; her verdicts in order: stop or rebuild; change one thing (cut the load: over 7 hours in 3 or more weeks); change the format (from Q2, on 3 or more uploads, click-through under 2% with impressions at or below her median upload's, or percentage viewed under its band; an upload under 2% with impressions above her median is recorded and not counted; corrected 12 Nov 2026, found by the P100 correction check, flag F42); continue (D3).
- Her checkpoint reviews: Monday 5 April 2027, 5 July 2027, 4 October 2027 and 3 January 2028 (the twelve-month review), each with the seven reads of D3 (D3).

Left free by PB1: her country (USD is the source currency of the one price checked, not hers); her niche's sub-topics; her actual numbers in any quarter; year two's dates (PB10). PB3 may move hours between the platforms and PB4 turns them into a calendar, inside the same 12 hours a fortnight.
