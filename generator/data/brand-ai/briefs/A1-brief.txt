Senior Director of Product Management (UX/AI) at a PE-backed software company; ADHD; ended competitive karate after the WIKF Global Cup (Oct 2026), still trains Wado-Ryū and kumite and coaches; next journeys are a sub-1:45 half marathon in spring/summer 2027, −15 kg, and a self-taught history course; owns Shōmen, a small karate-apparel brand; publishes in English with some Portuguese; has 5–7 h/week. Tone: direct, no flattery, no filler, no motivational language, no 'not X but Y' constructions. End with the unit's deliverable, built out in full, not described.
