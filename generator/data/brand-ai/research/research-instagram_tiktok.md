# Instagram and TikTok audience growth for a solo creator (2025–2026), and whether short-form feeds long-form

Research date: 27 Oct 2026. Figures are marked with the data period wherever the source gave one. Several "2026" benchmark reports (Socialinsider) explicitly state that their "2026" columns are 2025 full-year values relabelled; this is flagged below. Note that most large benchmark datasets are **brand** accounts, not personal creator accounts — creator accounts generally engage higher (Metricool/HypeAuditor: ~3x the likes+comments of brands).

## Key Question 1 — Instagram 2025–2026: format reach to non-followers vs followers, official ranking signals, reach/engagement by follower tier

### Takeaway
Instagram's official line (Mosseri, Jan 2025) is that watch time, likes and sends are the top three ranking signals, with sends weighted more for reach to non-followers ("unconnected" content) and likes more for followers. Reels are the format that most reliably reaches non-followers at small account sizes, but carousels out-reach Reels on views per post at every tier in Socialinsider's 2025 data; Stories go to followers only. Organic engagement rates for brand accounts are ~0.45–0.5% of followers per post and falling; audience growth for 1–5K accounts fell from 38%/yr (2024) to 22%/yr (2025).

### Cited Findings

**Official ranking signals (Instagram / Mosseri)**
- Mosseri, first video in a 2025 series on ranking (reported 22 Jan 2025): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." He advises checking average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach in Insights. "Likes are slightly more important for connected content" (followers' feeds); sends matter slightly more for unconnected (non-follower) content. Comments were not mentioned in that overview. — [Social Media Today, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- Mosseri (interview on *Build Your Tribe*, reported Apr 2025): confirmed a deliberate change to distribution that increased "unconnected"/"disconnected" reach (content shown to non-followers); no percentage given. Instagram's three stated priorities: valuing original content, helping small creators break through, accelerating trend cycles. More photos/videos are now shared in DMs than in Stories. — [Music Marketing Trends newsletter, 17 Apr 2025](https://musicmarketingtrends.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-win-on-instagram-from-it-s-ceo)
- Mosseri on carousels (2024): multiple slides mean more interactions and therefore more reach on average; "the algorithm gives your Carousel a second chance after it's posted," resurfacing it to **followers** who swiped past (follower-side mechanic, not non-follower). — [Lia Haberman, ICYMI, 14 Oct 2025](https://liahaberman.substack.com/p/the-best-instagram-content-formats)
- Mosseri on posting frequency (Reel reported 6 Dec 2024): no fixed number; some creators post once a month, others five Reels a day; "posting often helps with reach" but he warns about burnout; build a sustainable schedule. — [RouteNote, 6 Dec 2024](https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/)
- Mosseri on links: saying "link in bio" in captions does not hurt reach — "You're more than welcome to say 'link in bio'"; he uses it himself. eMarketer notes many creators report lower engagement when referencing external links but cites no data, and creators' informal tests are inconclusive. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/off-platform-links-remain-tension-instagram-creators)
- Trial Reels: launched Dec 2024; wider availability confirmed by Mosseri the week before 13 Jul 2025. A trial Reel is shown only to a selection of **non-followers**; followers don't see it in Feed or Reels tab; results (views, likes, comments, shares) after ~24 h; eligibility: public account with ≥1,000 followers. Instagram claimed (Jun 2025) Trial Reels helped creators "significantly increase reach" but published no figures. — [Social Media Today, Jul 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/)
- Secondary (unverified attribution, no primary cited): claims that Mosseri said sends carry "roughly three to five times more weight than likes" for non-follower distribution, and suggested ~2 Reels + 3–5 feed posts/week. Treat as unconfirmed. Also states Feed, Reels, Stories and Explore are four separate ranking systems; Stories rank by closeness/recency; hashtags lost most weight since late 2024; Reels up to 3 min eligible for non-follower recommendation. — [eClincher, 2026](https://www.eclincher.com/articles/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works-in-2026)

**Format reach (non-followers vs followers)**
- Stories links are "at least in theory" not reach-restricted but Stories are only shown to your followers. — [Social Media Today, 25 Nov 2024](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/heres-each-big-social-platform-stand-external-links/733946/)
- Metricool × HypeAuditor "2025 Instagram Content Playbook" (≈700M posts, 28M influencer accounts, Jan–Jun 2025, medians, released 14 Oct 2025): Reels lead in reach across all follower tiers; carousels increasingly win likes/comments for ≥500K accounts. — [Metricool press release, 14 Oct 2025](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-content-playbook/)
- Same study as summarised by Haberman: under ~50K followers Reels have the highest median reach; 50K–500K carousels 9.3K vs Reels 8K; 500K–1M images 63.8K ≈ carousels 63.7K; 1M+ carousels highest. Reels were 47% of brand posts at 10K–50K. (Note the article's "under 50K" vs "10K–50K" wording is inconsistent.) — [Lia Haberman, 14 Oct 2025](https://liahaberman.substack.com/p/the-best-instagram-content-formats)
- Socialinsider Instagram benchmarks (67.5M organic **brand** posts, 1K–1M followers; "2026" = 2025 values; Q3 2026 quarterly figures also given). Average views per post by follower tier (2025): 1–5K: Reels 580 / Carousels 993 / Images 417; 5–10K: 1,000 / 2,117 / 1,068; 10–50K: 2,460 / 4,275 / 2,340; 50–100K: 6,095 / 11,597 / 7,405; 100K–1M: 16,035 / 35,370 / 22,900. (Carousels out-view Reels at every tier in this brand dataset — contrasts with Metricool's "Reels lead reach under 50K".) — [Socialinsider Instagram Benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/instagram-benchmarks)

**Engagement rates and growth by tier**
- Socialinsider engagement rate by followers ((likes+comments)/followers): Reels 0.50% (2024) → 0.52% (2025) → 0.48% (Q3 2026); Carousels 0.55% → 0.55% → 0.50%; Images 0.45% → 0.37% → 0.35%; all formats 0.48% (2025) → 0.45% (Q3 2026); overall engagement reported down ~24% YoY. Average saves per post 1–5K tier: 1 (all formats); 10–50K: Reels 7 / Carousels 8 / Images 3. Comments per post 1–5K: Reels 3 / Carousels 2 / Images 1. — [Socialinsider Instagram Benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/instagram-benchmarks)
- Socialinsider audience growth rate (annual, % of followers) by tier: 1–5K 38.0% (2024) → 22.0% (2025); 5–10K 35.0% → 20.3%; 10–50K 33.8% → 17.2%; 50–100K 30.0% → 13.6%; 100K–1M 27.0% → 11.25%. — [Socialinsider Instagram Benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/instagram-benchmarks)
- Socialinsider cross-platform (Jan 2025–Jun 2026, brands 1K–1M): Reels engagement 0.48% (2025) → 0.45% (2026); average Reels views <5K followers: 625; 5–10K: 1,182; 10–50K: 2,745. (Tier sample totals in this page don't reconcile with headline totals — treat precise values with caution.) — [Socialinsider TikTok vs Reels vs Shorts](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-reels/)
- Influencer Marketing Hub 2025 benchmark (as cited): nano-influencer engagement ~5–8% on Instagram, >10% on TikTok (creator accounts, not brands). — [ContentGrip, May 2026](https://www.contentgrip.com/influencer-marketing-rate-card/)
- Metricool/HypeAuditor: influencer posts get ~3x the likes+comments of brand accounts; best posting window 20:00 local, Wed/Fri. — [Metricool, 14 Oct 2025](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-content-playbook/)

### Inferences
- For a personal account under 10K followers, a Reel reaches on the order of 600–1,200 views and a carousel 1,000–2,000 in brand data; creator accounts likely 2–3x that. Expect a few saves and a handful of comments per post — growth comes from the occasional Reel that Instagram pushes to non-followers (sends-driven), not from steady per-post reach.
- Stories are a retention/relationship tool (followers only), not a growth tool; Reels and trial Reels are the only formats explicitly designed to reach non-followers. Carousels are the stronger per-post format for follower engagement and resurfacing.
- Growth rates for small accounts roughly halved between 2024 and 2025 across Socialinsider's data; "22%/yr at 1–5K" implies a 2K account organically adds ~440 followers/yr without a viral hit.

### Gaps
- No official Instagram figure for the share of reach that is non-follower vs follower by format; Mosseri only confirmed the direction (more unconnected reach).
- No 2025–2026 Stories reach-rate benchmark located (Rival IQ's Stories page is 2023 and illustrative only: a 10% reach example, 26% exit rate for one frame).
- Benchmark reach **rates** (reach/followers) for creator accounts by tier were not found; only views per post (brands) and engagement rates.

## Key Question 2 — TikTok 2025–2026: For You distribution for new creators, realistic growth curve, regulatory status (EU/Portugal and US)

### Takeaway
TikTok's only official explanation (2020, still the live newsroom page) says follower count and past hits are "not direct factors" and the feed deliberately injects diverse videos, so small creators get tested; measured 2025 data shows 1–5K TikTok accounts average ~350 views per video but grow followers ~150%/yr — vastly faster than Instagram's 22%. The US ban is resolved (TikTok USDS joint venture closed 23 Jan 2026; ByteDance keeps 19.9%). In the EU, TikTok is not banned but faces DSA preliminary findings on addictive design (6 Feb 2026, up to 6% global turnover fine) and researcher data access (Oct 2025); Portugal is not an eligible country for the Creator Rewards Program.

### Cited Findings

**Official distribution mechanics**
- TikTok newsroom (18 Jun 2020; still the canonical page): For You ranks on user interactions (likes, shares, follows, comments, content created), video information (captions, sounds, hashtags) and device/account settings (lower weight). Strong signals such as finishing a longer video count more than weak ones. "Neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors in the recommendation system." The feed deliberately includes diverse videos, including some not matching stated interests or with few likes, giving "additional opportunities" to discover new creators. Just-uploaded or under-review videos may be ineligible for recommendation. — [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/)
- Third-party (not confirmed by TikTok): the widely repeated "new video shown first to a small follower subset, then widened on completion/saves/shares" model is attributed to community testing; SocialPilot explicitly notes TikTok hasn't confirmed it. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/de/blog/tiktok-algorithm)

**Measured growth and reach by tier (brands, 1K–1M followers; 40.7M organic videos; 2025 values labelled "2026"; published 22 Apr 2026, updated 22 Oct 2026)**
- Average views per video: 1–5K: 860 (2024) → 350 (2025); 5–10K: 1,575 → 945; 10–50K: 3,655 → 3,240; 50–100K: 8,688 → 9,900; 100K–1M: 25,198 → 34,900. Platform-wide views down 23% YoY. — [Socialinsider TikTok benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-engagement-rate-study/)
- Engagement rate by views: 1–5K 4.40% (2025); 10–50K 3.90%; 100K–1M 3.95%; H1 2026 3.85%, Q3 2026 3.90%. Engagement rate by followers (cross-platform page): TikTok 3.70% (2025) → 2.60% (2026) vs Reels 0.45% and Shorts 0.30%. — [Socialinsider TikTok](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-engagement-rate-study/); [Socialinsider cross-platform](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-reels/)
- Follower growth rate (% of starting followers per year): 1–5K 269% (2024) → 150% (2025); 5–10K 166% → 115%; 10–50K 86% → 60%; 50–100K 55% → 45%; 100K–1M 33% → 30%; average decline 33%. — [Socialinsider TikTok benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-engagement-rate-study/)
- Posting frequency (videos/month): 1–5K 6 (2024) → 8 (2025); 5–10K 7 → 12; 10–50K 9 → 16; 100K–1M 15 → 23 (average +40%). Shares per post 1–5K: 40; 10–50K: 120. — [Socialinsider TikTok benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-engagement-rate-study/)
- Shorts views per video by tier are far higher than TikTok or Reels at small sizes: <5K followers: Shorts 15,160 vs TikTok 350 vs Reels 625; 10–50K: 23,200 / 3,240 / 2,745. — [Socialinsider cross-platform](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-reels/)
- Unsourced calculator-site benchmarks (Feb 2026; no dataset cited; arithmetic extrapolation): "healthy" organic TikTok growth 5–15%/month; 0–1K accounts gain 30–150 followers/month; 0→1K takes ~7–33 months. Weak evidence, included only because no better small-account curve was found. — [TT Calculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/follower-growth-rate-normal/)
- Pew (18 Feb 2025, US): creator accounts are ~52% of accounts US TikTok users follow; of those creators (≥5K followers), 42% have 50K–499,999 followers, 10% >1M, 11% 5K–9,999. — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/?p=199986)
- Click Analytic (May 2026, FY2025 snapshot): 15.8M TikTok creator accounts with ≥10K followers vs 5.9M Instagram and 1.9M YouTube — TikTok has 2.68x Instagram's count of ≥10K creators (more competition at that tier). Only 6.1% of TikTok creators link an Instagram account and 2.7% a YouTube account on their profile. — [Click Analytic](https://www.clickanalytic.com/creator-economy-report/tiktok/)

**Regulatory status — US**
- Timeline: 15 Sep 2025 deal framework; 16 Sep 2025 fourth enforcement extension to 16 Dec 2025; 25 Sep 2025 executive order; 18 Dec 2025 binding agreements signed; **23 Jan 2026 deal closed, TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC established**. Silver Lake, Oracle and MGX each 15%; ByteDance retains 19.9%; seven-member majority-American board; JV controls algorithm security, data protection, content moderation; ~$14B valuation (Vance). App "no longer at risk of being banned." — [Tom's Guide, Jan 2026](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/online-security/what-will-happen-if-tiktok-is-banned)
- vidIQ (2026) reports technical issues, censorship claims and a spike in uninstalls after the US ownership change — reports, not measured data. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/tiktok-to-youtube/)

**Regulatory status — EU (applies to Portugal)**
- DSA investigation opened Feb 2024 (first DSA probe against TikTok). Oct 2025: Commission accused TikTok of breaching researcher data-access obligations. TikTok has since committed to providing ad repositories. **6 Feb 2026: preliminary findings that infinite scroll, autoplay, push notifications and personalised recommendations constitute addictive design and TikTok failed to assess/mitigate risks, including to minors.** No fine imposed yet; if confirmed, up to 6% of worldwide annual turnover; Commission says TikTok must change the "basic design of its service" in Europe (possible phase-out of infinite scroll, overnight screen breaks, adapted recommender). TikTok called the findings "categorically false and entirely meritless" and will challenge them "through every means available." — [Crowell & Moring, 9 Feb 2026](https://www.crowell.com/a/web/xjuaM4g6zeeVU5v229RCpe/tiktok-to-challenge-categorically-false-dsa-addictive-design-findings.pdf); [Malay Mail/AFP, 6 Feb 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2026/02/06/eu-tells-tiktok-to-fix-addictive-design-after-finding-risk-to-young-users/208300)
- Separate Dec 2024 probe into Romanian election interference. — [Malay Mail/AFP, 6 Feb 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2026/02/06/eu-tells-tiktok-to-fix-addictive-design-after-finding-risk-to-young-users/208300)
- Portugal usage: >4.2M TikTok users, "almost half" of the population (Sábado, 2 Oct 2025, citing a TikTok report; underlying source not named). — [Sábado, 2 Oct 2025](https://www.sabado.pt/dinheiro/amp/quase-metade-dos-portugueses-tem-conta-no-tiktok)
- Global context: TikTok 1.59B ad-audience users (early 2025), 55.7% male; men 25–34 = 20.7% of ad audience; Instagram 1.74B ad users (+5.5% YoY), ages 25–34 = 33%. — [Socialinsider cross-platform](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-reels/)

### Inferences
- TikTok's small-account arithmetic: ~350 views/video and 150%/yr follower growth at 1–5K means a 2K account can plausibly reach ~5K in a year posting ~8–12 videos/month; the same account on Instagram would add ~440. TikTok is the faster follower engine, but each view is worth less (engagement by followers fell from 3.7% to 2.6%).
- Regulatory risk for an EU-based creator is product change (forced redesign of the recommender/infinite scroll in Europe), not shutdown. For a US-facing audience, the ban risk is gone as of Jan 2026, but the US app is now a separate corporate entity whose algorithm security sits with the JV.
- Portugal is excluded from TikTok's main creator payout programme and from Instagram Gifts (see KQ6), so platform-native monetisation is weaker in Portugal than in DE/FR/UK/US.

### Gaps
- TikTok has published no post-2020 technical explanation of For You ranking; everything newer is reverse-engineered or anecdotal. The "first-batch test" mechanism is unconfirmed.
- No reliable dataset on time-to-1K/10K for **new** TikTok accounts; the Socialinsider data starts at 1K followers and covers brands.
- Could not find a 2026 DataReportal figure for Instagram/TikTok ad reach in Portugal (Statista pages behind paywall).
- No source on whether the US JV algorithm has diverged from the global one for EU creators' reach into the US.

## Key Question 3 — Cross-platform funnelling: do short-form followers convert to YouTube long-form subscribers or newsletter readers?

### Takeaway
The only large dataset found (AIR Media-Tech, 18,000 YouTube channels, Mar 2025–Mar 2026) is correlational and niche-dependent: a 25–40% Shorts mix correlates with +31–41% median subscribers in fitness, food, business and education at 100K–1M, but long-only channels lead in gaming (+85%), beauty (+45–52%) and science/tech, and channels that abruptly changed their Shorts mix did worst. No source gives a measured TikTok→YouTube or Reels→YouTube conversion rate; the one Shorts→long-form figure cited (0.04%→0.5%) is unsourced.

### Cited Findings
- AIR Media-Tech study (18,000 English-language channels, ≥40 videos incl. ≥10 long-form, 11 niches, 4 size segments, median subscribers; updated 28 May 2026). "Mix" (10–70% Shorts) vs "Long-Only" (<10%): Fitness mix +41% (100K–1M: 120,000 vs 84,900); Food +31% at smaller sizes but Long-Only +47% at 10M+; Business mix +39% (106,000 vs 76,350); Education mix +37% small, +12% mid; Gaming Long-Only +85% (70,100 vs 37,850); Beauty Long-Only +45–52%; Science/tech Long-Only +16% mid; Entertainment within 2% at small, Long-Only +17% to +90% at scale; Home/DIY <1% difference. Suggested optimum 25–40% Shorts (~1–2 Shorts/week alongside 3–5 long-form); above ~55% Shorts "long-form performance drops" (no data shown). Transitional channels (1,247 that shifted Shorts share >30 pts) had lower medians than both groups (e.g. Food 100K–1M: 46,750 vs 104,000 mix). Authors state it is correlational, subscribers are a proxy, quality is uncontrolled, and some high-tier cells rest on 15–35 channels. The article also cites a Shorts-to-long-form conversion improving from 0.04% to 0.5% with no source. — [AIR Media-Tech, May 2026](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- Metricool 2026 YouTube study (799,718 videos, 71,177 US accounts; reported 3 Aug 2026): Shorts feed = 61% of all measured views, Shorts traffic roughly doubled YoY; long-form views +76% YoY, watch time +11%, long-form engagement −45%; Shorts average view duration "fallen dramatically" (no figure); recommends 2–4 videos/week for max long-form views. — [Tubefilter, 3 Aug 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/03/youtube-shorts-long-form-study-metricool/)
- vidIQ (2026): no conversion data; qualitative reasons TikTok→YouTube is hard — mismatched intent ("show me something interesting" vs a repeatable channel promise), friction of any extra step, no destination (Shorts without a "watch next"), inconsistent branding, weak asks; TikTok interest "generally dies off in 48 hours" whereas YouTube videos keep being discovered via search. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/tiktok-to-youtube/)
- Shortimize (Jan 2025): no quantitative Shorts→subscriber rate; only advises measuring "percentage of viewers who become subscribers." — [Shortimize](https://shortimize.com/blog/converting-shorts-into-subscribers-analyzing-youtube-shorts-for-channel-growth)
- Cross-linking is rare in practice: only 2.7% of TikTok creators (≥10K) link a YouTube account on their profile and 6.1% an Instagram account (FY2025). — [Click Analytic, May 2026](https://www.clickanalytic.com/creator-economy-report/tiktok/)
- Socialinsider: Shorts deliver 15,160 views/video at <5K subscribers vs 350 on TikTok and 625 on Reels — YouTube's own short-form surface is the most generous to small accounts in this brand dataset. — [Socialinsider cross-platform](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-vs-instagram-reels/)

### Inferences
- If the goal is a YouTube long-form audience, the evidence favours posting short-form **on YouTube itself** (Shorts, 25–40% of uploads) over trying to funnel from TikTok/Instagram, where cross-links are structurally weak and no conversion rate has been measured. Whether Shorts help depends on niche: fitness/education/business benefit; gaming/beauty/tech do not.
- Abrupt pivots in format mix are associated with worse outcomes than either steady strategy — consistency matters more than the specific mix.
- Newsletter conversion from social could not be quantified from sources found; the Kit/Nathan Barry article redirected and returned no content.

### Gaps
- No measured TikTok→YouTube-subscriber or Reels→YouTube conversion rate from any platform, analyst or creator case study with numbers.
- No newsletter (Kit/ConvertKit, beehiiv, Substack) data on share of subscribers sourced from Instagram/TikTok was retrieved.
- AIR study measures subscribers, not long-form views/watch time; its case-study view figures are single channels.

## Key Question 4 — Posting frequency evidence (Instagram, TikTok) and hours cost

### Takeaway
Buffer's 2.1M-post Instagram study (Aug 2025) shows reach per post rising ~12% at 3–5 posts/week and ~24% at 10+ vs 1–2, and weekly follower growth of +0.26%/week at 3–5 posts vs +0.12% at 1–2 — i.e. frequency compounds, with the biggest marginal gain between 1–2 and 3–5 posts/week. TikTok brand accounts raised output 40% in 2025 (1–5K tier: 8 videos/month) while per-video views fell 23%. No source quantifies hours per post.

### Cited Findings
- Buffer (published 13 Aug 2025; ~2.1M feed posts from ~102,000 accounts; Stories excluded; fixed-effects model): reach per post vs 1–2/week: 3–5 ≈ +12%, 6–9 ≈ +18%, 10+ ≈ +24%. Weekly follower growth: 1–2 posts +0.12%/wk; 3–5 +0.26% (2.2x); 6–9 +0.44% (3.7x); 10+ +0.66% (5.5x). Z-score: no posts −0.08 ("no-post penalty"), 3–5 +0.07, 10+ +0.14. Illustrative 5K account: 1–2 posts ≈ +6 followers/wk, 3–5 ≈ +13, 6–9 ≈ +22, 10+ ≈ +33. Recommendation: 3–5/week sustainable; 6–9+ only if quality holds; not size-segmented. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/); [Social Media Today summary, 13 Aug 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/study-shows-posting-more-instagram-leads-to-more-reach/757633/)
- Metricool/HypeAuditor (Jan–Jun 2025, 700M posts): posting 2–3x/week associated with ~19% growth, 10+/week with ~79% (period not specified). — [Metricool, 14 Oct 2025](https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-content-playbook/)
- Socialinsider Instagram average posts/month (all tiers, brands): Reels 6 (2024) → 8 (2025); carousels 4 → 5; images 10 → 7. — [Socialinsider Instagram](https://www.socialinsider.io/instagram-benchmarks)
- Socialinsider TikTok posts/month by tier (2025): 1–5K 8; 5–10K 12; 10–50K 16; 50–100K 18; 100K–1M 23 (+40% YoY) while views/video fell 23%. — [Socialinsider TikTok](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-engagement-rate-study/)
- Mosseri: no prescribed cadence; more posting → more reach, subject to burnout. — [RouteNote, Dec 2024](https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/)
- YouTube long-form: Metricool recommends 2–4 videos/week for maximum long-form views (US data, 2026). — [Tubefilter, 3 Aug 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/03/youtube-shorts-long-form-study-metricool/)
- AIR Media-Tech suggests 1–2 Shorts/week alongside 3–5 long-form. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)

### Inferences
- Combining Buffer's rates with Socialinsider's 22%/yr baseline for 1–5K accounts: 3–5 Instagram posts/week roughly doubles organic growth vs 1–2; a solo creator posting ~1/week sits near the "no-post penalty" zone.
- The TikTok data shows an arms race: output up 40%, views per video down 23%, so holding reach constant required ~40% more videos in 2025.
- Hours cost must be estimated by the report writer from production assumptions; no study measured it.

### Gaps
- No dataset on hours per Reel/TikTok/carousel or total weekly time; all sources discuss counts only.
- Buffer's study is Instagram feed only; no equivalent TikTok frequency-vs-reach regression was found (SocialPilot cites its own 1.5M-post study but figures were not retrievable).

## Key Question 5 — Follower → engagement → off-platform clicks: link-in-bio CTR and platform hostility to outbound links

### Takeaway
Both platforms structurally suppress outbound links (no clickable links in Instagram captions or TikTok descriptions/comments; links only in bio and Instagram Stories, and Stories reach followers only). Mosseri denies a "link in bio" reach penalty, and no study proves one, but no reliable 2025–2026 link-in-bio click-through rate benchmark was found either.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram captions don't support links; Stories can carry a link sticker, are "in theory" not reach-restricted, but are shown only to followers. TikTok disallows URLs in descriptions and comments; no evidence of bio-link restriction; TikTok blocks links to e-commerce competitors such as Amazon (third-party claim). — [Social Media Today, 25 Nov 2024](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/heres-each-big-social-platform-stand-external-links/733946/)
- Mosseri: saying "link in bio" does not hurt reach. eMarketer editorial: creators' perception of a penalty changes behaviour regardless; creators depend on off-platform revenue, so Instagram must support cross-platform activity "in practice and in appearance." — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/off-platform-links-remain-tension-instagram-creators)
- Linktree traffic sources (undated, aggregator): Instagram 33.7% of Linktree clicks, direct 20.9%; >40% of Linktree traffic from Instagram in 2021 (SMH); ~20M clicks on monetised links (Tubefilter, Dec 2022); no CTR figure. Old data. — [ProductMint](https://productmint.com/linktree-statistics/)
- Contactability proxy: 39% of Instagram creators (≥10K) list a public email vs 4.6% of TikTok creators (FY2025). — [Click Analytic](https://www.clickanalytic.com/creator-economy-report/tiktok/)
- Rival IQ Stories page (Sep 2023): illustrative only — 10% reach-rate example, one frame with 26% exit rate; no link-tap benchmark. — [Rival IQ](https://www.rivaliq.com/?p=33578)
- Sends/DMs are Instagram's preferred "share" path: more photos/videos are shared in DMs than in Stories (Mosseri, 2025). — [Music Marketing Trends, Apr 2025](https://musicmarketingtrends.beehiiv.com/p/how-to-win-on-instagram-from-it-s-ceo)

### Inferences
- The off-platform path on both apps is bio-link only (plus Stories link to existing followers on Instagram); with per-post reach of hundreds to low thousands at <10K followers, bio-link clicks per post will be in the single or low double digits. The report should state that CTR is unquantified rather than cite a number.
- Instagram is more "contactable"/business-oriented (39% public emails) than TikTok (4.6%), consistent with Instagram being the better place to route people off-platform.

### Gaps
- No credible 2025–2026 benchmark for link-in-bio or Story link-sticker click-through rate (searches returned only how-to pages and a 2023 illustrative example).
- No controlled study of whether outbound-link mentions reduce reach; only Mosseri's denial and anecdote.

## Key Question 6 — Monetisation for small creators (1K / 10K / 50K): bonuses, affiliate, brand deals; Portugal/EU availability

### Takeaway
Platform payouts are largely unavailable to a Portugal-based small creator: TikTok's Creator Rewards Program (10K followers, 100K views/30 d, >1-min videos) is open only in US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, BR, MX as of Jul 2026, and Instagram Gifts (5K followers) was rolled out to 22 EU states but not Portugal, Spain or France; Instagram bonuses are invite-only US/KR/JP. Brand deals are the realistic income: nano (1–10K) Instagram post $50–500, micro (10–100K) Instagram post $500–5,000 and TikTok video $200–2,000 (USD, 2025–2026 compiled ranges).

### Cited Findings
- TikTok Creator Rewards Program (as of 26 Jul 2026): eligible countries US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Brazil, Mexico (Mexico added 1 Oct 2025). Thresholds: 18+, ≥10,000 followers, ≥100,000 video views in prior 30 days, original videos >1 minute, personal account in good standing; TikTok publishes no fixed RPM. **Portugal not eligible.** — [Quasa, 27 Jul 2026](https://quasa.io/media/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eligible-countries-in-2026)
- Meta (6 Nov 2023): Instagram Gifts expanded to Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Sweden plus non-EU countries; eligibility professional account, 18+, ≥5,000 followers. **Portugal, Spain, France, Cyprus, Malta absent.** Holiday/New Year's Reels+photo bonus invite-only in US, South Korea, Japan. Instagram Subscriptions expanded to 35 additional countries (unlisted), fees waived. Older data (Nov 2023); no later Portugal update found. — [Meta for Creators, Nov 2023](https://creators.facebook.com/giving-more-creators-around-the-world-ways-to-earn-money)
- ContentGrip rate card (8 May 2026, compiled from Shopify, Influencer Marketing Hub, Collabstr, Click Analytic 2025–2026): Nano (1K–10K) Instagram feed post $50–500 (outliers to $1,000); Micro (10K–100K) Instagram feed post $500–5,000, TikTok video $200–2,000, YouTube integration $500–5,000; Instagram Story bundle (3–5 frames, all tiers) $300–10,000. Collabstr 2025: Reel rates ~32% higher than TikTok video at equal follower counts. IMH 2025: nano engagement 5–8% Instagram, >10% TikTok. — [ContentGrip, May 2026](https://www.contentgrip.com/influencer-marketing-rate-card/)
- Click Analytic (FY2025): 28.8% of Instagram creators ≥10K have brand-deal history; only 0.47% meet "elite" audience quality (90%+ real followers and ≥2% engagement); 14.6% meet "good" (≥2% engagement). — [Click Analytic](https://www.clickanalytic.com/creator-economy-report/tiktok/)

### Inferences
- At 1–10K followers in Portugal, platform-native income is effectively zero (no Rewards Program, no Gifts, no bonus); the only levers are brand deals (tens to low hundreds of dollars per post), affiliate, and off-platform products — which pushes the growth question back to where the audience can be moved off-platform (KQ5) or onto YouTube (KQ3), whose Partner Program is available in Portugal (not researched here; out of scope).
- The ~2% engagement bar that brands use ("good" audience quality) is far above brand-account averages (0.45%) but within normal range for creator accounts (5–8% nano).

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 primary source on Instagram Gifts/Subscriptions/bonus availability in Portugal beyond the Nov 2023 list; status may have changed.
- No affiliate-income benchmarks by follower tier were found.
- Rate-card figures are compiled ranges from marketplaces, not measured medians; no EU/Portugal-specific rates.
